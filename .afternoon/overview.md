# Story Overview

## Premise

An alternate-perspective retelling of the fall of Lordaeron. Sylvanas Windrunner is finishing a troll sweep on the Quel'Thalas border when Cyndia's border post catches an unauthorized human mage inside the first ward boundary: Jaina Proudmoore, Antonidas's protégée, who sensed leyline corruption from afar and followed it north without Kirin Tor backing or authorization. What begins as a hostile interrogation becomes a continent-spanning competence thriller — and an unspoken, slow-burn romantic devotion forged inside disciplined tactical intimacy — as the two women uncover the Cult of the Damned, race against the Scourge's timetable, witness Arthas Menethil's descent into madness, track him through Northrend and back to Capital City, and ultimately assassinate him in the throne room of Lordaeron seconds after he murders his own father. There are no witnesses to Arthas's crime. Only two foreign women standing over two royal corpses with weapons drawn, condemned together while the real enemy walks past their cells.
