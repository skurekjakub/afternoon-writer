# Chapter 20: Frostmourne

**POV:** Sylvanas Windrunner (limited third, absolute)
**When:** Opens the morning after Chapter 19's landfall at Daggercap Bay and closes on the retreat from the Frostmourne cavern toward Muradin's camp.
**Where:** Daggercap Bay and the inland route through Dragonblight to the Frostmourne cavern.
**Travel:** Harbor survey on foot, forced march inland through Dragonblight, approach to Muradin's camp, then cavern assault and fighting retreat.
**Characters:** Sylvanas, Jaina, Cyndia, three Farstrider rangers, Dagny briefly, Muradin later, Arthas.
**Goal:** Track Arthas inland fast enough to reach him before whatever is calling him in the glacier finishes the turn.

## Character Arcs

### Sylvanas
- She opens knowing they are late, still wanting cleaner execution to recover days already lost.
- Muradin's wry joke catches how tightly she and Jaina move together, and she refuses to look up and see Jaina's face.
- In the cavern, Arthas's single look at Jaina hits her with a sharp, fierce possessive anger she does not name, even as her bow cracks in her hand.
- By the end, she accepts a clean defeat without blaming her partner, and Frostmourne—not the prince—becomes the real enemy.

### Jaina through Sylvanas's POV
- Jaina is tracker, magical striker, and the only person in the line who can hear what Frostmourne actually is.
- Sylvanas reads her through bread-smell, air-writing, tremor, and the exact expressions that mean Jaina has heard something she cannot yet put into words.
- Keep Jaina exterior. Her discoveries show through what she says, how fast she speaks, and what her fingers trace in freezing air.

### Partnership
- The pair opens fast, disciplined, and already reshaped by the crossing.
- Muradin is the first outsider to watch them work together over his map; his dwarf joke carries a sly edge noticing how completely locked onto each other they are.
- The cave proves both their peak and their ceiling.
- Let the held shot, the extraction, and Sylvanas's unspoken fury at Arthas's gaze carry the bond without spelling it out.

## Scene 1: The Burned Shore

**The action:** Daggercap Bay shows how completely Arthas cut his own line of retreat before marching inland.
**Cast:** Sylvanas, Jaina, Cyndia, three Farstrider rangers, Dagny briefly.
**Starting point:** Arthas came through this harbor days ago, and the source lies inland.

### Beat 1
- Daggercap Bay proves Arthas did not just land here—he burned **every** ship he arrived on. Only blackened ribs frozen into the harbor ice remain beside the intact hull of the *Wolfheart*. No vessel sits anchored in a side cove; Arthas left himself and his men no way back off this shore.
- Arthas treated his entire fleet and crew as one-way fuel, the bay smells of old char days later, and Dagny's *Wolfheart* is intact only because it arrived after the burning.
- They do not know what Arthas marched inland to claim, how he intends to leave Northrend if he ever means to, or whether anyone local survived to tell them.
- Ground the bay: charred ship ribs jutting out of harbor ice across every anchorage, cold smoke-stink trapped in snowbanks, the *Wolfheart*'s dark timber riding alone against a graveyard of burned hulls.
- Dagny agrees to hold the anchorage while Sylvanas's line steps off the docks with no spare ships left in the bay.

### Beat 2
- The inland track is broad, brutal, and unmistakable. Arthas drove his column at a pace that ignored supply sense, fallback camps, or tired feet.
- Bootprints and dragged sled ruts cut straight inland without scouting wings, Jaina's widened sensitivity locks directly onto the same bearing, and nothing along the trail suggests hesitation.
- They do not know how many days inland the site sits, or whether they can reach it before Arthas claims what called him here.
- Ground the trail: frozen bootprints and sled grooves in dirty snow, torn packing straw and snapped crate hoops littering a stripped camp edge, Jaina's face sharpening the moment the physical trail and the signal align.
- Sylvanas sets a forced-march pace straight up the sled tracks.

## Scene 2: The March

**The action:** Dragonblight grinds at the living, then a fortified dwarven camp yields the last living witness who saw Arthas before the cave.
**Cast:** Sylvanas, Jaina, Cyndia, three Farstrider rangers.
**Starting point:** The inland trail is clear and the target is moving hard.

### Beat 3
- Dragonblight slows every living step while Arthas's tracks stay relentless. The white wastes feel built to bleed heat and patience, and every mile deepens the pressure against their chests.
- Northrend's interior wears down endurance before an enemy appears, Jaina hears the northern signal louder with every ridge, and even without mage-sense Sylvanas feels the terrain pushing back.
- They do not know whether the man ahead is still a prince or already changed, or how far past the wastes the cavern sits.
- Ground the march: wind-hardened snow crust cracking under boots, gray-white horizon with no shelter, breath freezing on scarf wool faster than sentences can finish.
- They push across the frozen plain until timber palisades rise through the snow glare.

### Beat 4
- The dwarven camp holds through stubborn fortification rather than normal campaign luck. Men on the wall level crossbows and look both relieved and wary to see living travelers come out of the white.
- The garrison already knows this is worse than a border raid, Arthas passed through recently enough that their tracks are still fresh, and the palisade is a foothold under siege rather than safe shelter.
- They do not know how much the dwarves know about what Arthas went after, or whether their news can still beat him to the glacier.
- Ground the foothold: frost-rimed timber palisade, low cookfire smoke flattened under windbreaks, bearded sentries squinting down over iron sights before hauling the gate bar back.
- The gate opens onto the last camp that spoke to Arthas before the cavern.

## Scene 3: The Ally

**The action:** Muradin delivers the Frostmourne intelligence, catches the pair's unspoken sync over his map, and marches out with them.
**Cast:** Sylvanas, Jaina, Cyndia, Muradin, three Farstrider rangers.
**Starting point:** Arthas passed this camp days ago heading deeper into the glacier after something worth fortifying against.

### Beat 5
- Muradin Bronzebeard trusts them fast and lays out what happened when Arthas passed through, making plain that the prince is already close to whatever called him north. While Muradin talks, Jaina and Sylvanas lean over his map together—Jaina tracing the signal bearing with a fingertip while Sylvanas marks march stages with a knife point, each adjusting the other's line without looking up, shoulders almost touching. Muradin stops mid-sentence to watch how locked onto each other they are.
- Muradin saw Arthas fray up close and knows normal command logic is finished. Seeing the two women finish each other's measurements without a glance, he lets out a wry dwarf joke—he's known dwarven drill crews who needed three hammers and a song to keep time like that, with an edge that notices more than tactics—and goes back to his ale.
- They do not know whether the object in the glacier can still be reached before Arthas touches it, or what taking Muradin along will cost.
- Ground the talk: hot metal-tasting ale in a shelter smelling of forge soot and cold wool, beard-rings clicking as Muradin speaks, Jaina's fingertip and Sylvanas's knife point moving over the same parchment edge to edge.
- Muradin finishes his map notes with both women already agreed on the route.
- Inside: a dwarf watching her and Jaina over a map and catching more than he says; corrects Jaina's stage, gets corrected back without a word, likes it, and moves the knife on; will not look up to see what Jaina's face is doing; Arthas nearly at the thing, and she is oddly steady just standing elbow-to-elbow over a table

### Beat 6
- Muradin names the weapon: Frostmourne, a cursed runeblade calling to Arthas out of the glacier. Jaina recognizes its frequency at once—the same family as the plague, built as its sister-weapon.
- Frostmourne is the true target, Jaina's Scholomance education lets her explain not just that the blade is cursed but how it ties to Lordaeron's dead, and Muradin finally hears words that fit what he watched for months. He warned Arthas off the blade to his face when the prince passed the camp; Arthas marched past him anyway.
- They do not know whether Arthas already holds the sword, or what it does to a living wielder in real time.
- Ground the name: Jaina's bare fingers sketching invisible notation in cold shelter air, frost creeping along the timber joints as the word *Frostmourne* lands, Muradin's shoulders dropping an inch in grim relief at being believed.
- Frostmourne replaces Arthas as the real threat waiting in the glacier.

### Beat 7
- The hours left are terrible, and they move anyway. Muradin straps his hammer on instead of staying behind the palisade, turning the march into a sprint measured in hours.
- Muradin's local guide sense tightens their line without making the cavern safe, and every mile toward the ice ridge feels like a narrowing margin.
- They do not know whether they are racing a prince or what comes after a prince, or whether seven fighters can survive first contact with the blade.
- Ground the departure: Muradin's warhammer thudding onto its harness strap, snow kicked up from an immediate start with no meal or speech, Jaina nodding with too little breath to waste words.
- Seven figures strike out from the palisade toward the glacier mouth.

## Scene 4: Frostmourne

**The action:** They arrive too late, hit the ceiling of skill against the blade, survive a collapsing ice chamber when Arthas walks away, and carry out the one seam Jaina heard.
**Cast:** Sylvanas, Jaina, Cyndia, three Farstrider rangers, Muradin, Arthas.
**Starting point:** Frostmourne is real, Arthas ran for it, and the tracks ahead already say they are late.

### Beat 8
- The cavern mouth announces defeat before anyone steps inside. Bootprints go in and never come back out. Arthas's golden warhammer, Light's Vengeance, lies abandoned in the snow.
- Even Sylvanas feels the wrongness rolling out of the ice arch, and Jaina hears the blade without reaching for it—the channel is no longer searching; it is taking impact.
- They do not know whether anything of Arthas remains inside, or whether the cavern gives them a single foot of good ground.
- Ground the threshold: golden hammer metal dulling against blue-white snow, boot tracks leading solely inward, ranger instinct telling Sylvanas to pull her line back while Jaina is already stepping past the hammer.
- Sylvanas draws her bow and follows Jaina into the ice arch.
- Inside: the hammer in the snow says it, the tracks say it; would order any ranger back from this doorway; going in anyway, because crossing a sea to read a threshold is not to be borne; and because Jaina is already walking

### Beat 9
- Inside the main chamber, the turn is already finished. Arthas stands holding Frostmourne, flanked by loyal soldiers who look alive at first glance and wrong at second.
- Proximity to the blade has already hollowed out Arthas's guards before death fully claims them, Frostmourne's cold presses outward like an active weapon rather than winter air, and Arthas's face is still his while every trace of human hesitation inside it is gone.
- They do not know whether steel or fire can touch him, or how long they have before his men close the mouth behind them.
- Ground the chamber: sourceless blue-white light running under glacial walls, armored men standing too still to be breathing right, Arthas's voice sounding like his own with every scrap of warmth stripped out.
- They face a wielder who is already past saving.

### Beat 10
- The partnership opens at full speed. Sylvanas's best opening arrow drives true into Arthas, and he pulls it out and drops it on the ice. Frostmourne's field eats Jaina's first spell before it touches him.
- Ordinary lethal shots no longer stop him, and Jaina can read the blade's field fast enough to confirm its kinship with the plague without being able to crack it here. When Arthas pivots to answer Jaina, his left side opens cleanly to Sylvanas at short range. She draws to the ear, holds the arrow steady, and checks Jaina's face. Jaina shakes her head—the field is thickest on that side. Sylvanas shifts her stance and keeps the arrow on the string.
- They do not know whether any opening can reach him at all, or how many seconds remain before his soldiers pin them against the ice.
- Ground the exchange: the bent arrow clattering across glazed stone, Jaina's magic shattering harmlessly against invisible cold, Sylvanas holding a full draw while her eyes lock on Jaina's shake of the head.
- Their cleanest opening fails to touch him.

### Beat 11
- Arthas drives Frostmourne straight into the glacial floor and wall. Ice-shard blowback and a collapsing curtain of ceiling ice rip across the chamber—felling Muradin under falling shards, cracking Sylvanas's main bow limb in her grip, and fracturing the cavern floor between them and Arthas. Arthas could cut straight through the falling ice wall to finish all seven of them. Instead he pauses in the freezing dust, looks at Jaina *once* across the broken ice, and turns away—walking deeper into the northern glacier tunnel with his men because they are no longer worth stopping for.
- The collapsing ice curtain earns their survival without softening Arthas: he leaves them alive out of total dismissal, not mercy. Seeing Arthas look at Jaina through the dust hits Sylvanas with a sudden, fierce, unnamed possessive anger—that this emptied thing still thinks he gets to look at her—while her cracked bow hangs useless in her fist.
- They do not know whether Muradin is dead or breathing under the ice, where the northern tunnel leads, or whether they can pull anything useful out of the wreckage.
- Ground the break: glacial ice exploding like thrown knives as Frostmourne bites stone, a curtain of ceiling ice crashing down between the fighters, the dry snap running up Sylvanas's bow limb, Jaina's hands freezing mid-cast under Arthas's single cold look before his cloak disappears into the northern tunnel.
- Arthas vanishes deeper into the northern glacier tunnel, leaving the seven of them behind a wall of shattered ice.

### Beat 12
- There is no time for shock inside a collapsing cave—only extraction. Cyndia and the rangers haul unconscious Muradin clear while Sylvanas covers the mouth, getting all seven bodies out alive. On the snow outside, Sylvanas checks the fight coldly and finds nothing she would change—not the ground, not the timing, not the partner. Beside her, Jaina's shaking fingers are already tracing invisible marks in the freezing air: she heard a seam in Frostmourne's cycle.
- Nobody is left behind, Light's Vengeance stays abandoned in the snow because no hand is free to carry it, and Jaina's air-writing proves the defeat gave her something real to work on.
- They do not know whether Muradin survives the carry back to his camp, or what the seam Jaina heard can become once she has breath to build it.
- Ground the retreat: dwarf boots dragging twin grooves in snow between two carrying elves, Light's Vengeance glowing unanswered behind them at the cave mouth, Jaina's trembling fingers marking beats in empty air as she walks.
- They haul Muradin south through the snow while Jaina counts the seam she heard in the blade.
- Inside: would change nothing, the ground, the timing, the partner, and lost cleanly; he looked at Jaina once through that ice and something hot and ugly jumped under her ribs that she will not name; Jaina already drawing in the air beside her, and that steadies her more than the dwarf still breathing
