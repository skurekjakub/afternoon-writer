# Chapter 15: The Prince

**POV:** Sylvanas Windrunner (limited third, absolute)
**When:** Directly follows Chapter 14 on the north road. Opens on the approach to Hearthglen and closes with Jaina, Sylvanas, and Cyndia riding ahead of Arthas's main column toward Stratholme.
**Where:** North road into Hearthglen and Arthas's war camp.
**Travel:** Horseback approach from the Eastweald, on foot through Hearthglen and the camp, then back to horseback for the ride toward Stratholme.

**Goal:** Reach Arthas before he marches, lay the full chain down without wasting a minute, and decide whether there is still one move left before Stratholme goes under.

## Character Arcs

### Sylvanas
- She opens believing clear intelligence should still let her steer the prince she armed.
- Hearthglen's smoking tent-poles and Uther's quiet voice show her how fast Arthas is slipping past control.
- Watching Arthas freeze Jaina out with `"Lady Proudmoore"` while Jaina immediately turns dark scholarship into a desperate plan to save Stratholme's kitchens hits Sylvanas harder than she expects—it will not fit neatly beside her bitter read of the sanctum.
- The fourth farewell slips through camp wearing a clerk's face nobody recognizes, and she has only enough time to make Cyndia start a log before mounting up.
- By the end, Sylvanas leaves the prince to his banners and puts her horse right beside Jaina's on the road to Stratholme.

### Arthas through Sylvanas's POV
- At first glance he still wears a prince's authority.
- Then the cracks stand out sharp: dented Menethil plate left unmended, dried blood left in the grooves, calling Jaina `"Lady Proudmoore"` like a stranger, turning every sentence straight into a march order.
- At Hearthglen he already reached for torches before the sick were dead, and only Uther's body in the tent mouth stopped him.
- He does not deny the truth; he uses it to move faster.
- Keep him exterior only.

### Partnership
- The Scholomance crack and the ford near-miss still sit unspoken between them, yet in front of Arthas's court their stance locks together cleanly.
- Arthas's cold `"Lady Proudmoore"` contrasts sharply with Sylvanas stepping right beside Jaina's hand on the city map and choosing to ride into Stratholme at her flank.
- Any thaw here lives in where Sylvanas puts her horse when the clock starts, never in spoken forgiveness.

## Scene 1: Hearthglen

**The action:** They ride onto the smoking edge of Hearthglen while the fight against the newly dead is still raw, and Uther tells them beside the scorched tent poles what Arthas nearly did.
**Cast:** Sylvanas, Jaina, Cyndia, refugees, local soldiers, Uther.
**Starting point:** Hearthglen barely held, ford mud is still drying on their boots, and the Scholomance crack sits raw between Sylvanas and Jaina.

### Beat 1
- West of the Eastweald, the kingdom is coming apart field by field. Refugees choke the southbound ditch, orchard fruit hangs blackened on the bough, and wheat stands unharvested in dead silence.
- Here the plague had time to bite deep rather than lurk behind ledger ink; refugee carts spill over every verge, and Jaina's hand tightens visibly on her reins from the rising necromantic drag long before Hearthglen's roofs appear.
- They do not know what state Arthas is in after the battle or whether Hearthglen is holding or still bleeding out.
- Ground the approach: a child's laced shoe half buried in wheel mud, black apples clinging to bare limbs, overburdened family carts grinding slow past three lathered Thalassian horses.
- The road turns Kel'Thuzad's equations back into soot and displaced people.

### Beat 2
- They ride straight onto the smoking edge of camp where Hearthglen is still fighting its own dead. One entire row of fever tents lies collapsed in wet, black, still-smoking ash down to scorched poles, while the neighboring row stands packed with coughing men. Right across their path, two gray-faced soldiers haul a fresh corpse by the boots out of a canvas mouth toward the pyres before its jaw and fingers can finish jerking upright.
- Hearthglen's grain already woke inside living houses, the freshly dead still climb back up within minutes if soldiers miss a pulse, and the plague runs on without Kel'Thuzad breathing.
- They do not know how much of this outbreak is blind contagion and how much is still steered—or why one tent row burned black while the next still stands.
- Ground the city edge: greasy black smoke curling off charred tent poles, pale holy fire scorched into gate cobbles, two sweating soldiers dragging a stiffening corpse whose fingers scrape the mud as it tries to curl upright.
- Sylvanas sees the plague is now feeding itself in broad daylight.

### Beat 3
- They find Uther kneeling in the mud right beside the scorched tent poles, not standing over maps—healing a living fever patient with warm, steady golden Light pouring from his scarred hands until the man's rattling breath eases. Without rising or lowering his voice, Uther nods once at the black ash beside his knee and tells them plainly: when the first sick died and stood up, Arthas ordered the living fever tents burned, and Uther had to plant himself in the tent mouth until the prince took the order back. The burned row held only the dead.
- The Light in Uther's hands is unmistakably warm and good, and his quiet terror is real: he stopped Arthas once in a town this size, and he knows Stratholme is far larger.
- They do not know whether Uther thinks he can hold the prince a second time or whether Arthas will hear anything past haste once Stratholme is named.
- Ground the meeting: golden warmth lifting steam off Uther's wet sleeves inches from black ash, a fevered soldier catching a clean breath under his palms, Uther's tired eyes sliding toward the standing canvas row as he names what Arthas ordered.
- With Hearthglen's price still smoking at their boots, they walk straight into the command tent.
- Inside: she pointed this prince at the plague; torches on living sick, and an old paladin planting himself in a tent mouth; did she know what she was arming back in the Eastweald; yes, Uther warned her outside the canvas and she let him march; never trusted a human priest, and trusts this one

## Scene 2: The War Tent

**The action:** Arthas freezes Jaina out with formal distance, Jaina turns Scholomance's teaching into a narrow plan to save Stratholme's kitchens, Arthas marches anyway, and the fourth farewell slips right through the camp rush.
**Cast:** Sylvanas, Jaina, Cyndia, Arthas, Uther, Falric, staff officers, quartermaster's aide, grain carter.
**Starting point:** Hearthglen proves the outbreak is accelerating, and Stratholme is the next city on the grain road.

### Beat 4
- Arthas looks wrong before he finishes his first greeting. His shoulder plate is cracked clean across the Menethil crest with dried blood left in the grooves, his eyes stay pinned to the map, and when Jaina steps inside he greets her with a flat, glacial `"Lady Proudmoore"`—never looking up at Uther, never asking after the fever tents outside.
- Arthas has stopped spending breath on anything that does not drive him forward. Watching Jaina's chin lift a fraction under that cold title, Sylvanas sees at once how cleanly the prince has walled her out—and steps half a pace closer to Jaina's flank without thinking about it.
- They do not know the full history buried beneath that title, or whether Arthas can still hear any plan that buys hours instead of burning bodies.
- Ground the tent: a jagged dent splitting the Menethil lion on Arthas's pauldron, crusted blood dried dark along polished steel, Jaina's jaw tightening for one heartbeat at `"Lady Proudmoore"` before answering him in cool Dalaran cadence.
- Sylvanas sees a prince in name and a runaway weapon in motion.

### Beat 5
- Sylvanas and Jaina lay the chain down in a few stripped sentences—no long recap—while Arthas interrupts only for distances and road times. Before the room can harden into steel, Jaina puts her palm flat on one quarter of the Stratholme map and offers the narrow path: reach the granaries, bakehouses, and main feed stores before the city finishes spreading the flour, and she can break the plague's pull at the source.
- Jaina is spending Kel'Thuzad's teaching straight away to try to save living streets, aiming at concrete storehouses instead of grand spells. Watching her work the map under Arthas's cold stare, Sylvanas feels her bitter sanctum read crack at the edges—it does not fit the woman standing beside her now.
- They do not know whether Stratholme has already eaten too far past the granaries or whether Arthas hears a head start as a chance or an obstacle.
- Ground the proposal: belt daggers pinning curled map corners, Jaina's chalk-scarred fingers marking granaries and bakehouses on one district instead of the whole city, Uther leaning forward like a man spotting one last door before a wall.
- With the plan laid out, Arthas has to choose whether to hold the army or march beside it.
- Inside: spending Kel'Thuzad's teaching against his own plague, the very first thing she does with it; doesn't fit the sanctum table, and Sylvanas wants it to fit one way or the other; Arthas calls her Lady Proudmoore like a stranger and she doesn't even flinch; won't say she trusts her yet, and is already pricing the ride beside her

### Beat 6
- Arthas will not bet Stratholme on waiting. He slams orders across the table to march the main column immediately: Jaina, Sylvanas, and Cyndia can take fresh horses and ride ahead to hit the granaries, while his vanguard rides hard right behind them.
- Motion is still Arthas's only answer to dread; Uther stays with the column because the final break has not arrived yet; and Jaina's narrow source plan now runs on the exact same clock as an advancing army.
- They do not know whether their head start buys an hour or a day, or what Arthas will order if Jaina tells him the city is already too late.
- Ground the order: Arthas's scarred gauntlet striking the map beside Stratholme's east gate, staff officers scattering before his last word drops, Falric already turning for the tent flap on the first command.
- The tent empties into shouted orders, and the race to Stratholme starts at once.

### Beat 7
- The camp breaks down in dust and shouting. While Sylvanas and Jaina check cinches at the horse lines with Falric, a nondescript quartermaster's aide brings Cyndia their road packet by the supply crates. He pauses to look past Cyndia at the blue-and-gold column forming on the Stratholme road, passes a wax-sealed manifest to a waiting grain carter bound for Stratholme's granaries, and sends Cyndia off with `"May the stars keep your road."`
- The camp throws a fast scout team ahead within minutes; Cyndia recognizes the exact road blessing from the Eastweald orchard ambush at once, while the manifest looks like routine supply paper in a noisy yard.
- They do not know who the aide is or whether the grain carter is already rolling ahead of them into Stratholme.
- Ground the handoff: stiff new reins creaking from a tack chest, a route packet pinned down under a belt knife on a pine crate, `"May the stars keep your road."` landing quiet and clear right through shouted harness orders.
- Second read (plant only; the prose must not explain it): he is checking that the army marches on schedule, and the manifest sends grain and orders into Stratholme before the army arrives.
- Cyndia carries the report straight to the horse lines.

### Beat 8
- As they swing up, Cyndia gives Sylvanas the report in three clipped lines: the clerk, the sealed manifest for Stratholme's granaries, and the farewell—adding that it is an odd turn of phrase for a Lordaeron camp clerk. Sylvanas pulls Falric and the nearest quartermaster over and asks for the aide's name. Neither knows the face, and the column horns are already blowing. Sylvanas orders Cyndia to start logging every sighting from now on—where, when, and what face—and Cyndia scribbles the first entry in the back of her relay book braced across her saddle before they spur out.
- The farewell has come back wearing a fourth stranger's face, the one quick check Sylvanas can afford hits air, and Stratholme's clock outranks an unnamed clerk.
- They do not know what the list will add up to or when Sylvanas will get five quiet minutes to read it.
- Ground the report: Cyndia's stub pencil scratching fast across a page pressed flat on saddle leather, Falric shouting march orders over his shoulder, the grain carter's wagon rolling out onto the Stratholme road ahead of the vanguard.
- Second read (plant only; the prose must not explain it): nobody knows the aide because he is nobody's aide. "Odd turn of phrase" is the line being a Kaldorei blessing translated word for word into Common.
- The horses lunge onto the north road, leaving no breath left for camp puzzles.
- Inside: a fourth face, one question, and air; a clerk nobody can name against a city on a clock; writes it down because a Farstrider logs what she can't kill yet; one phrase for too many faces, or too many faces for one phrase, and no time for either

## Scene 3: The Head Start

**The action:** Riding knee-to-knee ahead of Arthas's rising dust, Sylvanas puts her speed behind Jaina's plan and leaves the rest for Stratholme to prove.
**Cast:** Sylvanas, Jaina, Cyndia.
**Starting point:** Arthas's army is rolling behind them, and Stratholme's granaries are already opening for morning trade.

### Beat 9
- The three women push their mounts hard down the Stratholme road. Leaning low over her horse's neck, Jaina is already cutting the city into real streets, granaries, and ovens she can hit in order. Choosing Jaina's small, desperate gamble over the prince's banners behind them, Sylvanas brings her horse up tight beside Jaina's flank—closing that half-length gap from the Eastweald without saying a word—and lets the city ahead decide the rest.
- Jaina is burning Kel'Thuzad's knowledge trying to keep strangers alive, Sylvanas backs the attempt with everything she has even with the sanctum silence still unspoken, and the partnership now races against both the plague and the prince behind them.
- They do not know whether the granary plan can beat the city's ovens, or what happens between them if it fails.
- Ground the ride: three Thalassian horses stretched flat along the road verge, Jaina shouting district names and granary order against the wind, Sylvanas pulling even at her shoulder while Arthas's vanguard dust climbs yellow behind them.
- Chapter 16 opens straight out of this gallop on the final stretch into Stratholme.
