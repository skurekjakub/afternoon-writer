# Chapter 15: The Prince

**POV:** Sylvanas Windrunner (limited third, absolute)
**When:** Directly follows Chapter 14 on the north road. Opens on the approach to Hearthglen and closes with Jaina, Sylvanas, and Cyndia riding ahead of Arthas's main column toward Stratholme.
**Where:** North road into Hearthglen and Arthas's war camp.
**Travel:** Horseback approach from the Eastweald, on foot through Hearthglen and the camp, then back to horseback for the ride toward Stratholme.
**Characters:** Sylvanas, Jaina, Cyndia, Arthas, Uther, Falric.
**Goal:** Reach Arthas, give him the full plague picture, and decide whether there is still one move left before Stratholme goes under.

## Scene 1: Hearthglen

**The action:** The road into Hearthglen shows what the plague looks like once it is fully awake, and Uther tells them what Arthas nearly did there.
**Cast:** Sylvanas, Jaina, Cyndia, refugees, local soldiers, Uther.
**Starting point:** Arthas is ahead, Hearthglen has already taken the blow, and the crack from Scholomance is still there.

### Beat 1
- The country west of the Eastweald is being lost piece by piece. Refugees crowd the southbound side of the road, orchards blacken on the branch, and fields stand dead where no one dared finish the harvest.
- The plague had time to take hold here in a way the earlier road only warned about, refugee movement is too large for local response to absorb, and Jaina is already straining under the necromantic pressure before the city comes into view.
- They do not know what Arthas is like after Hearthglen, or whether the city is merely scarred or still losing ground.
- Ground the approach: a child's shoe half sunk in mud, black fruit still hanging in an orchard, wagon wheels dragging under too much family weight.
- The road turns the plague from theory back into bodies.

### Beat 2
- Hearthglen held, but it is still fighting after the win. Hospital tents are full, pyres burn hot, and every fresh death risks standing back up before the priests can reach it. At the edge of the camp, one line of fever tents is burned to the poles, and the next line stands untouched.
- Hearthglen's grain already activated inside a living town, the newly dead keep rejoining the attack if the field misses them for even minutes, and the cult's work is still moving without Kel'Thuzad.
- They do not know how much of this is blind plague behavior and how much is still guided, or why one line of tents burned and the next did not.
- Ground the city edge: holy fire scored into stone near the gate, men dragging a body to the pyres before it can sit up, wet ash in the burned tent line with the next row of canvas still standing beside it.
- Sylvanas sees the plague is now feeding itself.

### Beat 3
- Uther is healing when they find him, not commanding. The Light in his hands is warm and ordinary and fully good. He tells them plainly about the tents: when the first sick died and rose, Arthas ordered the fever tents burned with the living still inside. Uther stood in the tent mouth until the prince took the order back. The burned line held only the dead.
- Uther is still a field healer first, speed and holy fire saved the city, and Arthas already reached for fire before the sick were dead. Uther stopped him once. He fears what Arthas will do in a city larger than this one.
- They do not know whether Uther thinks the prince can still be checked, or whether Arthas will hear anything except urgency once the next city is named.
- Ground the meeting: golden warmth lifting from Uther's scarred hands, a wounded soldier breathing easier before Uther even steps back, Uther's eyes going to the standing tents while he talks.
- With Hearthglen priced, they have to enter the tent where price turns into orders.
- Inside: she pointed this man at the plague; fire on the living, and an old priest standing in a tent mouth; did she know what she was arming in the Eastweald; yes, Uther told her outside the tent, and she let him march; never trusted a human priest, and trusts this one

## Scene 2: The War Tent

**The action:** Arthas's manner tells Sylvanas more than his words, and Jaina turns the plague picture into one narrow source plan before the room can collapse into steel.
**Cast:** Sylvanas, Jaina, Cyndia, Arthas, Uther, Falric, staff officers, quartermaster's aide, grain carter.
**Starting point:** Hearthglen proves the outbreak is accelerating and Stratholme is the next obvious city.

### Beat 4
- Arthas looks wrong before he says anything useful. His armor is damaged and unrepaired, his eyes stay fixed on the map, and he calls Jaina "Lady Proudmoore" instead of her name. He does not ask about Hearthglen's tents and does not look at Uther.
- Arthas has stopped spending time on anything that does not move him toward the next decision, the title is a choice rather than neutral etiquette, and Uther stands behind him carrying concern in his grip rather than his voice.
- They do not know whether Arthas can still hear a plan that buys time instead of spending bodies, or the full history between him and Jaina.
- Ground the tent: a cracked shoulder guard splitting the Menethil crest, dried blood left in polished grooves, Jaina tightening for a moment before answering him professionally.
- Sylvanas sees an ally in form and a danger in motion.

### Beat 5
- Sylvanas and Jaina give Arthas the chain in a few sentences, and he cuts in only for timing and roads. Then Jaina offers the narrower path herself. If they reach Stratholme's grain stores, bakehouses, and main feed points before the city finishes spreading the plague through itself, the theory can still matter.
- Jaina's countermeasure can be used against concentrated source nodes if she gets there early enough, the targets are specific stores and flow points rather than the city in the abstract, and Sylvanas sees Jaina spending Kel'Thuzad's teaching against the plague at once and cannot fit it cleanly into her old read of the sanctum.
- They do not know whether Stratholme is already too far seeded for the source plan to matter, or whether Arthas hears head start as opportunity or delay.
- Ground the proposal: daggers pinning the map corners, Jaina's hand flat on one quarter of the city map instead of the whole thing, chalk marking grain nodes, Uther looking at the plan like a man hearing one last door open.
- Once the plan exists, Arthas has to decide whether he waits for it or marches beside it.
- Inside: spending Kel'Thuzad's teaching against his plague, the first thing she has done with it; does not fit the sanctum, and she wants it to fit one way or the other; one quarter of the map, not the whole city, and that restraint again; won't trust it yet, and is already working out the ride

### Beat 6
- Arthas refuses to bet Stratholme on waiting. He marches the main column at once. Jaina, Sylvanas, and Cyndia can ride ahead and try the granaries, but the army moves now.
- Arthas's answer to uncertainty is still motion, Uther stays with the main column because the break has not happened yet, and the source plan and the army march now run in parallel against the same clock.
- They do not know whether the source team can buy enough time to matter or what Arthas will do if Jaina says the city is already too late.
- Ground the order: Arthas's gauntlet striking the map beside Stratholme, staff officers moving before the last words are finished, Falric turning for the tent flap on the first clear command.
- The chapter stops being a briefing and becomes a race.

### Beat 7
- The camp turns over fast. Quartermasters shout and tack comes late. Sylvanas and Jaina are at the horse lines with Falric. At the supply crates, a quartermaster's aide brings Cyndia their route packet. He stops to look past her at the column forming on the Stratholme road. He hands a sealed manifest to a waiting grain carter and tells him it is for the Stratholme granaries. Then he gives Cyndia the farewell.
- The camp can throw a fast rider team out ahead of the army within minutes. Cyndia hears the fourth farewell clearly, and she knows it from Chapter 9. The manifest reads as ordinary supply work in a camp full of it.
- They do not know who the aide is, or whether the head start is already too small.
- Ground the handoff: fresh reins still stiff from the tack chest, a route packet slapped onto a crate and pinned under a knife, May the stars keep your road landing in the middle of shouted orders.
- Second read (plant only; the prose must not explain it): he is checking that the army marches on schedule, and the manifest sends grain and orders into Stratholme before the army arrives.
- Cyndia carries it to Sylvanas.

### Beat 8
- At the mount-up Cyndia reports it in a few words: the aide, the manifest, the farewell. She adds that it is an odd way round for a camp clerk to say goodbye. Sylvanas asks Falric and the nearest quartermaster for the aide. Neither knows the face, and the column is already moving. Sylvanas tells Cyndia to keep a record from now on: where, when, and what face. Cyndia writes the first entry in the back of her relay book before she mounts.
- The farewell has come back from another stranger, the one question Sylvanas has time to ask comes up empty, and Stratholme outranks a clerk nobody can name. Sylvanas has the Scholomance crack and the city clock to carry, and the record is what she can spare.
- They do not know what the record will add up to, or when Sylvanas will have time to read it.
- Ground the report: Cyndia's pencil moving on a page braced against her saddle, Falric already shouting the next order over his shoulder, the grain carter's wagon turning onto the Stratholme road ahead of them.
- Second read (plant only; the prose must not explain it): nobody knows the aide because he is nobody's aide. "Odd way round" is the phrase being a Kaldorei blessing put word for word into Common.
- Once they mount, there is no room left for talk.
- Inside: a fourth face, and one question, and nothing; a clerk nobody can name against a city on a clock; writes it down because a Farstrider writes down what she can't use yet; one phrase for too many faces, or too many faces for one phrase, and no time for either

## Scene 3: The Head Start

**The action:** The road gives Sylvanas her first hard proof that Jaina is trying to use the dangerous knowledge the right way.
**Cast:** Sylvanas, Jaina, Cyndia.
**Starting point:** The main army is marching behind them and the city clock is already running.

### Beat 9
- The three women ride hard for Stratholme. Jaina is already breaking the city into real targets she can reach. Sylvanas decides to ride with the attempt and leave the rest for the city to prove.
- Jaina is spending the theory instead of sitting with it, Sylvanas puts her own speed and authority behind that attempt even while the crack remains, and the partnership now runs under the city's clock as well as its own.
- They do not know whether the source plan can beat the city's spread, or whether success or failure will change Sylvanas's read of the sanctum enough to matter.
- Ground the ride: three horses stretching into a faster line than the road wants to give them, Jaina shouting granary names and district order into the wind, the main column's dust starting to rise behind them.
- Chapter 16 opens on this same ride and the first attempt to kill the plague before it can finish the city.
