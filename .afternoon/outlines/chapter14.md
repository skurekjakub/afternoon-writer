# Chapter 14: The Revelation

**POV:** Jaina Proudmoore (limited third, absolute)
**When:** Directly follows Chapter 13. Opens on the road north after the Caer Darrow escape, breaks through a live undead crossing in the Eastweald, and closes at dusk after the Hearthglen relay and a stream stop.
**Where:** Eastweald road north from the Barov shore toward Hearthglen, crossing an overrun stone ford before late afternoon slides toward dusk.
**Travel:** On horseback from Darrowmere shore, forced onto foot briefly in the mud of an ambushed ford, then back to a hard gallop after the relay with one brief water stop.
**Characters:** Jaina, Sylvanas, Cyndia, a Farstrider relay rider, cult-steered risen dead at the ford.
**Goal:** Give Sylvanas the full plague chain without confessing the three prices, survive an overrun crossing where the Scholomance crack nearly gets them killed, and race north before Arthas outruns them.

**Jaina's starting position:**
- Kel'Thuzad is dead, and the full plague model sits complete in Jaina's head.
- She bought it with three true things about herself: that she went north without Antonidas's leave because she wants him proud of her, that when Arthas ended their romance she did not fight him because part of her wanted her books, and that she still thinks he would have listened if she had stayed, and that she has been happy learning at Kel'Thuzad's table.
- Sylvanas saw the calm room, the tea, and the chalk—not the trade paid inside it.
- Sylvanas now rides half a length ahead-right, watching where she used to trust.
- Cyndia holds the left flank and carries the relay. Lyriel is still dead.
- They are riding north for Hearthglen, where Sylvanas sent Arthas, with no fresh news of how it went.
- Jaina carries the stolen grimoire and a bundle of Kel'Thuzad's journals in her saddlebag, including the closed volume from his desk, which she has not opened.

- How ugly Sylvanas's climb down through Scholomance really was compared to Jandice's curated halls.
- Whether the Scholomance crack will hold or shatter the next time blood is on the line.
- What has happened at Hearthglen since Sylvanas sent Arthas north.

- No Jandice payoff.

- No Antonidas call and no Silvermoon runner. Both institutions stay off this road; their pressure arrives in Chapter 18.
- No inner statement from Jaina about what being right alone leads to.

**End of chapter state:**
- Sylvanas now has the full chain: Lich King, Burning Legion, resonance through living flesh, ward failure, Nathrezim journal note, and Kel'Thuzad's expected return as a lich.
- Jaina learns Sylvanas climbed through peasant holding rooms, failed bodies, and moving hands and jaws in trays that Jandice hid from her.
- At an overrun Eastweald ford, Sylvanas's half-beat pause on Jaina's ward warning nearly gets them both killed, proving the Scholomance crack is live and dangerous in combat.
- A Farstrider relay reports Hearthglen barely held, Uther is on the field, and Arthas is breaking camp at Hearthglen to march on Stratholme.
- At the stream crossing, washing mud and blood off their hands, Sylvanas asks once how Jaina got Kel'Thuzad to talk. Jaina gives the true, partial answer (`"He wanted someone to understand him. I did."`), withholding the three prices, and Sylvanas does not press.

## Character Arcs

### Jaina
- She opens believing clean facts can bridge the frozen half-length between their horses if she buries the three sentences she paid.
- Sylvanas tears the polite sheen off her tour with the butchered peasant trays, and the ford ambush proves silence and doubt can draw real blood.
- When Sylvanas drags her back out of the mud and later asks the one direct question at the stream, Jaina feels how badly she wants to spill the truth—and holds it back anyway.
- By the end, she has answered a direct question short and rides on with the omission heavy between them.

### Sylvanas through Jaina's POV
- She listens like a ranger checking cracked ice—sharper and colder because the sanctum hurt her worse than she will show.
- At the ford, her half-beat hesitation before trusting Jaina's shout almost kills them both; the fierce way she hauls Jaina clear in the mud afterward says everything she refuses to speak aloud.
- At the stream, she asks one quiet question, holds Jaina's gaze a beat too long when the answer stops short, and swings back into the saddle without pushing.

### Partnership
- This chapter takes the frozen quiet from Scholomance and smashes it straight into a live road fight.
- The half-beat pause in the mud shows the exact physical cost of lost trust—and the desperate closeness of pulling each other out alive keeps the unspoken pull vibrating underneath the hurt.
- Nothing is cleanly forgiven yet; both choose the race to Hearthglen over tearing the wound open on the road.

## Scene 1: The Road North

**The action:** Jaina rides inside a frozen silence vibrating with unspoken hurt, decides to withhold the three prices, and briefs the full threat chain while Sylvanas forces the butchered bodies back into it.
**Cast:** Jaina, Sylvanas, Cyndia.
**Starting point:** Kel'Thuzad is dead, the horses are pounding north off the Barov shore, and Sylvanas sits half a horse length ahead-right without looking back.

### Beat 1
- The silence off the lake shore vibrates with hurt neither woman will touch. Sylvanas rides half a horse length ahead on Jaina's right, bow across her shoulder, jaw set; Cyndia glances once across the gap and keeps her eyes front. Jaina splits Scholomance cleanly in two: everything Kel'Thuzad taught her goes on the table now, and the three sentences she paid—Antonidas's pride, Arthas and her books, being happy at his table—stay locked behind her teeth.
- Jaina knows Sylvanas dropped an army and climbed a wet cliff to pull her out only to find tea and chalk; calling the omission road necessity feels thin even inside her own head, because half of it is plain shame.
- She does not know whether Sylvanas will take the clean briefing or demand the truth of that doorway.
- Ground the silence: lake-damp reins slick in Jaina's gloves, the bundle of stolen journals thumping against her knee in the saddlebag, Sylvanas's rigid shoulders half a length ahead, hoofbeats hammering Eastweald dirt with no easy talk between them.
- Jaina spurs close enough to make herself heard and starts talking.
- Inside: everything he told her, nothing she told him; road necessity, and it is, and it is also shame; Sylvanas climbed through hell for her and won't even look back; does she need the three sentences to fight a lich; no; then why does keeping them feel like lying to her face

### Beat 2
- Riding side-by-side at a brisk trot, Jaina delivers the field chain stripped to the bone: the Lich King in Northrend, the Burning Legion behind him, resonance waking dead flesh through living grain-carriers, and why border wards that stop steel miss the plague entirely. Sylvanas cuts across her mid-sentence, voice like cold iron, naming what she climbed through two floors above Jaina's tea: penned peasants behind grates, failed bodies stacked on draining boards, severed hands and jaws still twitching in metal trays.
- Sylvanas now holds the chain, and her interruption rips the clean academic gloss straight off Jaina's memory of Scholomance. Jaina has to face how carefully Jandice curated every hall she walked—and still has no way to stop the plague at city scale.
- They do not know how to turn resonance theory into a field countermeasure, or whether Jaina can speak about Kel'Thuzad's work without sounding like she sat too long at his desk.
- Ground the cut-in: a small arcane diagram Jaina traces above her glove flickering out as Sylvanas's voice slices across it, both horses pinning their ears at the sharp tone, reins biting deep into Jaina's palm when Sylvanas names the twitching trays.
- The theory is nailed back to butchered flesh whether Jaina can stomach it or not.
- Inside: shown the scrubbed lecture halls and toured them like a visiting mage; peasants in trays two floors above her teacup; tries to slot the bodies back into the spellwork and they won't stay neat; the heat in Sylvanas's voice when she named them, and Jaina earned every blade of it

### Beat 3
- Jaina forces out the uglier piece she held back two minutes too long: Kel'Thuzad expected to outlive his own death and rise again as a lich. She adds what sits in her saddlebag—the stolen grimoire and his journals, including one she leafed through between sittings about the Nathrezim, demons who wear borrowed faces—while keeping the closed desk volume untouched.
- The delayed warning about lichdom lands badly, turning Kel'Thuzad's quiet smile in the sanctum into expectation rather than defeat. Sylvanas's knuckles flex once white on the rein, another small silence snapping shut between them—until Cyndia snaps a closed fist up at the bend ahead.
- They do not know how to block a lich's return or what waits inside the unopened desk volume.
- Ground the pause: saddle leather creaking sharply after Jaina says lich, Sylvanas's hand tightening on the rein without turning her face, Cyndia's sudden hand-signal pulling all three mounts off the crown of the road.
- Talk dies as the road drops toward a choked stone crossing.

## Scene 2: The Broken Line

**The action:** An overrun Eastweald ford forces a brutal close-quarters fight where Sylvanas's new half-beat hesitation nearly gets them both killed in the mud.
**Cast:** Jaina, Sylvanas, Cyndia, cult-steered risen dead.
**Starting point:** The crossing ahead is blocked by smashed refugee carts and cult-steered dead, and going around costs hours Arthas will not wait.

### Beat 4
- At a narrow stone ford over a swollen Eastweald creek, overturned refugee carts and bloated draft horses jam the banks. A tight pack of risen dead holds the crossing—not wandering village ghouls, but bodies herded cleanly into the choke point between smashed wheels, turning in unison the moment the horses crest the rise.
- Going miles out of their way around the creek risks losing Hearthglen altogether; cutting straight through the ford is the only way north, and the dead are positioned to funnel riders off horseback into the mud.
- They do not know whether a hidden cult trap sits buried under the ford stones or how many more dead lie crouched behind the wagon beds.
- Ground the ambush: splintered wagon spokes jutting out of brown water, gray hands dragging themselves over wet cart axles, crows bursting up from dead horses as Sylvanas slides her bow clear at a canter.
- Sylvanas calls the breach, Cyndia swings wide to hold the horses and the near bank, and Sylvanas and Jaina dismount into the mud to crack the choke point.

### Beat 5
- Halfway across the slicked stones, dead hands erupt from behind an overturned hay-cart while Jaina catches the cold sting of a buried necromantic snare wired straight under the wagon wheel Sylvanas is vaulting toward. Jaina shouts `"Stop—ward line under the axle!"` Before Scholomance, Sylvanas would have twisted away on the first syllable. Here, she hesitates for one raw fraction of a beat—eyes flicking to Jaina's face and down to the mud to verify it herself.
- That half-beat pause lets the snare snap hot beneath Sylvanas's boot and pulls three risen dead straight into the broken gap between them. Jaina lunges forward, throwing a cracking frost barrier right across Sylvanas's knee as cold fire rips her own hem; Sylvanas drops to one knee beside Jaina in the mire, loosing two arrows point-blank past Jaina's ear and grabbing Jaina's coat hard at the ribs to haul her backward out of clawing fingers.
- For five chaotic heartbeats neither knows whether the barrier will hold or whether the Scholomance crack just killed them both in an anonymous creek.
- Ground the collision: frost cracking loud as breaking glass against dark ward-light, bowstring snapping inches from Jaina's cheek, mud spraying across both coats, Sylvanas's gloved hand locked fierce against Jaina's ribs yanking her back against her own shoulder as a ghoul's teeth snap shut on empty air.
- Together, shoulder pressed to shoulder in the churned bank, they kill the last bodies choking the wheel-gap.
- Inside: she paused; half a heartbeat, checking the ground herself instead of moving on Jaina's voice; the snare snapped because of that half-beat; Sylvanas's arm hard across her ribs pulling her clear, breath hot against Jaina's ear in the mud; alive by inches, and both of them know exactly why it was inches

### Beat 6
- Cyndia drops the last two stragglers off the far bank and brings the blowing horses across the cleared stones. Jaina and Sylvanas stand panting in knee-deep mud, frost crusted up Jaina's cuffs, Sylvanas's half-healed shoulder wound bleeding freshly through her coat where the wrench pulled Lyriel's knot. Sylvanas lets go of Jaina's sleeve a beat slower than combat requires; neither mentions the pause.
- Both women know what that fraction of a second almost bought them—and that the sanctum frost is no longer private pride, it is a blade hanging over their necks. Still, neither opens the wound here; they swing back onto the horses and push off the crossing before more dead draw to the noise.
- They do not know whether the next fight will forgive another half-beat of doubt.
- Ground the aftermath: muddy water running red off Sylvanas's knuckles, Jaina's fingers trembling as she wipes black muck off her cheek, Cyndia taking in the blown ward-crater and the way Sylvanas's hand leaves Jaina's coat without saying a word.
- They spur out of the creek bed just as hoofbeats hammer down the north road toward them.

## Scene 3: The Relay and the Stream

**The action:** The Hearthglen relay turns the ride into a desperate sprint, and at a stream stop Sylvanas asks the one direct question Jaina answers short.
**Cast:** Jaina, Sylvanas, Cyndia, Farstrider relay rider.
**Starting point:** Blood and ford-mud are still wet on their hands, the near-miss is raw between them, and Hearthglen lies ahead.

### Beat 7
- A lathered Farstrider relay rider catches their line north of the ford and passes a sealed tube to Cyndia at a canter: Hearthglen barely held, Uther is on the field, and Arthas is already breaking camp to march straight on Stratholme.
- This is their first hard word since Sylvanas aimed Arthas north. The trail is no longer cool—the fuse is already burning toward Lordaeron's second city, and every minute counts.
- They do not know whether they can catch Arthas before the army marches out or how much of Hearthglen is left standing.
- Ground the relay: dust-caked green cloak slapping hard in the wind, a brass dispatch tube slapped hand to hand at a gallop, a dark smear of pyre smoke smudging the northern sky low over the trees.
- The horses are blowing too hard for the final push without water; Sylvanas signals a three-minute stop at the next clear creek.

### Beat 8
- Kneeling at the clear stream bank while the horses drink, Jaina and Sylvanas scrub dried blood and ford-mud off their hands side by side in the cold water. With the near-miss at the bridge still buzzing in the air between them, Sylvanas keeps her eyes on the dark water for a breath, then asks quietly how Jaina got Kel'Thuzad to give it up. Jaina looks at her wet, scarred knuckles and gives the true, partial answer: `"He wanted someone to understand him. I did."` Sylvanas lifts her eyes and holds Jaina's gaze a beat too long—hurt, distrust, and something fierce and unspoken locked tight behind her stare—says nothing, wipes her wet hand down her leather thigh, and swings back into the saddle.
- Every word Jaina spoke is true, and it leaves the three prices buried. She has now withheld them directly from Sylvanas's face, knowing Sylvanas will not beg for the rest.
- They do not know whether Sylvanas believed her, whether the half-beat pause is gone or waiting, or if they will ever get a quiet road to finish this sentence.
- Ground the stop: blown horses sucking cold creek water, pink water swirling off Sylvanas's freshly torn cuff, Jaina's wet glove gripping the reins hard enough to creak, dusk closing purple over the Eastweald with Hearthglen's smoke thickening ahead.
- Chapter 15 opens as they ride straight into Hearthglen's smoking edge.
- Inside: true, every word, which is the cowardice of it; he wanted to be understood, and she was lonely enough in that cellar to keep up, and that stays behind her teeth; Sylvanas's eyes holding hers a beat too long, raw from the bridge and raw from the doorway; wanted her to ask again, and relieved she didn't, and hating both
