# Chapter 14: The Revelation

**POV:** Jaina Proudmoore (limited third, absolute)
**When:** Directly follows Chapter 13. Opens on the road north after the Caer Darrow escape and closes at dusk with the party pushing hard toward Hearthglen and Arthas after the Hearthglen relay.
**Where:** Eastweald road north from the Barov shore toward Hearthglen, late afternoon sliding toward dusk.
**Travel:** Back on horseback after the lake crossing, with one relay pause and one water stop.
**Characters:** Jaina, Sylvanas, Cyndia, a Farstrider relay rider.
**Goal:** Brief Sylvanas on what Scholomance taught her, keep back what it cost, and keep moving north before Arthas outruns them.

## Scene 1: The Road

**The action:** Jaina sorts what she can say, reads the changed line, and decides what she will not tell Sylvanas.
**Cast:** Jaina, Sylvanas, Cyndia.
**Starting point:** Kel'Thuzad is dead, the knowledge is in Jaina's head, and the road north cannot wait.

### Beat 1
- Jaina splits Scholomance into two piles: what she learned and what she paid for it. The first pile can be reported. The second is three sentences she said aloud in that room.
- Jaina is already separating usable facts from private damage, and the world outside Caer Darrow feels too ordinary after days underground.
- They do not know whether that split will hold once Sylvanas starts listening.
- Ground the road: reins still damp from the lake crossing, late light over fields that are not clean, the bundle of journals knocking against Jaina's knee in the saddlebag.
- Once the facts are stacked, Jaina has to decide what to do with the rest.

### Beat 2
- Sylvanas rides half a horse length ahead on Jaina's right. No one speaks.
- Cyndia notices the silence and leaves it alone, and the old easy talk between Jaina and Sylvanas is gone for now.
- They do not know whether Sylvanas will ask directly about the sanctum.
- Ground the silence: hoofbeats on hard Eastweald dirt, Cyndia's glance across at them and then forward again, Sylvanas's bow riding on her back.
- Jaina realizes what kind of conversation this has to be.

### Beat 3
- Jaina decides to give Sylvanas everything Kel'Thuzad told her and nothing she told him: Antonidas's pride, Arthas and her books, and how happy she was at his table stay with her.
- The omission is useful and dishonest at the same time. Jaina frames it as road necessity, and she knows shame is mixed into it. The gap between what happened and what she will say is now deliberate.
- They do not know whether Sylvanas will accept the cleaner version.
- Ground the decision: saddle leather creaking between hoofbeats, Jaina's hand closing on her pack strap, the remembered scratch of a pen in a quiet room.
- The decision makes the briefing possible.
- Inside: everything he told her, nothing she told him; road necessity, and it is, and it is also shame; does Sylvanas need the prices to fight a lich; no; then why does the gap feel like a lie; files it under operational and does not believe the label

## Scene 2: The Briefing

**The action:** Jaina gives Sylvanas the threat picture in a few sentences, and Sylvanas drags the human cost back into it.
**Cast:** Jaina, Sylvanas, Cyndia.
**Starting point:** Jaina has chosen to report the product, not the price.

### Beat 4
- Jaina gives the chain in a few lines of field language: Lich King in Northrend, the Legion above him, resonance through living bodies, wards that block force and miss this. Sylvanas cuts across with what she saw on the way down: peasants in holding rooms, failed bodies, hands and jaws still moving in trays. Jaina is forced to admit her own route was curated.
- Sylvanas now has the chain. Jandice hid the ugliest work from Jaina, and Jaina still has no field-ready method to stop the plague at scale.
- They do not know how to apply the countermeasure across a city, or whether Jaina can explain the theory without sounding too much like the man who taught it to her.
- Ground the interruption: a quick arcane map over Jaina's glove faltering when Sylvanas cuts in, horse ears twitching at the sharpness in Sylvanas's voice, reins biting into Jaina's palm when the bodies are named.
- The theory is tied back to flesh whether Jaina likes it or not.
- Inside: shown the clean rooms and toured them like a visiting lecturer; peasants in trays two floors from her tea; tries to fit the bodies into the model, where they belong, and they won't stay put; Sylvanas's voice when she named them, and she earned that

### Beat 5
- Jaina says the part she should have said sooner: Kel'Thuzad expected to survive his own death as a lich. The delay lands badly. She also briefs them on what she carried out: the stolen grimoire and a bundle of Kel'Thuzad's journals. One journal describes the Nathrezim, demons who wear borrowed faces. She gives it a sentence and moves on.
- Bodily death is not the end state Kel'Thuzad planned for, and his calm in the sanctum now reads as expectation rather than courage. She leafed through the Nathrezim volume on his shelves between sittings. She has not opened the one from his desk.
- They do not know how to stop a lich transition, or what the unopened volume holds.
- Ground the pause: tack leather creaking in the silence, Sylvanas's hand flexing once on the bow grip, Cyndia cutting her eyes across and then forward again.
- The briefing is complete, and the price is still in Jaina's mouth.

## Scene 3: The Relay

**The action:** The Hearthglen relay turns the road into a race, and at the water stop Sylvanas asks the one question Jaina has decided not to answer.
**Cast:** Jaina, Sylvanas, Cyndia, Farstrider relay rider.
**Starting point:** The usable truth is on the road, and the price is still Jaina's alone.

### Beat 6
- A Farstrider relay rider catches the line with Hearthglen news. Hearthglen barely held. Uther is on the field. Arthas is breaking camp at Hearthglen to march on Stratholme. They press the horses harder.
- This is the first news of Hearthglen since Sylvanas sent Arthas toward it. The party is no longer following a cold trail. They are chasing a live fuse.
- They do not know whether they can reach Arthas before he marches, or how many other grain-fed sites are already too far gone.
- Ground the relay: a dust-streaked rider falling in beside Cyndia, a dispatch tube passed hand to hand at a canter, a dark smear of smoke low on the northern horizon.
- They have to water the horses before the last push.

### Beat 7
- At a stream crossing, with the horses drinking, Sylvanas asks how Jaina got him to give it up. Jaina says, "He wanted someone to understand him. I did." Sylvanas looks at her a moment longer, says nothing, and remounts.
- The answer is true and leaves out the price. Jaina has now withheld it from a direct question, and she knows the difference.
- They do not know whether Sylvanas believed her, or whether the question will come again.
- Ground the stop: horses blowing at the water, Jaina's wet glove tight on the reins, dusk coming on over the Eastweald with smoke ahead.
- Chapter 15 opens with arrival at Hearthglen and Arthas's war camp.
- Inside: true, every word, which is the trick of it; he wanted to be understood, and so did she, and that stays out too; Sylvanas looked a moment too long; would she have pressed, before Scholomance; relieved she didn't, and disappointed she didn't
