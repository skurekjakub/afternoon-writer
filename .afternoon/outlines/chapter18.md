# Chapter 18: The Wall

**POV:** Jaina Proudmoore in Scenes 1-3, Sylvanas Windrunner in Scenes 4-5 (limited third, absolute)
**When:** Directly follows Chapter 17. Opens on Stratholme's inner wall after the Culling, runs through the afternoon on the south road and a night in a Farstrider field camp, and closes the next morning with the line riding north toward the coast.
**Where:** Stratholme's inner wall above the main gate, the south road out of the city, then the field camp of Lor'themar's forward mobilization south of Stratholme.
**Travel:** Static witness position on the wall, descent on foot, horseback south through the afternoon, camp overnight, then horseback north at dawn.
**Characters:** Jaina, Sylvanas, Cyndia, Uther, Antonidas by sending stone, Vereesa, the Farstrider escort.
**Goal:** Get off the wall, read what Stratholme says about the source, answer both institutions, and turn after Arthas.

**The pair's starting position:**
- The source plan was real and worked locally, but Stratholme had already pushed too much contaminated grain into ovens, homes, and bodies.
- Arthas answered that failure with the Culling and carried it through to the end. Uther could not stop him this time.
- Each woman knows her own hand in it. Sylvanas aimed Arthas in Chapter 11. Jaina answered him alone at the gate, over Sylvanas.
- Jaina paid Kel'Thuzad with the truth about Arthas: when he ended their romance she did not fight him, because part of her wanted her books, and she still thinks he would have listened to her if she had stayed. Sylvanas does not know this yet.
- Arthas came out of the gate with clean hands.
- Sylvanas turned from the farewell carter to take the wall. Cyndia keeps the record.
- Neither institution has reached them since Scholomance.

**Still in the dark about:**
- Whether the source model now points to a single broadcaster, a relay chain, or something else entirely.
- Where Arthas goes next.
- Whether Antonidas can still speak to the person Jaina is after Stratholme.
- How far the Convocation will go to drag Sylvanas home, and what Lor'themar has left to spend.
- What each of them is willing to lose by saying north out loud.

**Hold back:**
- No explicit Frostmourne identification.
- No softening of the Culling into something noble because it prevented a wider turn.
- No narrated diagnosis of Jaina's likeness to Kel'Thuzad and Arthas. One concrete beat carries her guilt.
- No confession of the Chapter 12 prices. Jaina tells Sylvanas at sea in Chapter 19.
- No spoken absolution between the women.
- No Sylvanas access to Jaina's exact words on the sending stone in Scenes 4-5, and no Jaina interiority there.
- No spoken thesis from Sylvanas about borders or Quel'Thalas. Her refusal of the recall is silent.
- No ship specifics yet. That belongs to Chapter 19.

**End of chapter state:**
- Sylvanas's gauntlet on Jaina's shoulder gets Jaina moving; Jaina leans into the weight of it for half a breath before turning. On the descent the verification pause is gone, and Sylvanas rides level with Jaina again. The crack is closed.
- Antonidas reaches Jaina on the road, offers extraction, and asks her home. She gives him the Lich King and the Legion, keeps back the room and its price, and answers `"Alive. Not yet."`
- Jaina's model now points past contaminated grain toward a source or relay broadcasting the activating frequency.
- Uther, riding west to warn Terenas, tells them Arthas is taking ships north to Northrend with the men who stayed through Stratholme.
- That night Vereesa reaches the field camp with the Convocation's recall. Sylvanas refuses it by silence: she never takes the document and gives it no answer.
- Vereesa reports Lor'themar's price: he spent nearly everything to buy this small forward mobilization, the camp they are sleeping in.
- Jaina calls Antonidas herself without being asked, tells him she is going north, and cuts the Dalaran line cold.
- At dawn Vereesa rides home with the recall unanswered. Sylvanas gives the order `"North."` The relay network thins toward the coast.

## Scene 1: The Wall (Jaina)

**The action:** Jaina stays on the parapet long enough for the Culling to sink into her body, and faces her own hand in it.
**Cast:** Jaina, Sylvanas, distant purge teams below.
**Starting point:** The city is dead, the source plan failed by timing rather than bad theory, and Arthas has already come through the gate with clean hands.

### Beat 1
- Jaina does not move. A city this large should still be making human noise, and it is making none. Under the shock her body reads the city-wide after-hum of the plague before her mind turns it into theory.
- Sylvanas stays beside her in quiet watch, and the parapet holds Jaina in place through comprehension rather than fear. The dead air coming off Stratholme behaves differently from anything Jaina measured in the villages.
- They do not know what the dead city's signal will teach her, or whether Jaina can pry her hands off the stone under her own power.
- Ground the wall: warm parapet stone under both palms, fire eating through timber block by block, bread-taste wrongness thick at the back of her throat.
- Jaina stands rigid at the stone while the quiet city hums through her palms.

### Beat 2
- Jaina looks at her own hands on the parapet, still gray with flour from the stores, and down at the gate where Arthas stepped out with his clean. She hears herself at that gate saying `"Not cleanly. Not now."` and Sylvanas starting to speak before she cut across her. The sentence she gave Kel'Thuzad about Arthas comes back to her word for word.
- She answered Arthas alone, and the answer was true, and this is what he did with it. She told Kel'Thuzad that Arthas would have listened to her if she had stayed; today she stood beside him, and he listened. Sylvanas, a pace away, is staring at the same gate arch.
- They do not know whether Jaina will ever say any of this aloud, or to whom.
- Ground the guilt: flour packed into the creases of her knuckles, heat rising through the stone from the fires below, Sylvanas's gloved hands resting on the parapet a forearm's length from hers.
- Jaina does not wipe the flour off her hands.

## Scene 2: The Anchor (Jaina)

**The action:** Sylvanas's hand breaks Jaina's paralysis, and the descent proves the Scholomance crack has closed.
**Cast:** Jaina, Sylvanas, Cyndia.
**Starting point:** Hours have passed on the wall, and Jaina still cannot make herself turn away from the gate.

### Beat 3
- Hours later Jaina is still fixed to the parapet when Sylvanas sets a leather gauntlet on her left shoulder. Jaina leans into the weight of that hand for half a breath before turning, and Sylvanas says, `"South."` On the watch-steps down, Cyndia already has the horses, water, and tack ready without asking either woman for a word. When Jaina speaks once on the stair, Sylvanas acts immediately—the small verification pause she kept since Scholomance is gone. Out on the south road, the half-length gap disappears too; Sylvanas draws her horse up level with Jaina's.
- Sylvanas waited until touch could move Jaina instead of breaking her; `"South."` gives Jaina permission to stop staring, and the half-breath lean answers it without words. On the road, the crack from Scholomance is shut in practice, paid for by Stratholme.
- They do not know whether south leads to escape or the next real move, or what waits for them once the smoke falls behind.
- Ground the contact and descent: warm leather gauntlet resting solidly on Jaina's left shoulder, smoke caught in both their cloaks, worn watch-steps under boots with Sylvanas's back a step ahead, and Sylvanas's mount pulling up nose-to-nose beside Jaina's on the gate road.
- They ride south level with each other, leaving Stratholme's smoke behind their shoulders.

## Scene 3: The Road (Jaina)

**The action:** Antonidas reaches Jaina and takes her first refusal, the broadcast model clicks into place, and Uther brings the news that points north.
**Cast:** Jaina, Sylvanas, Cyndia, Antonidas by sending stone, Uther and a few riders.
**Starting point:** The crack is closed, and Stratholme's smoke still hangs behind them on the south road.

### Beat 4
- Antonidas reaches Jaina by sending stone. He has fragments of Stratholme, offers extraction, and asks her to come home. She gives him the Lich King and the Burning Legion in a few plain sentences—the first time Dalaran has heard them—and tells him nothing about Kel'Thuzad's room or what it cost. To "come home" she answers, `"Alive. Not yet."`
- This is Jaina's first refusal of Dalaran. Antonidas now holds the real chain and is frightened by it, while Jaina cannot fit the wall into words that travel through a stone. She keeps the Scholomance price from him just as she kept it from Sylvanas.
- They do not know what Antonidas can do with the warning, or whether his voice will still sound like home after tonight.
- Ground the call: violet stone-light against Jaina's glove, Stratholme's smoke column still standing behind the riders while she speaks, the stone cooling in her palm before she drops it into the saddlebag beside the journals.
- Jaina closes the call with the Dalaran line still alive, barely, in her bag.
- Inside: he sounds old through the stone, or she hears him old now; gives him the Legion and keeps the room, the same cut she made for Sylvanas, and easier the second time; home, and what would she do there, lecture; not yet, and she doesn't know whether she means it

### Beat 5
- Once the call ends, the new model sharpens on the ride. Grain is an amplifier answering a signal sent from somewhere else; a whole city behaves like a network picking up a broadcast. Kel'Thuzad's death removed one relay, and the rest still runs.
- The countermeasure was sound and built for villages, not for a broadcast reaching a city. Stratholme's failure points Jaina past loaves and granaries toward whatever is transmitting.
- They do not know what object, place, or being is sending the signal, or how far away it sits.
- Ground the model shift: the frequency humming in Jaina's teeth like a tune she cannot drop, bread-taste thinning with distance while the pattern sharpens, ink notes balanced open on her thigh as her horse trots south.
- Jaina shuts her notebook knowing the real target lies far past the granaries.
- Inside: relief at having a problem again, and the smoke still behind her; a city behaving like a network, which is elegant, and she wishes it weren't; the countermeasure right and too small, and she reruns it to see if it was ever not too small; amplifier, so what is broadcasting, and how far

### Beat 6
- At dusk Uther and a handful of riders overtake them at a hard pace, cutting west. He reins in just long enough to tell them the orders he heard at the gate camp: Arthas is not riding home. He is marching for the coast to take ships north to Northrend with the men who stayed through Stratholme. Uther is riding hard to warn Terenas. He glances down at Jaina's flour-stained hands, says nothing about them, and spurs west.
- Arthas is sailing for Northrend with men who already proved at Stratholme they will follow him past anything, Uther is racing west for Capital City, and Jaina's broadcast model and the prince now point in the same direction. Nobody says north yet.
- They do not know whether three riders and an escort can cross a sea after him, or whether north holds the source, a trap, or both.
- Ground the meeting: Uther's horse lathered white across the chest, his hammer still clean at his saddlebow, dusk wind cooling over stubble fields as his riders pull away west.
- Cyndia turns the line off the road toward the Farstrider field camp for the night.

## Scene 4: The Camp (Sylvanas)

**The action:** Silvermoon arrives in the person of Sylvanas's sister, carrying the recall, Lor'themar's ruin, and a childhood name, and Sylvanas answers with silence.
**Cast:** Sylvanas, Vereesa, Jaina by the fire, Cyndia, the Farstrider escort, Vereesa's riders.
**Starting point:** Arthas is marching for ships north, the signal points the same way, and Silvermoon has finally caught up with them.

### Beat 7
- The Farstrider field camp is small and orderly under Stratholme's faint northern glow. After dark, Cyndia's bird-call intercept reads clean—friendly riders, which makes it worse. Sylvanas knows her sister's seat in the saddle before firelight hits her face. Vereesa dismounts in full Ranger Captain register, reads the Convocation's recall aloud, and holds the red-waxed vellum out between them. Sylvanas never lifts a hand to take it.
- The Convocation chose Vereesa instead of a clerk because family cuts deeper. The order suspends Sylvanas's foreign deployment, brands noncompliance as desertion, and cites both Stratholme and Millhaven's dead breaking on the border wards as proof this was never Silvermoon's war.
- They do not know how much of the document Vereesa believes, or how private this can stay with Jaina and the escort sitting in earshot around the fire.
- Ground the arrival and recall: bird-call signals at the treeline, a gray mare lathered across the chest in firelight, Jaina looking up from the notes in her lap and then down again, red wax bright on good vellum while Vereesa's formal voice cracks only after the last word.
- Vereesa stands in the firelight holding out an order Sylvanas refuses to touch.

### Beat 8
- Vereesa drops the clerk's phrasing and gives the real cost report. To keep this field line open even a few days longer, Lor'themar spent nearly everything he had left in Silvermoon, and what it bought is this camp: one small forward mobilization. The dry wit is burned out of him; he is down to chamber counts and bone, and even this escort may not survive the next session.
- Lor'themar's ruin is no rumor from a letter—it is the ring of bedrolls around Sylvanas's fire. At the horse line, Cyndia listens to her lord described hollowed out on Sylvanas's account and says nothing.
- They do not know whether Silvermoon's walls hold if the fight turns east, or what Cyndia thinks of the price.
- Ground the report: Vereesa's ink-stained fingers ticking off chamber votes against her palm, the small ring of bedrolls and tack around the fire, Cyndia motionless beside the picket line.
- Sylvanas looks around the small camp knowing it is all Lor'themar had left to spend.

### Beat 9
- Vereesa drops rank altogether and argues blood: Alleria gone, the Windrunner name gutted, home still open if Sylvanas rides back tonight. Once, low, she uses a childhood name. Sylvanas keeps her hands at her sides, says nothing, and looks north past the fire.
- Everyone in earshot sees the break. There is nothing for Vereesa to carry back except silence, and desertion stops being ink on vellum. Across the fire, Jaina has heard every word—including the childhood name—and Sylvanas minds Jaina hearing it more than she minds the recall itself.
- They do not know whether Vereesa can carry that silence back to Silvermoon without breaking, or what Jaina will do with what she just witnessed.
- Ground the silence: the red-waxed recall still gripped in Vereesa's hand, a log settling in the coals, Sylvanas's jaw set and eyes fixed on the dark north, Jaina's head bent over her closed notebook across the flames.
- Sylvanas refuses Silvermoon without speaking a single word.
- Inside: the childhood name, and it lands, and she gives it nothing; Alleria gone, and now she is the one not coming home; desertion, then, and the word fits better than she expected; the Convocation sent her sister because they knew; Jaina across the fire heard every word of that name, and Sylvanas minds that more than the recall

## Scene 5: North (Sylvanas)

**The action:** Jaina cuts Dalaran without being asked, Vereesa rides home with the unanswered order, and Sylvanas gives the word that turns them toward the coast.
**Cast:** Sylvanas, Jaina, Vereesa, Cyndia, the Farstrider escort.
**Starting point:** Sylvanas has refused Silvermoon in silence, and the night camp is still waiting for the morning order.

### Beat 10
- Without looking at Sylvanas or waiting to be asked, Jaina stands up from the fire, walks out to the dark edge of camp, and wakes Antonidas's sending stone herself. Sylvanas cannot hear the words—only the set of Jaina's back and jaw. It is not a request; it is notice. The call is short. When it ends, the violet light dies cold and flat, unlike the lingering warmth on the afternoon road, and Jaina drops the dark crystal to the bottom of her saddlebag.
- Parallel severance: Sylvanas never asked Jaina to cut Dalaran, and Jaina never asked Sylvanas to cut Silvermoon. Neither speaks about it when Jaina walks back toward the fire.
- They do not know what Antonidas said back before the stone went dead, or what cutting the line cost Jaina.
- Ground the severance: violet crystal light flaring briefly at the camp edge, Jaina's straight spine and lifted chin against the trees, the stone going dead dark before she buries it at the bottom of her bag.
- Sylvanas watches the closed bag longer than she watches the fire, knowing Jaina is riding north with her.
- Inside: can't hear the words and doesn't need them; never asked Jaina to cut Dalaran, and Jaina never asked her to cut Silvermoon; glad, and refuses to look at how much; the stone gone dark at the bottom of a bag, and she watches the bag longer than the fire

### Beat 11
- At first light Vereesa mounts and rides back toward Silvermoon with the unanswered recall folded inside her saddlebag. Before her horse's dust settles, Sylvanas turns to Cyndia and says one word: `"North."` Tents come down fast. Jaina is in her saddle before the word finishes sounding. Three rangers from Lor'themar's escort fall in behind them. As they push toward the northern harbors, the Farstrider relay posts thin out, salt cold replaces inland smoke, and Jaina's set face says the signal ahead is growing stronger.
- Vereesa accepts the refusal while hating what it means, and `"North."` is the only answer Silvermoon receives. Past the coast, there are no relay posts and no institutions left—only hired passage and the women riding together.
- They do not know what ship will take them to Northrend, or what waits across the water besides Arthas and the broadcast.
- Ground the turn: Vereesa's gray mare turning back toward Silvermoon, canvas struck and saddle-straps cinched in minutes, Jaina already mounted and looking north, salt wind hitting their faces before the sea comes into sight.
- The line rides hard for the northern coast, with the last Farstrider relay post falling behind them.
