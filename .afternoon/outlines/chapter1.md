# Chapter 1: The Border

**POV:** Sylvanas Windrunner (limited third, absolute)
**When:** Story start. No prior chapter.
**Where:** Southeastern Eversong Woods, on Farstrider patrol near the Amani frontier.
**Travel:** Thalassian horses for Sylvanas, Lor'themar, and the mounted patrol.
**Characters:** Sylvanas, Lor'themar, unnamed Farstrider patrol.
**Goal:** Finish the troll sweep, dodge the Convocation budget session, and stay outdoors one more day.

- **Worldbuilding references:** [Silvermoon & Quel'Thalas](stories/the-plague-road/locations/silvermoon-and-quelthalas.md), [Thalassian Horses](stories/the-plague-road/locations/thalassian-horses.md)
- **Character references:** [Sylvanas profile](stories/the-plague-road/characters/sylvanas.md), [Sylvanas voice](stories/the-plague-road/characters/voice-sheets/sylvanas-voice.md), [Lor'themar profile](stories/the-plague-road/characters/lorthemar.md), [Jaina profile](stories/the-plague-road/characters/jaina.md), [Jaina voice](stories/the-plague-road/characters/voice-sheets/jaina-voice.md), [Cyndia profile](stories/the-plague-road/characters/cyndia.md)

**Sylvanas's starting position:**
- Routine Amani pressure exists on the southeastern frontier.
- The ward network looks stable in every formal report.
- The Farstriders keep files on powerful human mages near the border.
- Jaina Proudmoore is one of the names worth watching.

**Still in the dark about:**
- Anything about the plague.
- What is wrong inside the ward-stones, or that it comes from leyline corruption.
- That a human mage has already crossed the ward boundary.
- Why Jaina is in Quel'Thalas.

**Hold back:**
- No trust between Sylvanas and Jaina.
- No proof that this is more than a human trespass.
- No Jaina dialogue before the chapter boundary.

**End of chapter state:**
- Sylvanas has felt one ward-stone read wrong under her own hand, and the mage's claim matches it.
- Jaina is in Farstrider custody and has to make her case directly to Sylvanas.
- Sylvanas knows the prisoner is Jaina Proudmoore, not a random trespasser.
- The breach has turned from patrol annoyance into a serious interrogation.

## Character Arcs

### Sylvanas
- She opens treating anything south of the wards as someone else's problem unless it touches her stones, roads, or riders.
- She wants to finish the troll work, stay on her horse, and avoid the Convocation budget session.
- A ward-stone hums wrong under her own palm. Then a runner reports a human mage inside the wards who says the leylines are wrong, and an easy patrol day becomes her problem.
- By the end, the border mess belongs to her, and the prisoner is waiting in her own holding space.

### Jaina through Sylvanas's POV
- Before Sylvanas sees her, Jaina is just the human mage who ruined an easy day.
- Sylvanas expects a polished Kirin Tor problem: spy, diplomat, or both.
- What she finds instead is a road-worn young mage who looks exhausted, furious, and very far from court.
- Even then, Jaina is still only the mage, the human, or the prisoner inside Sylvanas's head.

### Partnership
- There is no partnership here. One woman is in custody, and the other decides how hard the door stays shut.
- Jaina shifts from paperwork headache to the one person Sylvanas has to hear out.
- The chapter ends on their first hard face-to-face with no warmth and no softening.

## Scene 1: The Hunt

**The action:** Show Sylvanas in her element, have a ward-stone read wrong in her own hand, and bring the runner whose news confirms it.
**Cast:** Sylvanas, Lor'themar, mounted patrol, Amani raiders, Farstrider runner.
**Starting point:** Routine frontier work. Trolls are the only threat on the board.

### Beat 1
- Sylvanas finishes the sweep, dropping the last Amani axethrower cleanly as he breaks from cover.
- She reads the woods effortlessly—footing, range, openings. This is where she belongs, doing normal border work.
- They have no idea anything worse than trolls is out there.
- Ground the aftermath: a wet arrow impact, crushed ferns, troll blood in the sweet Eversong air, birdsong returning.
- The clean kill gives them a breather and proves exactly why Sylvanas would rather stay out here than ride back to the city.

### Beat 2
- In the quiet after the fight, Sylvanas stalls. She uses checking the ward-stones as an excuse to stretch the patrol south, and Lor'themar sees right through it. Then she checks the nearest stone herself, bare palm flat on the rune face, the way every Farstrider is taught.
- A healthy stone hums warm and even under the hand. This one hums out of step, and there is cold under the warmth, deep in the stone. Every formal report calls the network stable. She is no magister and cannot say what she is feeling.
- They don't know what is wrong with the stone, or whether it is the only one.
- Ground the check: steel wiped clean on a fern, moss on the stone's shoulder, the hum out of step under her palm, horses shifting under riders who know them perfectly.
- She tells Lor'themar it hums wrong. He calls it work for the magisters. She wipes her palm on her thigh and rides on south.
- Inside: stalling, and Lor'themar knows it, which annoys her more than the stone; real cold or an excuse she went looking for; every report says stable, signed by magisters who never put a palm on one; no word for what she felt, and resents needing one

### Beat 3
- They ride south in easy company. Lor'themar needles her about the court business she keeps dodging.
- He is the only one who can take her irritation without slowing her down. Sylvanas doubts even Cyndia expects a real threat from the south, and she keeps the cold stone to herself for the rest of the stretch.
- They don't know the day is about to crack open.
- Ground the ride: amber light through the canopy, a low ward-hum under the horses' strides, the cold still in Sylvanas's palm.
- An urgent rider comes hard from the south, cutting the easy rhythm in half.

### Beat 4
- A Farstrider runner arrives from Cyndia's post at full speed with the kind of report that ruins the whole patrol.
- A human mage crossed the first ward ring before dawn, got caught studying the stones without credentials, and claims the leylines are wrong. Sylvanas has had her hand on a wrong stone a short ride back. The mage's claim matches it.
- The scouts don't know the mage's identity, who sent her, or if her warning means anything.
- Ground the arrival: lather on the runner's horse, foam at the bit, clipped patrol shorthand from tired breathing.
- Sylvanas loses the rest of her easy day and has to treat this border breach as a serious threat.

### Beat 5
- She turns irritation into command. Lor'themar takes the patrol north to handle the politicians, while she rides hard for the Pass to judge the prisoner herself, with the runner beside her.
- An unauthorized human mage near the wards is too big to hand off, and a mage talking about wrong leylines fits no spy file she knows. Riding south, Sylvanas turns the problem over and grows certain it matters.
- She doesn't know she's riding to meet Jaina Proudmoore, or whether the intruder is hostile, reckless, or right.
- Ground the split and the ride: horses separating at the fork, the soft northern road giving way to hard southern track, warm forest air thinning into the mountain cold, ward-stones along the road pulsing faster.
- She hits the border post and demands Cyndia's report directly.
- Inside: glad of the excuse to miss the budget session, and irritated at being glad; spy, then why talk about leylines, and what spy says that to rangers; Lor'themar left holding the Convocation, and she will not thank him; the cold still in her palm, and she keeps rubbing it

## Scene 2: The Prisoner

**The action:** A generic border arrest becomes a high-stakes problem that locks them both into the next chapter.
**Cast:** Sylvanas, Cyndia, border rangers, Jaina.
**Starting point:** A human mage is in a holding cell. No verified identity. No trusted warning. Sylvanas has felt one stone read wrong.

### Beat 6
- Cyndia gives the raw facts of the arrest. They make the mage stranger.
- The mage arrived before dawn, hit the second ring, and instead of bolting, argued to stay and work. The ward-stones read as if they were malfunctioning because of something inside them. Her staff and gear sit in lockdown.
- Cyndia and Sylvanas have no clue if this human is arrogant, terrified, or completely correct.
- Ground the room: heavy stone walls, bright ward-light, rangers standing in stiff, tired alert.
- The report is close enough to what Sylvanas felt at the stone that she decides to see the prisoner's gear and then the prisoner, now.

### Beat 7
- On the way to the cell, Sylvanas stops at the lockdown rack. The mage's staff, travel kit, and a hand-drawn map sit under ranger guard.
- The map marks Thalassian ward sites in a human hand, with lines run up to them from the south. The stone Sylvanas checked this morning is circled. Cyndia has matched the Kirin Tor ink and the staff's make against the Farstrider file on human mages near the border.
- They don't know how the mage knew to circle that stone, or whether the map is a survey or a target list.
- Ground the gear: ink smudged where a thumb held the map flat in wind, the staff's crystal dim under the post's dampening ward, Cyndia's file open on the rack.
- Cyndia puts a name to the prisoner: Jaina Proudmoore, Antonidas's apprentice.

### Beat 8
- Sylvanas pulls back the door. The human in the chair breaks her picture of a pampered, court-trained nuisance.
- She expects a silver-tongued Kirin Tor diplomat. She gets a young mage in practical travel gear, worn boots, frost-burned knuckles, and ink stains, furious that nobody believes her.
- They still don't know why Antonidas let her walk out alone, or if he even knows where she is.
- Ground the reveal: cold ward-light on Jaina's face, the empty pegs where a staff would hang, the raw, exhausted anger she's projecting.
- This is a diplomatic and magical incident now, with a famous name attached.

### Beat 9
- Sylvanas reads the prisoner the way she reads ground before she commits to it. Jaina reads her back.
- Jaina's eyes keep going to the ward-stone set in the cell wall, and she frowns at it each time. Sylvanas's palm still remembers a stone like that one.
- They don't know what the mage hears in the stone, or whether she can prove any of it.
- Ground the read: Jaina's ink-stained hands flat on her knees, her boots planted, the wall stone's steady hum, rangers in the doorway waiting on the Ranger-General.
- Throwing her out and letting her stew are both worse than hearing her now.

### Beat 10
- Sylvanas shoves down her temper and takes the floor in front of the chair.
- Releasing a trespasser like this is impossible, and stalling the interrogation is riskier still. The next conversation changes everything they thought they knew about the border.
- They don't know if Jaina is telling the truth, and they definitely don't have any reason to trust her.
- Ground the standoff: rangers clearing a path for the Ranger-General, blue ward-light hitting Sylvanas's boots, the long quiet right before someone breaks it.
- Sylvanas hits her with the opening demand for chapter two: "Start talking."
- Inside: send the mage home with an escort, or hold her and wait on a magister; angry at Antonidas, and at the mage for being exactly what her file says; wants to hand it to a magister and knows she won't; decided before she reached the door, and resents it
