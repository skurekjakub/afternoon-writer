# Chapter 5: The Grain Road

**POV:** Sylvanas Windrunner (limited third, absolute)
**When:** Directly after Chapter 4, with the party riding southwest from the sick village toward the source.
**Where:** Lordaeron trade roads between village depots and the routes feeding into Andorhal.
**Travel:** Mounted on Thalassian horses.
**Characters:** Sylvanas, Jaina, Cyndia, Lyriel.
**Goal:** Work backward along the grain trail until they find the hub feeding poisoned flour across the shires.

**Sylvanas's starting position:**
- Grain is carrying the sickness.
- Someone organized the wagons and is killing loose ends.
- The trail points southwest toward Andorhal.
- Most towns are only in the early stage of the sickness, but Millhaven was hit hard.
- They stopped the grain in time at the sick village, and it lives.
- Jaina can read contamination before other people know a place is sick.
- The old carter from the Millhaven road and the survivor girl's newer grain man still feel connected, even if Sylvanas cannot prove it.

**Still in the dark about:**
- Who is really running the trade route.
- Whether the Barovs built the plague, helped cover it, or know nothing.
- Whether the old carter matters or just looks suspicious in hindsight.

**Hold back:**
- No certainty that the Barovs are guilty.
- No Mal'Ganis naming.
- No sudden speeches or confessed warmth between Sylvanas and Jaina. Their closeness stays physical, guarded, and work-first.
- No proof that the old carter is the creature behind the plague.

**End of chapter state:**
- The party is on the southern road toward Andorhal, passing straight through Arthas's military zone near Strahnbrad.
- The Barov name keeps turning up on charred seals and manifests, though their guilt remains unproven.
- The depot overseer burned a day off their clock with a forged magistrate's transfer and weighted decoy wagon tracks; they fought his cleanup crew in the depot yard at dusk while he slipped out the back gate alive.
- Sylvanas thinks "Jaina" for the first time at the midnight map stop.
- The old carter comes back to Sylvanas as a nagging memory, not yet a pattern.

## Character Arcs

### Sylvanas
- She starts the ride treating the trail like familiar road work: read the wagons, check the ledgers, ride faster than the enemy.
- A forged quarantine order and weighted decoy tracks beat her at her own craft and cost her a day—forcing her to admit the enemy is anticipating Farstrider eyes.
- Fighting back-to-back with Jaina in a smoke-choked yard and sitting still while Jaina binds her arm breaks the last of Sylvanas's habit of keeping the mage at arm's length.
- By the end, she stops thinking "the mage" and starts thinking "Jaina"—and builds the next road choice directly off Jaina's read.

### Jaina through Sylvanas's POV
- Sylvanas opens still half-expecting scholar's softness when steel comes out in tight quarters.
- Jaina matches her beat for beat in the depot yard, stops a knife with frost before Sylvanas can turn, and later binds Sylvanas's forearm with Lyriel's knot without flinching or asking permission.
- Sylvanas notices far too much over a single tallow candle—ink on Jaina's thumb, the warmth of her sleeve a breath from Sylvanas's shoulder—and tells herself it is only exhaustion and good work.

### Partnership
- They begin splitting paper and magic like two rivals forced onto one table.
- The dusk yard skirmish forces them back-to-back in a closed kill-box, trusting each other's timing without a word spoken.
- Over the midnight candle, the distance between them narrows to inches; neither woman names it, yet neither pulls away until the map is finished.

## Scene 1: The Paper Trail

**The action:** Sylvanas and Jaina turn scattered sickness into a regional supply map and realize someone is clearing the road ahead of them.
**Cast:** Sylvanas, Jaina, Cyndia, Lyriel, depot clerks, innkeepers, merchants.
**Starting point:** They know grain is the carrier and are riding southwest along the trade route.

### Beat 1
- They stop chasing rumors from inn to inn and start working the plague like freight. Ledgers, weigh-slips, tavern books, and Jaina's magical reads lock together across a scarred table.
- Millhaven's grain line matches a dozen other settlements across the northern shires, and Jaina can tell which towns are deep in fever and which only baked their first bad loaves this week.
- They do not know who directs the shipments or where the main storehouse sits.
- Ground the work: dusty account books, ink-stained fingers hovering beside Sylvanas's knife-tip, horses tied outside airless clerk offices.
- Millhaven is one notch on a supply map covering half the province, and Sylvanas circles the central depot where four wagon roads meet.

### Beat 2
- Every new tally-book makes the reach worse. Poisoned sacks are moving through ordinary Lordaeron commerce, mixed into legitimate grain loads so no village questions the cart.
- Dozens of hamlets sit on the same wagon routes, yet the central hub is still buried under layers of middlemen and waystations.
- They do not know how far south the tainted sacks already ran or which depot holds the master books.
- Ground the tracking: repeated town names underlined twice, overlapping parchment notes, Jaina's neat margins colliding with Sylvanas's rough charcoal route lines.
- They spur hard for the regional depot that should cut through the middlemen.

### Beat 3
- By late afternoon they pass a smaller roadside waystation already swept bare ahead of schedule. Someone is moving grain and scrubbing paper out in front of their horses.
- They are not studying an old crime; they are chasing live handlers who may already know four riders are coming down the pike.
- They do not know whether the regional depot will still have a single page left by morning.
- Ground the ride: damp flanks on tired horses, dark treelines closing over the road, Lyriel riding at Jaina's knee making her laugh about a clerk's crabbed handwriting while Cyndia watches the brush with an arrow across her saddle.
- The ride to the regional depot turns into a hard push against the light.

## Scene 2: The Decoy and the Yard

**The action:** Real enemy counterintelligence costs them a day, and racing back at dusk pitches them into a violent yard ambush against a cult cleanup crew.
**Cast:** Sylvanas, Jaina, Cyndia, Lyriel, depot overseer, a toll-keeper, cult cleanup crew (armed handlers and sickened dead).
**Starting point:** Someone is cleaning the trade route ahead of them, and the regional depot is their best chance to catch the trail warm.

### Beat 4
- They hit the regional depot near midday and find the storehouse stripped, swept, and guarded by an overseer who does not flinch or bluster. Calm and weary, he produces a forged Lordaeron magistrate's quarantine transfer—crisp wax, proper countersigns, grain order numbers—and gestures toward fresh, deeply cut wagon ruts rolling north on the Brill road.
- The deception is built to fool hunters, not clerks: the cult ran deliberately weighted decoy wagons out before dawn to lay genuine deep-loaded ruts north. Jaina reads no dark magic on the overseer, only real sweat and petty bureaucratic resentment. Sylvanas crouches over the ruts herself; the wheel depth matches full grain carts. With hours burning, she buys the trail and swings the party north.
- They do not know the seal is forged, that the wagons carried stones instead of sacks, or who warned the overseer to expect Farstriders.
- Ground the trap: heavy red magistrate wax unbroken on thick parchment, deep fresh wheel ruts cutting cleanly through damp mud toward Brill, the overseer's dry tone and ink-smudged cuffs.
- They ride hard north toward Brill on a trail laid specifically to draw them away.

### Beat 5
- Hours up the Brill road, the decoy cracks at the stone toll-bridge. Half a mile short of the water, the heavy ruts veer off into an abandoned gravel quarry—and the toll-keeper swears no grain wagons have crossed his bridge in a month.
- Someone built a counter-trail for Farstrider eyes, knowing how rangers read weight in mud. Sylvanas takes the hit flat in her pride, wastes no breath cursing, and wheels the horses south at a punishing gallop with the sun already dropping.
- They do not know whether the depot is already empty or still being scrubbed.
- Ground the turn: gravel churned where heavy carts dumped stone and doubled back, the toll-keeper spitting into the ditch, lather flecking the horses' shoulders as Sylvanas drives them back south into lengthening shadows.
- They race the dusk back to the depot, half a day poorer and hungry for blood.

### Beat 6
- They reach the depot at dusk and catch the tail end of a cult cleanup crew finishing the burn. Armed handlers are feeding the last ledgers into a smoking courtyard pit; a decoy wagon blocks the main archway, and as Sylvanas's arrows fly, a handler kicks the wagon's tailboard loose—spilling two freshly turned, sickened dead into the yard to cover their escape while the overseer bolts alive through the far postern gate.
- High storehouse walls choke Sylvanas's bow range. Cyndia and Lyriel pin the gate arches while Sylvanas and Jaina end up back-to-back in the smoke-filled yard—Jaina snapping frost across a lunging corpse's ankles and throwing a hasty barrier that deflects a handler's knife inches from Sylvanas's ribs; Sylvanas pivoting inside Jaina's spell-light to put an arrow through the handler's throat before his second blade clears his coat, taking a shallow slash across her forearm in the close crush.
- They do not know where the overseer bolted or whether more handlers wait outside the walls.
- Ground the skirmish: ash whirling off the burning pit, splintered wagon slats, wet dead teeth snapping inches from wool, frost crackling across cobblestones, warm blood soaking Sylvanas's left cuff as her shoulder brushes Jaina's back in the smoke.
- The yard goes quiet with the handlers down, the two dead pinned and burned, and the overseer vanished into the dusk timber.

### Beat 7
- With Cyndia and Lyriel holding the gates and the overseer gone alive into the dark, Sylvanas and Jaina rake through the smoking ash pit and pry open the decoy wagon's false bottom.
- Charred manifest corners, half-melted Barov wax seals, and shipping tallies survive the fire. Every intact route slip pinches straight toward Andorhal. The Barov seal sits on half the scraps—proof of Barov paper, not proof of Barov guilt, and Sylvanas refuses to blur that line even with blood still drying on her sleeve.
- They do not know whether the Barovs are directing the cult or being used as cover, or who tipped off the depot to expect elves.
- Ground the search: charred parchment crumbling under ash-blackened gloves, unbroken crimson Barov wax shards, blood stiffening Sylvanas's cuff, the overseer's empty chair tipped sideways by the back door.
- They carry the charred slips out of the smoking yard to find dry cover and lay out the last road to Andorhal.
- Inside: weighted wagons turned off at a quarry, built for ranger eyes; someone out here knows how Farstriders track; a cut forearm and a lost day to show for it; wants the Barovs guilty because guilty is simpler; seals on burnt paper aren't a trial, and she has to keep telling herself so

## Scene 3: The South Road

**The action:** Over one midnight candle, Sylvanas accepts Jaina as a true partner, the old carter memory snags hard, and they choose the road through Strahnbrad.
**Cast:** Sylvanas, Jaina, Cyndia, Lyriel.
**Starting point:** They have the Barov seals, Andorhal as the hub, a day lost, and blood still drying on Sylvanas's arm.

### Beat 8
- At a midnight stop in a dry loft off the road, one tallow candle lights the charred manifests and Jaina's map while Cyndia and Lyriel hold outer watch. Before touching the papers, Jaina sees the dried blood stiff on Sylvanas's cuff, pulls clean linen from her pack without asking, and cleans and binds the forearm cut—finishing it cleanly with Lyriel's one-handed knot while Sylvanas holds her arm still instead of pulling back.
- Shoulders nearly brushing over the single flame, they fuse the charred shipping tallies with Jaina's arcane map. Jaina gives hard, exact answers with zero ego and zero hesitation. Sylvanas catches the faint ink smudge on Jaina's thumb, the warmth of Jaina's sleeve inches from her own, and hears herself think "Jaina" instead of "the mage"—and leaves the name standing.
- They do not know who designed the plague or what the Barov seals truly mean.
- Ground the work: one tallow flame throwing sharp shadows, cool water stinging a cut forearm, Lyriel's knot pulled snug by ink-stained fingers, shoulders nearly touching over charred paper, quiet concentration instead of another argument.
- Every surviving route line locks onto Andorhal.
- Inside: let a human bind her arm and didn't pull away; Lyriel's knot, tied fast without looking; no handling needed, rare in a human mage and rarer in Dalaran; catches herself thinking Jaina and lets it stand; close enough to feel the heat off her sleeve, and shifts back an inch for room over the map, only for room

### Beat 9
- Once the forged transfer and weighted decoy wagons prove someone knew Farstriders were coming down this road, the old carter from Millhaven snags hard in Sylvanas's memory: the empty wagon, the polite flat voice, his eyes sliding along their horse line, asking casually after the young grain-man.
- The decoy was too tailored for coincidence, yet one strange carter on a country road is a memory, not a pattern. Sylvanas carries the snag forward without pretending she can build a case out of one face.
- They do not know why the carter won't leave her head, who he was, or how the depot knew their pace.
- Ground the memory: grain dust on old wagon slats, the echo of a courteous flat farewell, Sylvanas rubbing the fresh bandage on her forearm while staring past the candle.
- She folds the carter back into the corner of her mind and returns to the roads they can actually ride.
- Inside: one odd old man on a road, and she has told rangers a hundred times that one is nothing; then why him; an empty wagon going home empty; the depot built a decoy for Farstrider eyes, and who told it we were coming; irritated at a memory she can't pin to the map

### Beat 10
- They skip the watched main highway and pick the southern feeder road into Andorhal—the fastest clean line, running straight through Arthas's military zone near Strahnbrad.
- Taking the southern road makes an encounter with Lordaeron's army unavoidable. Sylvanas pins the parchment at Strahnbrad with her knife and warns Jaina bluntly what the prince's soldiers will do: throw every sword at the orcs they can see and bury poisoned flour beneath the urgent fight.
- They do not know whether Arthas will listen, help, or stand in their way.
- Ground the choice: steel pinning parchment at Strahnbrad, Jaina's eyes lifting sharply from the blade to Sylvanas's face, cold pre-dawn air as horses turn south toward distant camp smoke.
- Chapter 6 opens on the southern feeder road approaching Strahnbrad.
