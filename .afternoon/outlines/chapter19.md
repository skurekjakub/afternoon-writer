# Chapter 19: The Voyage

**POV:** Jaina Proudmoore (limited third, absolute)
**When:** Directly follows Chapter 18. Opens at a northern Lordaeron harbor after the ride out of the interior and closes with landfall at Daggercap Bay in Northrend.
**Where:** Northern Lordaeron harbor quay, the North Sea aboard the *Wolfheart*, and Daggercap Bay.
**Travel:** Horseback arrival, harbor bargaining on foot, schooner crossing aboard the *Wolfheart*, then dockside landfall.
**Characters:** Jaina, Sylvanas, Cyndia, three Farstrider rangers from the Chapter 18 escort, Captain Dagny Ironhull, and the *Wolfheart* crew.
**Goal:** Secure passage north and force the crossing across open water after both institutions have fallen away.

## Character Arcs

### Jaina
- She opens believing that if she can keep north in reach, being right can still matter.
- The crossing turns that belief into a physical bill paid in blood, exhaustion, and a widened channel she cannot close.
- At the mainmast she ends the withholding she kept since Chapter 14 and hands Sylvanas the exact three truths she paid Kel'Thuzad.
- By the end, she steps onto Northrend knowing the chase is no longer only on the map; it is inside her body.

### Sylvanas through Jaina's POV
- She is commander, counterweight, and the only person aboard whose voice stops Jaina before she burns herself through.
- Jaina reads Sylvanas's fierce, unspoken care through the single command, the warm waterskin from inside her cloak, the thumb wiping salt and blood from Jaina's chin, and the steadiness of her staying in the dark cabin.
- Keep Sylvanas exterior. Everything lands through timing, proximity, and the fact that she reads Jaina's limit before Jaina will admit it.

### Partnership
- The pair opens stripped of institutions and reduced to hired passage and shared direction.
- Open water forces stillness—the emotional centerpiece of their slow burn—where knee-to-knee honesty at the mast and quiet, physical care after the wind-burn deepen a devotion neither woman puts into speeches.
- Sylvanas hears the three Kel'Thuzad admissions from Jaina before anyone else can weaponize them. Her answer is practical, steady, and unchanged.
- Do not name the feeling ahead of the action.

## Scene 1: The Harbor

**The action:** They buy passage north, drop their last landbound ties at the quay, and watch the Eastern Kingdoms vanish behind the stern.
**Cast:** Jaina, Sylvanas, Cyndia, three Farstrider rangers, Dagny, harbor hands, Farstrider contact, *Wolfheart* crew.
**Starting point:** North is chosen, the relay network ends at the coast, and the chase dies unless they get a ship.

### Beat 1
- Northrend is a foul run in good weather, and this party brings no royal seals or tower warrants—only coin, urgency, and risk. Captain Dagny Ironhull agrees to take them aboard the hard-water schooner *Wolfheart*, raising the price instead of refusing while sizing up the weather and Jaina together.
- The *Wolfheart* already works northern trade waters, Dagny bargains like a woman pricing danger rather than glory, and this quay is the last ground where Silvermoon or Dalaran infrastructure can still touch them.
- They do not know whether the sea will let them across or whether Dagny's weather clause will turn into a literal fight.
- Ground the harbor: salt wind and gull noise over tarred rope, horses steaming at the dock after the hard northbound ride, Dagny's weathered hand counting coin while her eyes flick between the northern clouds and Jaina's face.
- Dagny takes their gold and orders the gangplank kept down for boarding.

### Beat 2
- Boarding is quick and practical; leaving land is not. Horses and saddles stay at the quay. Cyndia passes a warm brass relay cylinder with harbor and destination notes to the last usable Farstrider contact. Jaina checks her dead sending-stone twice anyway—Antonidas's habit of testing a result twice, turned on his own silence—gets nothing, and keeps the cold crystal in her coat pocket. The *Wolfheart* clears the breakwater, and the Eastern Kingdoms shrink from cliff line to gray strip to nothing as the northward signal sharpens cleanly over empty water.
- Every institutional support ends at the dock, Sylvanas and Cyndia fold into deck watches as smoothly as road marches, and Jaina cannot bring herself to throw away the dead stone even though she cut it herself.
- They do not know whether any message left on shore can matter before Arthas acts, or what waits out on open water.
- Ground the departure: wet deck planks under boots, saddles left stacked on the quay, the dead stone heavy in Jaina's pocket, harbor bells fading behind the stern until only rigging strain and sea-cold remain.
- The coastline disappears behind the wake, leaving the pursuit alone at sea.
- Inside: cut the line herself and keeps checking it anyway; Antonidas taught her to test a result twice, and she is using his habit on his silence; no correction coming, which is what she wanted, and it is awful; keeps the stone, and has no good reason, and keeps it

## Scene 2: The Crossing

**The action:** The source fights back through unnatural headwinds, open water forces an overdue confession knee-to-knee at the mast, and Sylvanas pulls Jaina back from burning herself out.
**Cast:** Jaina, Sylvanas, Cyndia, Farstrider rangers, Dagny, *Wolfheart* crew.
**Starting point:** They are committed to open water, northbound, with no harbor behind them.

### Beat 3
- The headwinds are not weather. They carry the same frequency family as the plague—pushing straight against the bow with deliberate force—and Jaina realizes her own probing northward is what lit them up to the source.
- The wind behaves like a hand held flat against the sails, Jaina's sensitivity runs both ways so every probe announced them, and when Dagny hears the truth she does not turn back; she recalculates what Jaina's body can buy against the gale.
- They do not know whether Jaina can force the schooner through without wrecking herself, or whether the opposition has a ceiling.
- Ground the resistance: a gale slamming the canvas like a shut door, shroud lines thrumming with no natural lull, Jaina reaching into the wind and feeling something cold press back against her mind.
- The crossing turns from a sail into a direct contest against whatever waits in the north.

### Beat 4
- The *Wolfheart* can only claw north in bursts, leaving forced quiet hours the road never gave them. Sitting knee-to-knee against the cold mainmast in the spray, out of earshot of the crew, they talk about what they walked away from. Sylvanas says she stopped reading the Convocation's letters weeks before the recall arrived. Jaina answers with what she has held back since Scholomance: all three truths she paid Kel'Thuzad—about Antonidas, about Arthas, and about enjoying keeping up at his table—spoken aloud in the exact words she gave him in Chapter 12, and that he wrote in his book after each one.
- Sylvanas does not flinch, does not offer pity, and asks only one practical question: is that book in Jaina's bag? It is, unopened, beside the other journals. Sylvanas nods once and stays sitting right where she is, knees nearly touching Jaina's on the wet deck. Then the harder comparison surfaces between them: Arthas broke his father's orders too, and he was certain.
- They do not know whether their certainty differs from Arthas's in kind or only in result, or what sits between them now that every secret and every banner has been set down on the deck.
- Ground the confession: cold mainmast timber against Jaina's spine, wet rope coiled under Sylvanas across a foot of deck, salt spray beading on Sylvanas's brow while she listens without moving away, no witnesses anywhere near the bow.
- Jaina sets the last Scholomance secret between them, and Sylvanas stays anchored across from her without shifting an inch.
- Inside: said all three, his room's words in the open air, and Sylvanas asked about the book and nothing else; knees inches from hers in the spray and Sylvanas never pulled back; Arthas was sure too, so what is the difference, results, timing, who is watching; wants a difference in kind and can only find one in degree; strangely steady here, with Stratholme's smoke barely out of her clothes

### Beat 5
- To claw back the lost days, Jaina matches the headwinds by turning herself into the schooner's second engine. The *Wolfheart* surges forward, but the bill comes straight out of Jaina's body: blood from her nose drying across the back of her hand, reserves scraping bottom, and the bread-taste locking in permanently instead of fading between casts.
- The run becomes raw endurance between Jaina's output and the northern resistance, and holding the channel open that wide lets Jaina feel not just a single distant point but a vast, cold structure answering back across the sea.
- They do not know whether she can recover before landfall, or whether the widened channel can ever close again.
- Ground the push: dark blood drying across Jaina's knuckles while her hands stay lifted into the wind, hull planks shuddering between two opposed gales, Dagny watching her like a captain watching an engine run past red.
- Every mile north is paid out of Jaina's blood and breath.

### Beat 6
- Jaina will not stop while her sums say the ship can still make knots. Sylvanas crosses the pitching deck and decides for her: one word, flat and absolute, cutting straight through the roar. Jaina drops her hands before she even decides to obey—stopped by Sylvanas in a single syllable when Antonidas never could stop her like that. Sylvanas eases her down out of the wind, pulls a leather waterskin warm from inside her cloak, tips it to Jaina's mouth, and wipes dried salt and blood off Jaina's chin without a speech or an apology.
- Jaina cannot set her own limit once the goal is in reach; Sylvanas's authority reaches the bone, and the fierce, quiet physical care lands harder than any comfort spoken aloud. Beneath the cut spell, ordinary sea-wind returns as if weather had been waiting under the fight all along.
- They do not know whether the widened channel will shrink once she rests, or what holding it open that long has left inside her.
- Ground the stop: one word snapping across a loud deck, Jaina's knees folding onto a coil of line, the waterskin warm from Sylvanas's ribs against Jaina's cold lips, Sylvanas's hand steady at Jaina's chin wiping salt and blood away while neither woman looks elsewhere.
- Jaina lets go of the wind and takes the water from Sylvanas's hand.
- Inside: the sums said she could keep going, and the sums were hers; one word, and she obeyed before she decided to; furious, and grateful, and drinking anyway while Sylvanas's thumb clears the blood from her chin; Antonidas never could stop her like that, and she will not ask herself why Sylvanas can

## Scene 3: The Approach

**The action:** One unanswered question hangs in the dark cabin, Northrend's ice closes around the hull, and Daggercap Bay proves Arthas is already inland.
**Cast:** Jaina, Sylvanas, Cyndia, Farstrider rangers, Dagny, *Wolfheart* crew.
**Starting point:** The wind-push bought them the crossing, and Jaina is visibly spent.

### Beat 7
- In the dark cabin below decks, Sylvanas stays in the narrow bunk across from Jaina instead of going back up to the watch. Into the dark between them, Jaina says, `"Kel'Thuzad was right about parts of it. So is Arthas."` Sylvanas, awake in the opposite bunk, does not argue or soothe her—she tells Jaina to sleep, because Dagny wants her on the ice watch at first light.
- Nobody answers the sentence. The ice watch is real: floes begin at dawn and Dagny needs Jaina's eyes and sense on the bow. Jaina lies awake listening to the hull creak and Sylvanas's steady breathing a few feet away in the dark.
- They do not know whether the question has any clean answer, or what it will demand the next time Jaina has to decide fast.
- Ground the night: narrow wooden bunks rocking inches apart in a dark cabin, bread-taste and salt air sharing the close space, cold northern stars sliding past the deadlight, Sylvanas's voice low and unhurried across the dark.
- The sentence stays hanging unanswered in the dark cabin as the ship drives north.
- Inside: right about parts of it, so which parts, and who sorts them; she would have said method, not certainty, a month ago; Sylvanas said sleep, an answer of a kind, or a mercy, or just staying in the room with her; lies awake ranking her certainty against theirs and dislikes the ranking

### Beat 8
- By dawn the unnatural headwinds die—not from mercy, but because the *Wolfheart* has entered ice floes and ambient cold that belong to the coast itself. Jaina stands on the bow and no longer has to reach north for the signal; this close, it reaches straight into her.
- Northrend's chill presses like a physical weight rather than ordinary weather, the source hums through everything at this range, and the plague echo inside Jaina locks onto it harder with every cable-length toward shore.
- They do not know how much of this heightened pull fades if they ever leave Northrend, or whether the harbor ahead holds a trail or a dead end.
- Ground the approach: iron-tipped boat hooks ringing off blue-white floes, rime webbing the shrouds at dawn, cold shifting from wind-bite on skin to a steady ache behind Jaina's eyes.
- The *Wolfheart* noses into Northrend's ice with the source pressing directly against Jaina's skull.

### Beat 9
- They make landfall at Daggercap Bay, and the harbor strips away any hope of arriving first. Expanded dwarven trade docks, bootprints, empty camp traces, and charred ship ribs frozen into the harbor ice show days of military landing and departure. Arthas is alive, inland, and still days ahead.
- The headwinds stop entirely once they cross into the bay, the wrecks prove Arthas landed in force and moved fast, and sea-work ends in frozen mud and inland tracks.
- They do not know whether anyone local survived inland to tell them what Arthas found, or how far past the harbor the signal sits.
- Ground the landfall: frozen mud crunching under boots after four days of pitching planks, fresh timber nailed onto dwarven docks, blackened ship ribs sticking through bay ice, the resonance pressing from every direction at once.
- They step off the *Wolfheart* onto frozen ground with Arthas's tracks already leading inland.
