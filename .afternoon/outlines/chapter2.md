# Chapter 2: The Mage

**POV:** Jaina Proudmoore (limited third, absolute)
**When:** Opens on the exact beat where Chapter 1 ends.
**Where:** Farstrider border post at Thalassian Pass.
**Travel:** On foot inside the ward circle at open; southbound on Thalassian horses once Sylvanas lets her out.
**Characters:** Jaina, Sylvanas, Cyndia, Lyriel, Neral, one unnamed escort ranger.
**Goal:** Get Sylvanas to act on the contamination before more time is lost.

**Jaina's starting position:**
- The leylines south of the border are contaminated by something necromantic.
- She can identify the signature as broadly death-aligned even if the banned discipline itself sits outside her formal training.
- The contamination is spreading.
- She crossed into Quel'Thalas while tracking the corruption.
- The Kirin Tor did not authorize the trip, and Antonidas does not know exactly where she is.

**Still in the dark about:**
- Whether Sylvanas will listen.
- Whether Sylvanas already knows who she is.
- Whether the elves will release her or keep her prisoner.
- What the contamination is doing to the physical ground beyond the leylines.
- The exact process that turns living contamination into post-death behavior.

**Hold back:**
- No confirmation that grain is the carrier.
- No organized undead column.
- No immediate trust between Jaina and Sylvanas.

**End of chapter state:**
- Sylvanas is convinced enough to release Jaina and ride south with her to investigate.
- The contamination is physical, lethal, and large enough to threaten the whole region, not just the leylines.
- Sylvanas, Jaina, Cyndia, Lyriel, Neral, and one unnamed escort ranger form the riding party heading toward Millhaven.

## Character Arcs

### Jaina
- She opens convinced that if she can explain the magic clearly enough, any sensible person will act.
- She starts out treating Sylvanas as another soldier standing in her way.
- Because she assumes soldiers need the short version, she talks smaller than she really thinks.
- By the end, Jaina still hates being under guard, but she stops treating Sylvanas like a blunt instrument and starts noticing how fast her mind moves—and how steady her hands stay when the road turns ugly.

### Sylvanas through Jaina's POV
- Jaina first reads Sylvanas as a cold military obstruction: dangerous, quiet, and far too still in a cramped room.
- That read cracks when Sylvanas listens hard, cuts straight to the junction on the map, and handles a sudden road ambush with zero wasted motion.
- Once Sylvanas proves she can keep up, Jaina drops the watered-down explanation—and catches herself noticing the precision in Sylvanas's voice and hands long after the argument ends.

### Partnership
- They start as prisoner and custodian. If Jaina gets out, it will be on a leash.
- As the chapter moves, the custody escort turns into a tense, sharp-edged hunt.
- There is still no trust and no warmth—only charged friction and the first grudging recognition that each can keep up with the other.

## Scene 1: The Interrogation

**The action:** Jaina has to talk her way out of custody and into an escorted ride south.
**Cast:** Jaina, Sylvanas, Cyndia, Lyriel, Neral, escort detail.
**Starting point:** Jaina knows the contamination is real. Sylvanas only knows she has a high-profile trespasser inside the wards.

### Beat 1
- Sylvanas orders Jaina to speak. It is a demand, not an invitation, delivered so quietly that the rangers at the door fall completely still.
- Jaina finally has someone whose attention does not glaze over. She is acutely aware of Sylvanas's stillness—hands steady at her belt, eyes cutting straight past courtesy—so Jaina drops diplomacy and opens with the danger.
- She doesn't know if Sylvanas will believe a word of it.
- Ground the room: the heavy press of the wards on Jaina's magic, Sylvanas's shadow across the containment ring, the quiet cut of Sylvanas's voice against the rough edge in Jaina's tired throat.
- Jaina throws the warning down as hard as she can.

### Beat 2
- Jaina explains the corrupted leylines, but makes the mistake of trimming the explanation down for a soldier.
- She explains she tracked a broadly necromantic taint until the Thalassian ward-stones carried the same damage, and crossed the border to confirm it. Her map and tools are confiscated. Among them is a paired sending stone to Antonidas. She has kept it silent so he cannot order her home.
- She has no idea if Sylvanas hears a regional threat or just an overreaching mage's theory.
- Ground the explanation: Jaina's hands tracing currents in the air without thinking, frost burns raw on her knuckles, Sylvanas's eyes tracking her hands once before snapping back to her face, Cyndia listening motionless by the door.
- Sylvanas ignores the trimmed-down theory and zeroes in on the weak practical point: why Jaina came alone.

### Beat 3
- Forced into the worst part of the story, Jaina admits the Kirin Tor didn't send her and Antonidas only has a note she left behind.
- No credentials, no backing, no delegation. Jaina's isolation makes her look both reckless and honest. Nobody in Dalaran acted on her warning.
- She doesn't know if Sylvanas reads this isolation as proof of obsession or proof of a real emergency.
- Ground the silence: ward-stones humming in the gap after the admission, Jaina holding her chin up so embarrassment doesn't show in her voice.
- Sylvanas goes quiet, weighing whether to lock the door or take the mage out onto the road.
- Inside: the note on Antonidas's desk, and whether he has read it yet; the silent sending stone, which she calls caution and is not; would she believe herself, from the other side of the circle; probably not, and she would still be right

### Beat 4
- Jaina realizes argument will not be enough. If she wants out of the circle, she has to prove the danger on the road.
- Sylvanas steps right up to the ward edge—close enough that Jaina feels the cold draft off her cloak—and asks which stones read worst and where. Her steady hand rests inches from the hum without flinching, eyes locked on Jaina's mouth as she waits for the exact names.
- They don't know if the road will prove Jaina right or make her look foolish. Jaina does not know Sylvanas already laid her own palm on one of those stones that morning.
- Ground the decision: Sylvanas's hand steady by the ward border, her gaze shifting from Jaina's face toward the southern pass door as she counts riders in her head.
- Sylvanas offers a conditional release.

### Beat 5
- The terms are blunt: armed escort, watched hands, no wandering. Jaina accepts instantly because time matters more than pride.
- Jaina openly admits she needs them to stay alive on the road. Sylvanas hears the practical truth in that and drops the last of her court-spy suspicion, though her tone stays razor-flat.
- Jaina doesn't know if the escort sees her as a guide or just a prisoner with temporary walking privileges.
- Ground the release: the barrier dropping when Cyndia pulls the anchor stone, raw leyline noise flooding back into Jaina's awareness, her legs stiff from hours on the warded floor.
- The moment the ward falls, Jaina feels how much worse the contamination has grown since dawn.

### Beat 6
- Jaina gets her staff back, with the silent sending stone returned alongside it. She checks the resonance and realizes the corruption has worsened sharply in eight hours.
- Cyndia, Lyriel, and Neral join the mounted detail. Neral is positioned as the relay rider if they need to send word back fast. The group mounts up immediately.
- They do not know what awaits them once they clear the Pass.
- Ground the departure: the familiar, heavy resonance when the staff snaps back into Jaina's grip, horses brought up to the post, escort riders falling smoothly into place around Sylvanas.
- The party leaves the Pass and crosses into Lordaeron.

## Scene 2: The Road to Millhaven

**The action:** The warning leaves the map and starts showing itself in the soil, crops, and air.
**Cast:** Jaina, Sylvanas, Cyndia, Lyriel, Neral, one unnamed escort ranger.
**Starting point:** The contamination is urgent, but they still don't know how it enters the physical world.

### Beat 7
- The moment they leave the dampening ward network, the corrupted leyline signal hits Jaina at full strength.
- The elven wards had been muting the true scale of the rot. Out here, the pressure is far worse than Jaina could prove inside the post. The elves read the change in their horses' ears and shortened strides once they cross off home ground.
- They still don't know if the source is a place, an object, or an ongoing event.
- Ground the ride: the clean cutoff of the ward-hum behind them, raw arcane pressure hitting Jaina behind the eyes, horses tightening their gaits under the rangers.
- Jaina reins in to lay her bare hand directly on the road.

### Beat 8
- She dismounts and presses her bare palm to the dirt, sinking so deep into the spell that the rangers have to fan out and cover her while she kneels in the open.
- The wrongness has seeped out of the leylines into the soil, the roots, and the groundwater. When Jaina stays down a beat too long, Sylvanas swings off her horse, catches Jaina by the upper arm with a steady, unhurried grip, and hauls her upright before a shying mount crowds her—letting go a beat faster than necessary while warning her with one sharp look.
- They don't know why some stretches of road feel heavier than others.
- Ground the test: packed dirt under Jaina's palm, a metallic, sick taste in her mouth, Sylvanas's gloved hand steady and warm through wool on Jaina's sleeve, Lyriel and Neral watching opposite angles while Cyndia scans the treeline.
- Jaina remounts with the print of that grip still warm on her arm, then immediately misreads her audience again.

### Beat 9
- She slips into a careful, simplified explanation. Sylvanas cuts her off cleanly, leans from her saddle to tap the parchment map with one steady gloved finger, and names Millhaven as the exact throat where the tainted lines pinch together.
- Jaina realizes she has been talking down to the wrong woman; her picture of a blunt soldier breaks on the spot. Lyriel, riding at Jaina's right, leans in and tells her quietly that Sylvanas does that to everyone. She is the first ranger to talk to Jaina as anything other than a prisoner.
- They don't know if Millhaven is the source or just the place taking the worst of the hit.
- Ground the correction: Jaina stopping mid-gesture, Sylvanas's crisp voice cutting through tack-creak, Lyriel's dry aside from the right flank, the sudden heat in Jaina's face that has nothing to do with the wind.
- Jaina drops the watered-down talk just as the farms ahead stop looking alive.
- Inside: talked down to her the whole road; embarrassed, then annoyed at being embarrassed, then sharper-awake than she has been since Dalaran; who else has she simplified for without noticing; the small, dangerous pleasure of someone keeping up, filed under useful and left there

### Beat 10
- Before the village roofs clear the trees, the land itself looks spoiled.
- Farms sit empty, chimneys stand cold, and crops are blackening in the rows. The rot is hitting kitchens, fields, and hearths, not just ward-stones and currents.
- They have no idea what happened to the people who lived here.
- Ground the approach: cold chimneys dark against the pale sky, sickly rot in the fields, the crystal on Jaina's staff humming hard without command.
- Brush cracks loud on both flanks before the first dark roofline comes clear.

## Scene 3: The Wildlife Attack

**The action:** The plague turns physical and violent, and Jaina sees how Sylvanas handles a real fight.
**Cast:** Jaina, Sylvanas, Cyndia, Lyriel, Neral, escort.
**Starting point:** The land is contaminated, but the living cost hasn't shown its teeth yet.

### Beat 11
- Sick, wild-eyed deer burst from the brush and wolves slam the horses from the flank. The ambush hits so fast Jaina throws frost on pure instinct.
- Sylvanas reads the whole clearing in one sweep and calls targets in half-sentences—string drawn to her cheekbone, release without a wasted twitch, steady hands turning chaos into clean ground. Cyndia, Lyriel, and Neral seal the flanks effortlessly. One stag keeps lunging after an arrow takes it through the chest.
- They do not know if this is one cursed patch of woods or the new rule for the whole valley.
- Ground the clash: filmed white eyes, wolves crashing in from the verge, antlers scoring dirt, Sylvanas's clear flat voice cutting over screaming horses, the heavy thud of Cyndia's killing shot.
- Once the brush goes quiet, Jaina dismounts to open the carcass and see what the spell did to actual flesh.

### Beat 12
- Jaina forces herself to inspect the ruined stag to see whether this is isolated or part of the same spreading rot.
- The necromantic taint sits inside the blood, muscle, and gut—working inside living flesh before death, not waiting for the heart to stop. Sylvanas holds the company completely still, bow half-drawn over Jaina's back, waiting for Jaina's read before choosing the next move.
- They still don't know how the sickness first entered the animals.
- Ground the autopsy: wrong heat inside dead flesh, Jaina's bare hand smeared red on the hide, Sylvanas's boots planted two paces away keeping the treeline covered, horses shying from the smell of spoiled blood.
- They remount and push hard toward the village where the wrongness knots tightest.
- Inside: active before death, which is the interesting part, and she hates that it is; hand in a dead deer and already drafting the next test; who ate from these fields, and how long ago; puts the sick feeling off until after the read, as if it will wait

### Beat 13
- They close the last stretch to Millhaven. The village already looks as dead as the stag in the ditch.
- Millhaven's hearths are cold and its lanes sit empty. The arcane wrongness pulls tightest right behind its first fence line.
- They have no idea if a single living person is left inside.
- Ground the arrival: no woodsmoke on the breeze, dark roofs against the fading light, the road narrowing into an unnatural hush as Sylvanas halts the line at the first cold yard.
- Chapter 3 opens at the village edge with the whole party poised to sweep Millhaven.
