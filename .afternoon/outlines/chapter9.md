# Chapter 9: The Trap

**POV:** Sylvanas Windrunner (limited third, absolute)
**When:** After hard days of Eastweald patrols following Andorhal. The hunt is still live, but the best traces are already cooling.
**Where:** Cult-held Eastweald east of Andorhal, on the approach toward Barov country.
**Travel:** Mounted on Thalassian horses into the Eastweald, then on foot through orchards, outposts, and the outskirts.
**Characters:** Sylvanas, Jaina, Cyndia, Lyriel.
**Goal:** Hit a live Eastweald cult site before the trail cools, pull hard proof pointing above the local cells, and turn the Andorhal victory into a path toward Kel'Thuzad's seat.

**Sylvanas's starting position:**
- Grain is the poison, Andorhal was the heart of the plague, and treated grain is already moving through northern Lordaeron.
- Kel'Thuzad has been named as the mastermind somewhere east of Andorhal.
- The Cult of the Damned is a hidden organization with living members, mage support, and discipline.
- Barov holdings keep turning up in the records, but the name is still a paper trail, not proof.
- Jaina's magical read keeps them ahead of most of the crown's awareness, though still behind the spread itself.
- Millhaven was the first village lost, and the eastward spread has mostly hidden itself inside ordinary sickness or quiet occupation.
- The old carter, the newer grain-man, and the repeated-farewell clue are still unresolved.
- The four women now move like one hunting pack.

**Still in the dark about:**
- Kel'Thuzad's exact lair.
- Whether the Barovs are patrons, hosts, or a corrupted noble house.
- Whether Farstrider and Kirin Tor leadership will keep backing the hunt once more evidence goes missing.
- Whether the repeated farewell belongs to one being, one cult circle, or coincidence.
- Which Eastweald trail actually reaches the cult's inner circle.

**Hold back:**
- No Mal'Ganis identification.
- No claim that the Barov name equals total guilt.
- No assumption that Arthas will help once reached.
- Do not flatten the old carter, the newer grain-man, or the repeated-farewell clue into a solved answer.

**End of chapter state:**
- Lyriel is dead, and the pack is permanently broken.
- Jaina leaves with the Barov papers, Cyndia's estate-approach notes, and fractured spell fragments.
- Sylvanas and Cyndia leave with a torn Hearthglen dispatch and the Arthas thread under collapsing political backing.
- The courier's Tyr's Hand lead is false. The dispatch contradicts it.
- The farewell reaches Sylvanas a third time, secondhand from Cyndia. The man who said it was a robe at a distance, walking away. There is nothing to hunt.

## Character Arcs

### Sylvanas
- She opens believing hard hunting and fast calls will crack the Eastweald before the trail goes cold.
- She trusts Jaina, yet still treats Jaina's magical sweep as backup to a ranger's eye rather than the final safety line.
- She reads a real patrol seam and moves half a beat before Jaina finishes reading the wards. That gets Lyriel killed and leaves Sylvanas bleeding.
- Twin-sin wiring (structural note; the prose never names it): this is Sylvanas's first act of being right alone. Her second is Chapter 11 B6. Jaina's matching act is Chapter 16 B8.
- By the end, she knows Jaina's ward-read is not support work—it is the difference between walking out and dying in formation—and sending Jaina alone across a lake cuts deeper than tactical risk.

### Jaina through Sylvanas's POV
- Sylvanas sees Jaina as the warning line and the only person who can turn scorched stone and ledgers into proof.
- Jaina pulls real value out of the ruined farmhouse: spell fragments and Barov transit papers that matter.
- None of that saves them once Sylvanas moves before the read is finished.
- After Lyriel's death, every exchange between them grows shorter, heavier, and harder to look away from when Jaina's hands are on Sylvanas's shoulder tying the bandage.

### Partnership
- The four women open the chapter moving almost on reflex.
- One bad call proves magical sight and physical movement are the same survival line.
- Grief stays practical: shorter sentences, Cyndia checking an empty flank, Jaina pulling Lyriel's knot tight with her teeth at Sylvanas's collarbone.
- The split hurts because neither woman can pretend anymore that the other is only a useful ally.

## Scene 1: The Approach

**The action:** The pack reaches the first live Eastweald site and tears usable proof out of a ruined farmhouse before the trail cools.
**Cast:** Sylvanas, Jaina, Cyndia, Lyriel.
**Starting point:** Eastweald is no longer rumor or cold ash. It is occupied cult country.

### Beat 1
- The pack pushes east through cult-held country toward a marked hideout. Someone has worked this land for defense: cleared verges, trimmed sight lines along the hedgerows, and dead orchards stripped of cover tell Sylvanas the cult holds this ground rather than hiding in it.
- The four women still move with easy, drilled spacing—Lyriel loose on the right flank, Cyndia reading the verges, Jaina riding close behind Sylvanas's shoulder—but surprise cannot be assumed here.
- They do not know which site ahead matters most or how closely the enemy has already studied their patrol pattern.
- Ground the approach: dead apple boughs black against gray sky, pale fungus crusting ruined furrows, a wagon road swept too clean and empty to trust.
- Jaina reins in sharply when something under the scrub pulls at her sight.

### Beat 2
- Jaina catches a warm magical scar inside a half-collapsed farmhouse. Sylvanas grants her a tight window while Cyndia and Lyriel watch the yard, and Jaina pries loose fractured spell fragments and Barov-marked transit papers before Sylvanas waves them back to cover.
- The work here is fresh, the Barov name now sits on live grain-movement papers instead of old ledgers, and the fragments confirm necromantic bindings tied to living bodies—still missing the final waking curse.
- They do not know whether the farmhouse was dropped in haste or left warm to slow them down, or whether the fragments will be enough to unlock what comes next.
- Ground the search: heat shimmer wavering over scorched hearthstones, dark sigil lines cut deep into flagstones, thick amber reagent clinging too slowly to broken glass.
- With the trail still hot, Sylvanas pushes them toward the larger compound ahead.

## Scene 2: The Trap

**The action:** Sylvanas reads a real patrol seam, moves half a beat before Jaina finishes reading the wards, and the hidden blast kills Lyriel and wounds Sylvanas.
**Cast:** Sylvanas, Jaina, Cyndia, Lyriel, cult enforcers, robed figure, cult courier.
**Starting point:** The farmhouse papers point straight to a larger compound while the trail is still live.

### Beat 3
- At the edge of the compound, Sylvanas spots a genuine seam in the cult patrol and signals the move before the gap closes. Jaina reaches out, voice low and urgent, asking for one more sweep because the earth ahead feels layered with buried wards. Sylvanas calls the crossing half a beat before Jaina finishes her sentence.
- The minutes spent at the farmhouse press at Sylvanas's spine, yet they do not excuse the call. Her read of the patrol is right—the seam is real—and moving on her half before Jaina's half is in turns discipline into a kill zone.
- They do not know how many ward layers sit buried in the mud or whether the gap leads inside or straight into a trap.
- Ground the decision: wet scrub oak hiding their crouched shoulders, Sylvanas counting sentry boots through the mist, Jaina's hand half raised and her warning cut off mid-word.
- The pack steps into the seam.
- Inside: the seam is real and closing; one more sweep, there is always one more sweep with mages; a day late since Millhaven and not again; calls it over Jaina's half-finished sentence and names that command

### Beat 4
- The buried ward line detonates on exact Farstrider spacing. The first blast takes Lyriel instantly—no shout, no second breath. Jaina throws an arcane barrier up in time to catch the second pulse as the ground splits, and Sylvanas drags Jaina and Cyndia clear through the smoke while shrapnel and cold fire rip across her shoulder and side.
- Whoever laid this ward knew ranger spacing well enough to measure the blast to it; this compound was rigged to kill disciplined scouts, not scare off militia. Given five more seconds, Jaina would have read the trigger.
- They do not know who engineered the ward or how deep the trap belt runs around the compound.
- Ground the hit: Lyriel gone from the right flank in a white-violet flash, ozone and sweet rot choking the air, hot blood soaking fast through Sylvanas's leathers, Jaina's barrier cracking like ice under the second shock.
- They break contact dragging grief, blood, and proof out of the smoke.

### Beat 5
- As cult enforcers scatter from the blown perimeter, one robed figure at the far tree line does not run. He stands still in the drifting ward smoke and watches the scorched earth where the blast took Lyriel. A courier bolts from his side clutching a leather dispatch case. On the flank, Cyndia is close enough to hear the figure send the runner off with `"May the stars keep your road."` Then the robed man turns unhurriedly and walks away into the scrub.
- Bleeding and hauling survivors clear, Sylvanas sees only a dark robe standing still at long bowshot. On a first glance he looks like one more cultist watching a botched ambush, and Cyndia breaks after the running courier as the target carrying real orders.
- They do not know who the robed man is or why a cultist would give a runner a road blessing.
- Ground the figure: one dark robe motionless while every other cultist runs, pale ward smoke drifting across his boots, the courier's heels hammering toward the orchard terrace.
- Second read (plant only; the prose must not explain it): he is checking that the trap did its work, and the courier carries his order. The ward was laid to the riders' spacing he watched on the Millhaven road and the Andorhal road. The rider who answered him at Millhaven is the one it killed.
- Cyndia sprints after the courier.

### Beat 6
- They pull back hard with everything they can carry. Jaina clutches the spell fragments and Barov papers against her coat. Cyndia runs the courier down on a crumbling orchard terrace and gets a fist on his dispatch case. Pinned against the low wall, he talks fast: the orders run south to Tyr's Hand, where the master waits. He wrenches loose, the case rips in Cyndia's grip, and he pitches backward over the terrace wall to his death on the stones below. Cyndia rejoins them breathless with the torn half of the dispatch and the farewell she heard the robed figure speak. Sylvanas hears it secondhand, bleeding, with Lyriel still back in the smoke, and the phrase stabs her memory without yielding an answer.
- The torn dispatch ties cult watchers straight to Hearthglen and Arthas's troop movement, exposing the Tyr's Hand lead as a lie. Sylvanas's shoulder wound is deep enough to slow a long draw, and Cyndia already snaps her eyes toward Lyriel's empty flank before catching herself.
- They do not know whether Hearthglen is target, bait, or battleground, or whether the repeated farewell belongs to one being, one cult circle, or coincidence.
- Ground the retreat: a blood-soaked cloth knotted tight on the run, broken terrace stone below the orchard, Cyndia checking an empty right flank twice, the torn dispatch rattling dryly in Jaina's stained fingers.
- Second read (plant only; the prose must not explain it): the courier was sent out with the Tyr's Hand story ready in case he was caught. The lie is the robed figure's.
- The surviving proof splits clean into two roads.

## Scene 3: The Split

**The action:** The evidence divides into two urgent tracks, outside backing shrinks to nothing, and Lyriel's death forces Sylvanas and Jaina apart.
**Cast:** Sylvanas, Jaina, Cyndia, Lor'themar's runner, Antonidas via sending stone.
**Starting point:** Lyriel is dead. The survivors hold spell fragments, Barov papers, one torn Hearthglen dispatch, and no room left for another bad call.

### Beat 7
- On an exposed stone ridge, Sylvanas presses Cyndia for a face on the robed man. Cyndia has nothing but a dark robe at a distance walking into brush—nothing to hunt. Then the proofs sort themselves without mercy: the Barov papers and spell fragments belong to Jaina's line, and the Hearthglen dispatch belongs to Sylvanas's. Lor'themar's runner arrives with reports of scrubbed cult sites and Silvermoon's patience snapping, while Antonidas's voice through Jaina's sending stone turns Kirin Tor doubt into formal warning.
- The runner also brings word of Millhaven's dead: the shambling column reached the outer runestones at the Thalassian border and burned on the wards. Silvermoon takes the ash as proof the borders hold and calls the plague Lordaeron's trouble.
- Barov ties look heavier yet still lack proof of guilt; Hearthglen is now a live military flashpoint; and institutional backing is closing behind both women.
- They do not know whether Arthas will hear the plague as a disaster to stop or only as a target to charge, or whether Jaina can crack the Barov house before Dalaran pulls her back.
- Ground the pressure: sharp wind scraping bare ridge rock, Lor'themar's cramped ink on folded parchment, Antonidas's cool crackle from the stone, Lyriel's missing voice hollowing out every pause.
- One party cannot ride both trails.
- Inside: she put Lyriel on that flank; the seam was real, she was right, and Lyriel is dead anyway; Jaina said wait, and she would rather not remember the exact word; Silvermoon reads burned dead as proof the wards hold, of course it does; wants to hit someone on the Convocation and settles for the map

### Beat 8
- The split cannot be avoided. Jaina must take the Barov road alone because only she can read the fragments, push the transit papers, and talk her way past noble doors. Sylvanas and Cyndia must ride south for Hearthglen and Arthas because only an army is big enough to answer what is coming.
- Jaina packs the Barov papers, Cyndia's estate-approach notes, and the spell fragments. Sylvanas keeps Lor'themar's runner off the home relay and posts him on the Darrowmere shore as a lake watch on Jaina's line: if Jaina crosses to Caer Darrow and does not come back, he rides south until he finds Sylvanas.
- Before they mount, Jaina steps close to re-dress Sylvanas's shoulder wound. She pulls the linen snug and ties it off one-handed with her teeth using the Farstrider knot Lyriel taught her back in the sick village. For one breath-close heartbeat neither moves—Jaina's warm breath against Sylvanas's collarbone, her fingers resting a fraction too long against the unlaced leather at Sylvanas's shoulder, Sylvanas held rigidly still so she does not lean into the touch. Sylvanas drops her eyes to the knot instead of Jaina's face, says `"It will hold,"` and steps back toward her horse.
- They do not know which trail will reach real power first, or whether splitting now saves the hunt or breaks the only thing keeping them alive.
- Ground the split: clean linen pulled tight between Jaina's teeth, warm breath ghosting over Sylvanas's collarbone in the cold ridge wind, Jaina's ink-stained knuckles pausing against blood-dark leather before dropping away, Cyndia checking the empty right flank even while holding the reins.
- Chapter 10 and Chapter 11 break apart from this ridge.
- Inside: sending her across a lake alone, a day after moving too fast got one of her own killed; posts the runner on the shore and calls it procedure; Jaina's breath warm against her collarbone and every nerve in her shoulder going still; would go with her, and can't, and won't let her see how close she is to saying stay; Lyriel's knot in Jaina's teeth; says it will hold, and steps back before she reaches for her wrist
