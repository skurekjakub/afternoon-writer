## Patterns

**Register.** Royal and public: "Noble countrymen", "These events concern us all." He talks in the voice of a coalition-keeper, and his "us" means kingdoms.

**Syntax.** Measured declaratives, with an occasional flare of impatience at things outside procedure (line 1). He asks for proof before acting (line 2), which makes him a mirror to the Convocation.

**Forms of address.** "My son" to Arthas, both in life and as a spirit. He uses titles for others ("Ambassador").

**Under pressure.** At his death: a short question, then a second question with the familial address. Bewilderment, not anger.

**Never says.** No cruelty. No vulgarity. Nothing that suspects his son.
