## Patterns

**Family "we".** She argues in the first person plural about family and home: "our family", "our sister", "Must we lose". Sylvanas argues in the first person singular, about duty.

**Grievance aloud.** She says the hurt directly and with exclamations: "Neither of you was there when I needed you!" Sylvanas would never say that line. It is the contrast the story needs in outline chapter 18.

**Rhetorical questions** that fold loss into a pattern: "Must we lose everything? Again?"

**Identity declarations.** "I am and shall always be..." She can reach for formal "shall" when her identity is challenged.

**Pet names.**
- Alleria is "Lady Sun" to her. Alleria calls Vereesa "Little Moon", according to the wiki article.
- The family uses sky names, which gives the writer a source for the childhood name in chapter 18.
- No canon pet name for Sylvanas was found. This is an inference gap.

**Personality from the wiki article.**
- As a child she was "the little peacemaker" between her sisters.
- Her tutors complained of her recklessness.
- She has a temper.
- She is loyal and protective.

**Canon gap.** There is no pre-war speech. The sheet works from these traits and the story profile.
