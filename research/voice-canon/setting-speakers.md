# Setting speakers: canon lines for the original cast

The Plague Road's original characters (Cyndia, Lyriel, Neral, Corin, Havel, Dagny, Brask, Marsh, Thornton, Ashcombe, Eressea, Maric) have no canon dialogue. These lines come from canon speakers of the same kind (Farstriders, Lordaeron soldiers and townsfolk, a magistrate, Kul Tiran guards), so the writer can study how these people sound. They are register evidence only. Never put them in an original character's mouth.

**How these were gathered.** WebFetch summaries of Warcraft Wiki (warcraft.wiki.gg) pages, fetched 2026-10-12. The summaries were asked for verbatim text, but a summarising model produced them, so check exact wording against the wiki page before quoting anything in print. Wowpedia (fandom) returned HTTP 402 and could not be read.

## Patterns to carry into the sheets

- **Lordaeron soldiers** use short service phrases ("Aye, my lord", "Say the word") and grumble dryly about pay and danger. They have one war cry, and it names the king or the kingdom. The Light is the human oath.
- **Townsfolk** describe the plague by what they see ("Everyone I know..."), guess at causes ("something in the water"), and use no specialist words at all.
- **High elves** are courteous with a built-in edge, call humans "human", and are sure of their own competence. Farstriders state their duty plainly ("sworn to defend").
- **Magistrates** speak formally and procedurally, name their court and its authority, and dislike wasted time.
- **Kul Tiran** canon speech is later-era dialect ("ye", "yer", "avast"). For a Third War merchant captain, keep the trade bluntness and drop the pirate colour.
