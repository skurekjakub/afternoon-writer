## Quotes
- "I remember you, evil prince. You're the one that killed poor Muradin!" — WC3 *The Frozen Throne*, *Into the Shadow Web Caverns* — post-Third War (Arthas undead) — trait-only
- "I won't let ye through this door, traitor." — same mission — post-Third War — trait-only
- "The recent quakes have awakened dark things under the ice... ancient, horrid things." — same mission — post-Third War — trait-only
- "We've vowed to keep them locked where they are." — same mission — post-Third War — yes (oath-keeping trait)
- "That was for Muradin! Now you'll never get across!" — same mission, after blowing the bridge — post-Third War — trait-only
- "You an' that prince of yours are gonna pay for what he did to ole' Muradin!" — WoW Legion (spirit, death knight campaign), /wiki/Baelgun_Flamebeard — later era — trait-only
- "What... what have I done?" — Legion, death line — later era — trait-only

No Baelgun lines exist in Reign of Chaos, and none were found in WotLK Howling Fjord. He is dead by WotLK. Baelgun's Excavation Site lists no Baelgun NPC, and its quest dialogue ("The Delicate Sound of Thunder") belongs to other NPCs.

## Patterns
- **Dialect markers (verified).** "ye" for you, "an'" for and, "ole'" for old, "gonna". The surrounding expedition dwarves use "'em", "bastards", "That there treasure" and "A pint for every ghoul". Muradin, his commander, uses "lad", "boy", "Bloody hell!", "Damn" and "my mates". Use "lad" only by analogy; no verified Baelgun use. Mountain King unit lines add "Aye", "Brilliant", "bloody", "For Khaz Modan!" and pub and drink jokes.
- **Sentence length.** Short and declarative. He pairs an accusation with a refusal ("I won't let ye through this door, traitor."), and his one longer line is a warning that trails off in ellipses ("ancient, horrid things").
- **Forms of address.** All his canon lines target Arthas as an enemy: "evil prince", "traitor", "that prince of yours". No canon line shows him addressing a living, allied Arthas. For the AU, "Prince Arthas", "yer highness" (said a bit dry) or "lad" are plausible but unverified. Muradin calls Arthas "lad"/"boy", and Baelgun as second-in-command likely would not presume that far.
- **Loyalty to Muradin.** Every verified line keys to Muradin ("poor Muradin", "ole' Muradin", "That was for Muradin!"). He grieves openly and personally, and the affection is open and simple. This is his core, and it fits the AU where he carries Muradin out.
- **Oaths and vows.** He speaks in vows: "We've vowed to keep them locked where they are." He is a gatekeeper and a duty-holder, and he will not yield.
- **Humour.** No verified jokes from Baelgun himself. Expedition and Mountain King lines are gallows-hearty ("Kill 'em all twice, that's what I say!", "A pint for every ghoul ye blast!"). Dwarven humour in his company is drink-and-fight banter.
- **Under pressure.** He gets more defiant, not more cautious: he blows the bridge and holds the door. The non-canon RPG calls him wily, a fighter who retreats when outmatched. That is non-canon, so treat it as flavour only.
- **Never says (on record).** Diplomatic hedging, flattery of humans, or surrender.

## Later-era flags
- All Frozen Throne lines come after Arthas takes Frostmourne. Use their hostility toward "the prince" only after the story's turn. Before that, keep the dialect and the Muradin loyalty and drop the hatred.
- Legion lines come from a spirit or undead echo ("What... what have I done?" implies corruption). They are later era, trait-only.
- The non-canon Warcraft RPG has him survive with 200 dwarves. Ignore it.

## Thinness note
The canon is thin. He has about five Frozen Throne lines and three Legion lines, all hostile to Arthas and all after the Third War. The wiki says novel ch. 15 shows him organizing the dwarves' defences, but no novel dialogue was found [unverified whether he speaks there]. No Reign of Chaos or Howling Fjord lines exist. Build his living voice from verified dwarven dialect markers, his steadfast duty, and his devotion to Muradin.
