## Patterns

**Humour.**
- Dry, deflating and a little vain about his marksmanship (lines 7 and 8).
- He needles Sylvanas about how bossy she is.
- That is the "wit like a knife" in the profile, made friendlier: he teases the person he serves.

**Precision about rank.** He corrects titles (line 1). He knows exactly what authority he holds and what he doesn't. That suits the Convocation voice in the profile.

**Duty sentences.**
- First person, plain, with Quel'Thalas as the object (lines 3 and 4).
- He accepts being used and says so. This is the man who will spend his whole political life to buy a camp.

**Nostalgia.** He misses the field (line 6). In this story he is losing it to the chamber.

**Address.** He uses "Sylvanas" by name, flat, when he is being serious (line 2).

**Under pressure.**
- His later lines grow formal and inward (line 9).
- The profile's barometer holds: when the jokes stop, he is spent.

**Canon gap.** No era-compatible speech was found. The sheet's register is inferred from these traits and the story profile.
