## Patterns
- **Hostess-tease.** Her best line is "Oh, careful not to bleed on the rug, please dear?" She cares about the furnishings more than the person dying, and phrases it as a polite request with an endearment. This is the old-money Barov register in one line. Use the courtesy as the cruelty.
- **Endearments downward.** "dear" for a stranger she has just killed. It is condescending and soft-voiced, and comes at the end of the sentence.
- **Taunting questions.** "Feeling a bit... dizzy?", "Well?", "What makes something real?" She asks questions she knows the answer to and pauses before the key word. Her ellipsis is a magician's beat, not hesitation.
- **Carnival and nursery register.** "Come, try your luck!" (a fairground barker), "Ashes, ashes, we all fall down" (a children's rhyme), and a theatrical laugh. She treats the fight as a game she is running.
- **Mild vulgarity used for effect.** "real stones" is a slangy jab from a noblewoman, chosen to surprise.
- **Illusion and reality theme.** "Mistress of Illusion", "What makes something real?", and her dying "Unreal..." All three are about what is real. The lore backs this: she was "once an archmage of Dalaran and a powerful illusionist", and the Adventure Guide calls her "a valued educator". So she teaches, and she teaches by tricking.
- **Sentence length.** Very short. Fragments, a question, an exclamation. No speeches on record.
- **Oaths.** None on record.
- **What she never says (on record).** She never mentions the Cult, Kel'Thuzad, necromancy as a cause, her family, or her father. She is never sincere, never pleads, and never explains herself.
- **Under pressure.** Only her death line shows it. The poise breaks into fragments and real surprise ("death... hurts?"), then a last dry word that is still a pun ("Unreal..."). Her wit survives, but her control does not.

## Later-era flags
- Every WoW line comes from her undead-ghost boss form (MoP, 2012). Use them as evidence of traits only: playful, hostess-cruel, illusion-minded. Do not port the cackle or the boss taunts into the story's living, young Jandice.
- Hearthstone lines are non-canon and comic, but they show her alive and her parents owning the school. That premise is the closest match to this story.
- **Age mismatch.** Canon makes her a former Dalaran archmage, so an adult at least of rank. The story's young peer of Jaina is an AU choice and has no canon dialogue to draw on.

## Thinness note
The canon is thin. She has six WoW boss lines (all ghost era), one Hearthstone play line and one flavor text. She has no Classic dialogue, and I found no novel or quest-text dialogue. Her voice in the story has to be built. The safest canon anchors are the hostess-tease ("careful not to bleed on the rug, please dear?"), the taunting question with a pause ("Feeling a bit... dizzy?"), and the idea that she teaches through illusion.
