## 2. Her real flaws at this age

- **Eagerness to prove herself.** She "badgered the Kirin Tor's leader Archmage Antonidas into accepting her as an apprentice." With Kael'thas she "wanted to advance on her own merits and not simply because an elven prince enjoyed her company." ([Jaina Proudmoore](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Jaina_Proudmoore), Student of Dalaran)
- **Pride in being right and being clever.** Antonidas "drummed into her not to be arrogant" and told her the stories of Medivh and Aegwynn as warnings (summary of *Rise of the Lich King* via search results on the Jaina pages). The wiki's Personality section: "one of the most inquisitive and talented sorceresses in Lordaeron."
- **Curiosity that outruns caution.** See the abomination exchange above: she wants to study it while it is still alive.
- **Believing the best of people.** She "sensed great power in the Prophet and thought that perhaps they should heed his warnings" when Arthas and Antonidas would not. She feels "compassion for the captive orcs" where Arthas does not. ([Jaina Proudmoore](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Jaina_Proudmoore), Third War)
- **Not fighting for herself in love.** When Arthas ended it she "was very hurt by the decision but did not fight him on it." Earlier she had "promised Arthas that she would never deny him." He took her leaving at Stratholme "as a betrayal of the promise she had made." (same page)
- **Guilt that assumes responsibility.** Later canon: she was "still haunted by Arthas' descending to darkness, thinking it was her fault," and "filled with regret over her past actions (or lack of them), such as whether she could have saved Arthas." (Jaina page, Relationships > Arthas) This regret is a pride-shaped guilt: it assumes she could have stopped him.

The wiki: "Arthas soon began to question whether the two of them were ready to be together, and abruptly ended the relationship so Jaina could focus on her magical studies in Dalaran, while he could focus on his commitments to Lordaeron." The Arthas page's Personality section gives his reason as "he decided he wasn't ready to marry yet." ([Arthas Menethil](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Arthas_Menethil)). The Winter Veil setting is from secondary summaries ([outof.games Jaina lore](https://outof.games/hearthstone/guides/73-the-story-of-jaina-proudmoore-the-missing-pages-of-book-of-heroes/)) **[unverified against the novel text]**.

## 3a. Canon grounding: Arthas history (verified this session on warcraft.wiki.gg)

**The breakup.**
- **Who:** Arthas. "Arthas began questioning whether the two of them were ready to be together and abruptly ended the relationship" ([Jaina Proudmoore](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Jaina_Proudmoore), early biography).
- **Why (stated):** "so that Jaina could focus on her magical studies in Dalaran and Arthas could focus on his commitments to Lordaeron" (same). The Arthas page's Personality section adds: "Arthas abruptly ended their relationship when he decided he wasn't ready to marry yet" ([Arthas Menethil](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Arthas_Menethil)).
- **Her reaction:** "Jaina was very hurt by the decision but did not fight him on it." "Both of them agreed to remain friends." (Jaina page)
- **When:** after the Noblegarden and Hallow's End courtship. After Hallow's End "Jaina promised Arthas that she would never deny him." A wiki summary places the breakup at a Winter Veil ball, and a secondary guide says it was "while she was staying with his family in Capital City for the holiday of Winter Veil". I could not quote Winter Veil verbatim from the wiki page, so treat it as likely but **[not verified verbatim]**.
- **Later:** "After some initial awkwardness, Arthas and Jaina began rekindling their relationship" on the plague investigation. At Stratholme he took her leaving "as a betrayal of the promise she had made".
- **For the author's question** ("if Jaina left him because of her studies then that's that"): in canon **Arthas** ended it, and her studies were the reason he gave. Jaina did not leave him. She let him go without a fight. The trade is built on exactly that.

**The Ch6 replacement memory: the internment camp night (canon, *Rise of the Lich King* ch. 3).**
- "On their way to Dalaran, the young Prince Arthas Menethil and Lady Jaina Proudmoore decided to spy on the orcs." Despite guard patrols and two watchtowers, "the pair managed to slip past them to the camp." Jaina "saw the females and infants" and "felt remorse and doubts"; Arthas "was adamant that the camps were necessary." ([Lordamere Internment Camp](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lordamere_Internment_Camp), citing RotLK ch. 3. The wiki notes that the camp in the scene is not Lordamere itself.)
- The Jaina page says it was his idea: "the prince convinced her to sneak off with him and look at one of the internment camps", and "Unlike Arthas, Jaina found herself feeling compassion for the captive orcs."
- Why it fits Ch6 B5: it is rash (the heir sneaking past armed watchtowers at night on a whim), he was sure before he looked, and he was still sure after she saw the families. "Today he was slow, polite, and just as sure" lands on it.
- **Rejected: Invincible's death.** It is canon (RotLK ch. 4: he rode out in a growing snowstorm, the horse fell on icy rocks, and Arthas killed him), but "He never revealed the truth of what had happened to anyone else" ([Invincible](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Invincible)). Jaina could not know it, so it cannot be her memory.

## 4. Antonidas

- She pushed her way into his apprenticeship. He saw her as his "finest pupil" and "most promising apprentice", and the wiki says he "looked forward to the day when he would hand his power and mantle of leadership over to Jaina."
- Her memorial to him (later canon): "Your sacrifices will not have been in vain, dearest friend." ([Antonidas](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Antonidas))
- At the end she "wanted to help him defend the city", and he had to talk her into leaving. She did not want to go against him even when going was right.
- Kel'Thuzad was "another member of the Council of Six" whom Antonidas investigated and threatened with exile before he left for the Lich King. In this AU Antonidas also trained him (`characters/antonidas.md`). So Kel'Thuzad is a former pupil of the same master, which makes the Ch12 mirror work.

**What this means for the confession.** Her love for Antonidas is a daughter's love that wants his pride. What she would be ashamed to have read aloud is that she went north without his leave partly to come back with the answer he would be proud of, and that his good opinion matters to her more than being right. That is tender and proud, and it costs her. It is also exactly Kel'Thuzad's history with the same man.

## 5. How she speaks when cornered or ashamed

- Short, first person, plain verbs: "I can't watch you do this."
- She apologises before the hard thing, even when she is the one in the right.
- She says the real reason directly once she has decided to. She does not hedge or put on irony.
- She does not turn round. In Ch12 she looks at the shelf instead of at him (keep it).
- From the voice sheet: when frightened she gets more exact; when guilty she over-focuses on method. When she pays, the method has run out, so the sentence is short.

## 8. The new trades and their grounding

1. **Antonidas:** "I didn't ask his leave, because he'd have said no and I'd have stayed. I want him to be proud of me. I want that more than I want to be right."
   Canon basis: she badgered him into taking her, he relied on her "inquisitive nature", he meant her to succeed him, and she later called him "dearest friend". It is tender (a daughter's wish for his pride) and proud (she admits she went north to win it). Kel'Thuzad, Antonidas's other gifted pupil, mirrors it back to her.
2. **Arthas** (canon-grounded version, replacing the Alterac line): "When he ended it, I didn't fight him. Part of me wanted to go back to my books. I still think that if I'd stayed, he would have listened to me."
   Canon basis: Arthas ended it so she could focus on her studies, and she "was very hurt by the decision but did not fight him on it". "All I ever wanted was to study" is her canon refrain. Her later regret is "whether she could have saved Arthas", and she thought his fall was her fault. The cost is tender (she loved him and let him go), shameful (part of her was glad to have her books back) and proud (she believes she could have held him). After Stratholme (Ch18 B2) the last sentence turns on her, because she was at his side and he listened.
3. **The knowledge (last and dearest):** "I'd learn the rest of it, even the parts I'll never need to stop it. I've been happy at this table. You teach me as fast as you can talk, and I keep up."
   Canon basis: "Let's study it after we kill it, okay?", "That's curious.", "All I ever wanted was to study." This is curiosity past what duty needs, plus her pride in keeping up. It is Kel'Thuzad's own delight in being understood, and it is not a wish to use the spell.

## 7. Tests applied to each new trade

1. Would twenty-three-year-old canon Jaina say it, in earnest, to a man she is afraid of? It must be a sincere admission, not a cynical or manipulative pose.
2. Does it cost her because it is tender, proud or frightened?
3. Would she hide it from Sylvanas, and would it shame her read aloud in court?
4. Is it tied to a canon fact?
5. Is it one to three short spoken sentences, with nothing from `references/slop-hitlist.md` (no negation framing, no document voice, no epigram cap)?
