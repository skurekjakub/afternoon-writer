## Quotes

- "Nearly. There are only a few ships that--" — same mission (cut off by Arthas) — living Third War — yes
- "Prince Arthas, we haven't found any trace of Mal'Ganis." — same mission, Mission Complete — living Third War — yes
- "Yes, milord." — same mission, accepting the order to hold the site — living Third War — yes

Note: the five *Shores of Northrend* lines were written for the unnamed RoC Captain. Marwyn's name is a Reforged retcon, so they are his only by later assignment. The novel gives him no dialogue on the wiki pages consulted.

## Patterns
- **Address.** "Milord" and "Prince Arthas", matching Falric's field register. No verified "my prince" or "Your Highness". He is addressed as plain "Marwyn!" by Falric, his superior (novel).
- **Sentence length.** Short. Observations, reports and acknowledgements. He gets cut off mid-report ("Nearly. There are only a few ships that--"), so he is the one who is interrupted, not the one who interrupts.
- **Observes Arthas.** He is the man who notices the prince is not shivering and asks if he is alright. Concern for Arthas comes through as an observation rather than advice. This fits the wiki line that he saw Arthas "not just as his prince, but as a true friend" since boyhood.
- **Oaths.** "Light-forsaken" as a curse word. Mild, Lordaeronian and Light-framed, like Falric's "Light preserve us".
- **Humour.** None on record. His tone is plain and slightly rueful about the weather. Any humour should be quiet and observational.
- **Under pressure.** No verified living lines show him in combat. He acknowledges orders briefly ("Yes, milord.") and reports negatives straight ("we haven't found any trace").
- **Never says (on record).** He gives no orders on record, has no long speeches, and never disagrees openly. He is subordinate to Falric (wiki: "subordinate to Captain Falric").
- **Contrast with Falric.** Falric hedges then speaks bluntly. Marwyn notices and asks. (This is inferred from very few lines.)

## Later-era flags
- All Halls of Reflection lines are undead gloating, and the Terenas line is a mockery of the regicide: trait-only, later era. Only "As you wish milord" (HoR spawn) echoes his living deference.
- In the Shadowlands era he was re-raised by Mawsworn kyrian. Not relevant to voice.
- The wiki notes that he appears as a translucent spirit in HoR but was corporeal in Reign of Chaos.

## Thinness note
The canon is very thin. Marwyn has no verified novel dialogue. His only living lines are five short WC3 Captain lines retroactively named "Marwyn" in Reforged. His characterization rests on one wiki biography sentence (lifelong friend of Arthas, King's Guard, under Falric). Most of his voice will have to be built in the story. Keep it consistent with "milord", short reports, and noticing Arthas's condition.
