# Voice sheet brief (shared by all voice agents)

Repo: /home/jakubs/repositories/afternoon-writer. Story: The Plague Road, a Warcraft AU set before and during the Third War. Sylvanas is alive (pre-undeath). Jaina is pre-Theramore. Arthas starts as a prince and falls. Kel'Thuzad and Arthas die in this book.

Read first:
- the existing sheets in stories/the-plague-road/characters/voice-sheets/ (the format to follow)
- the character profiles in stories/the-plague-road/characters/
- references/slop-hitlist.md, because sample lines must not contain any hitlist pattern
- the outlines in .afternoon/outlines/ where the character appears (grep the name), to see what the story needs them to say

## Research (canon characters only)
- Use WebSearch/WebFetch to pull real speech from Warcraft III (and The Frozen Throne where era-appropriate), WoW quest and NPC lines, the novels (Christie Golden's *Arthas: Rise of the Lich King*, *Cycle of Hatred*, *Day of the Dragon*, Richard Knaak, the Warcraft manga, and later novels only for underlying patterns), and Wowpedia/Warcraft Wiki quote pages.
- Save findings to research/voice-canon/<character>.md:
  - short quotes only, one or two lines each;
  - for every quote: the source, the era, and whether it is era-compatible with this story;
  - aim for 8 to 20 quotes per major character and 3 to 8 per minor one.
- From the quotes, extract patterns: sentence length, imagery, forms of address, oaths, humour, what they never say, and how pressure changes them. Patterns matter more than lines.
- Flag lines from later eras (undead Sylvanas, post-Theramore Jaina, Lich King Arthas). Use them only as evidence of an underlying trait, never as a register.

## Sheet rules
- One sheet per character at stories/the-plague-road/characters/voice-sheets/<firstname>-voice.md (lowercase, apostrophes dropped: lorthemar, kelthuzad, malganis). Refine existing sheets in place: keep what is right, fix what canon contradicts, remove sample lines that read as AI-tells.
- Add a section "Canon reference lines" with 3 to 6 short canon quotes and their sources. The writer copies from voice sheets, so label it clearly.
- Write the "Voice samples" as story-specific shapes, not canon quotes. Keep them plain, and check each one against the hitlist (no permission tags, no counting, no claim-then-gloss, no aphorism caps).
- Major characters get the full format. Minor characters get a short sheet (40 to 70 lines): baseline register, three to five syntax rules, forms of address, one or two tells, three to six sample shapes, red flags, voice test.
- **POV characters only (Sylvanas and Jaina):** add an "Inner voice (free indirect thought)" section before "POV filter". It covers:
  - how she talks to herself: idiom, oaths, dry asides;
  - what she argues with herself about, and how (questions, bad answers, self-correction);
  - who she is unfair to in her head;
  - what she won't admit;
  - the gap between her spoken voice and her inner voice;
  - four to six short shape examples, checked against the hitlist;
  - a test: could she mutter it under her breath?
  
  Sylvanas speaks in finished judgments aloud, but inside she argues, sardonic and impatient with herself. Jaina self-corrects mid-thought, pushes her guilt into method, and is dry about herself.
- Do not edit outlines, agents or references. Write only the sheets and research/voice-canon/ files.
