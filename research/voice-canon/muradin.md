## Patterns

**Dialect.** In WC3, in the story's era, his dialect is light: nearly standard English with "lad", "aye" and an occasional "ya". The heavy "ye / ta / me hammer" spelling belongs to later WoW. Spell a little of the accent and let word choice carry the rest.

**Syntax.**
- Short declaratives.
- He states a feeling and then a commitment ("bad feeling... But I promised").
- Commands start on a verb: "Hold", "Leave it be", "Forget".

**Forms of address.**
- "Lad" for Arthas.
- He drops to "Arthas" when he is worried or warning (lines 2 and 6).
- He is gruff with humans as a group ("slow for a human").

**Oaths.** "Damn", "hell", "bastard" (WC3 attack line). They are used like punctuation, never stacked.

**Humour.** Ribbing about humans. Jokes about his brothers (Magni the king, Brann the explorer) and about himself.

**Under pressure.**
- His questions get shorter and more direct.
- He reads things aloud and says what they mean (line 4).
- Line 5 shows him blaming himself: "I should've known".

**Never says.** No speeches. No theory. He explains only as far as the danger in front of him.
