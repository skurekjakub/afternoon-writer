## Patterns

**Forms of address.** These carry his arc, as the profile already says.
- "Lad" when he is teaching and kind.
- "Son" when he is steadying.
- "Boy" when he is rebuking.
- "Arthas" alone, with no title, when he passes judgement (line 4).
- He uses the title "prince" only to set it beneath a higher claim: "You may be the prince, but...".

**Syntax.**
- He leads with the imperative, then gives the reason: "Easy, lad. Brave as you are, you can't...", "Watch your tone... You may be...".
- "We" statements define the order: "We are paladins."

**Oaths.** "By my honor", "For the Light", "Light curse you!". "Hell" appears once, at the extreme.

**Humour.** Tired and self-directed: "I'm getting too old for this." He never jokes at another man's expense.

**Under pressure.**
- He gets shorter, and he states rank or principle as fact.
- He refuses without hedging ("Nor would I obey that command even if you were!").
- His sentences stay grammatical. He doesn't fragment.

**Never says.**
- Nothing cynical about the Light.
- No flattery.
- No long theology. His faith is practical ("My church is the field of battle").
