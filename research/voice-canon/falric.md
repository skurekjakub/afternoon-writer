## Patterns
- **Address.** He calls Arthas "sir" in the novel, often to open a sentence ("Sir, if I may", "Sir..."). He uses "Prince Arthas" in WC3 reports and "milord" as the WC3 Captain's address (Dissension, now Luc's). No verified "my prince" or "Your Highness". Use "sir" in private and "Prince Arthas" or "milord" in the field.
- **Sentence length.** Short to medium. Plain declaratives, a report followed by an assessment ("We can hold out for a while, but..."). He gives facts first.
- **Hedging before candour.** "Sir, if I may. I'm... well..." He asks leave and stumbles, then says the blunt thing. He is deferential in form and honest in content.
- **Oaths.** "Light preserve us." It is mild and pious, a soldier's Light-invocation. No profanity on record.
- **Register.** Common-born, rural Lordaeron. He uses "one of them undead" (nonstandard "them") and references to farmers and the countryside. The novel (ch. 10) says his father wanted him to be a farmer. The generic WC3 Captain's line "I should have been a farmer like my father wanted" echoes this. The echo is inferred, not stated as Falric's.
- **Humour.** Dry and self-deprecating at most. The generic Captain's pissed lines, "Lousy pay, constant danger" and "at least I get to hobnob with royalty", fit him, but they are unit lines, not Falric's.
- **Under pressure.** His speech gets more fragmented ("Who- what- we lose in numbers") and more blunt, but he keeps the "sir". He reports the situation honestly and does not panic or mutiny. His fear of undeath is his stated personal line.
- **Never says (on record).** Flowery court speech, open defiance of Arthas, or theology beyond "Light preserve us". He addresses no one by first name except his subordinate ("Marwyn!").
- **Bond.** Arthas brought him warm drinks on foul-weather watches, which earned his "genuine respect" (wiki biography, novel). His loyalty is personal, not just a matter of rank.

## Later-era flags
- All Halls of Reflection lines ("Despair... so delicious...", "Sniveling maggot!", "The children of Stratholme fought with more ferocity!", "As you wish milord.", "Marwyn, finish them...") are undead, gloating death-knight speech: trait-only, later era. Only "As you wish milord" carries forward his deference and his address form.
- Hearthstone flavour text ("God I freaking love pears.") is a joke and non-canon for voice. Ignore it.
- Reforged reassigned the WC3 Captain's lines among Falric, Marwyn and Luc Valonforth (patch 1.33), and later to Luc alone (1.36.1, per the Frostmourne page). Treat any "Captain" line as contested.

## Thinness note
The canon is moderately thin. He has about five novel lines (verified via the wiki, not the book itself) and one or two WC3 lines with contested attribution. No verified Falric line is set at Hearthglen or Stratholme. The Culling page lists him as present (novel ch. 12) with no dialogue. Build his voice from the novel's "sir" and candour pattern and from his rural, common-born background.
