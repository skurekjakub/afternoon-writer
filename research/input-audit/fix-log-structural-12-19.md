## For the Ch24 agent: the Kel'Thuzad trade, verbatim

Jaina's three payments, as written in Ch12 (End of chapter state, B3, B6, B9). Kel'Thuzad wrote each into the bound journal at his elbow, quoting her. She thinks it is his research log. The volume goes out of Scholomance in her bundle (Ch13 B8, Sylvanas sees her take it). She never opens it (Ch14 B5, Ch18 handoff).

| Order | What he gave | What she paid (topic) | Her words |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | The carrier piece (grain is only the carrier; the body is part of the process), to prove he has the goods | Nothing | — |
| 1 (Ch12 B3-B4) | Resonance: conditioned tissue keeps patterned potential, and resonance wakes it into answering | Antonidas | "I love him, and he's too slow. I stopped waiting for his leave a long time ago. I still call him first, so he won't know." |
| 2 (Ch12 B6-B7) | The waking trigger, and the extra binding that keeps a thinking self (crude ghouls vs preserved dead) | Arthas and Alterac | "At Alterac I saw what he would do with a hard choice. I left him instead of staying to stop it, and I was glad to go." |
| 3, last and dearest (Ch12 B8-B10) | The Lich King in Northrend, the Burning Legion above him, the Scourge as preparation for invasion, Kel'Thuzad's expected life past death | What she would do with the knowledge. He refused two answers first: "Stop it" ("That is what you will tell Antonidas") and "Take it to Dalaran" ("That is what you would write in a report") | "I'd keep your notes. I want to watch it work once before I stop it." |

Facts the trial can lean on:
- Price 2 gives the prosecution a motive reading (she knew what the prince was and left him).
- Price 3 matches the evidence: the notes were found in her kit.
- Sylvanas never hears any of the three before Ch24. Ch14 B7: asked how she got him to talk, Jaina said, "He wanted someone to understand him. I did."
- Antonidas hears the chain (Ch18 B5) but not the price.
