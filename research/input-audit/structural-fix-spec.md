## House rules for every edit
- Keep each outline's existing format: header block, References, starting position, Still in the dark, Hold back, End of chapter state, Character Arcs, Cast and handoff rules, Next chapter starts, Scenes with `**The action:**`, `**Cast:**`, `**Starting point:**`, and beats of five bullets (the event; the facts; "They do not know..."; "Ground the ...:"; the outcome line). Mal'Ganis beats keep their `Second read (plant only; the prose must not explain it):` bullet.
- Write in plain English. Avoid every pattern in `references/slop-hitlist.md`, especially:
  - permission tags ("and she let it");
  - contact verbs with agency (found/settled);
  - counting devices;
  - unplaceable-then-placed;
  - negation framing;
  - theme-stating epiphany lines.
  The outlines feed the writer, and the writer copies their phrasing.
- **Keep chapter numbers as they are now (1 to 25) while you edit.** The coordinator renumbers after the Ch18/19 merge. Do not rename files unless your section says so.
- Update every summary block your beat changes touch: End of chapter state, Character Arcs, Cast, handoff, and "Next chapter starts" or "When". Neighbouring chapters owned by another agent must stay consistent with the cross-chapter facts below.
- Log every change in your fix log: chapter, beat, before (short), after (short), and why.

## Cross-chapter facts (all agents treat these as canon)

   - Ch3: it sticks for a second.
   - Ch6: Sylvanas notes the second use and asks Jaina, who gets no magical read. The man is gone. Sylvanas files it as wrong but not yet a pattern.
   - Ch9: Cyndia reports it secondhand. On the ridge Sylvanas asks Cyndia to describe the robed man: a robe, at a distance, walking away. There is nothing to hunt.

   - Ch17: Sylvanas hears it herself, turns to follow the carter, the city starts breaking behind her, and she chooses the wall.
   - Ch25: she recognises it.
