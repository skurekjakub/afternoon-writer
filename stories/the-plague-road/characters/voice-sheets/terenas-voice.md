# Voice Sheet: King Terenas Menethil II

*Story mode: an elderly king who holds the Alliance together. He has one scene in the throne room (outline chapter 22): relief, confusion, death, the crown on the marble. He speaks very little. Every word has to be his.*

Canon research: `research/voice-canon/terenas.md`. **Canon is thin.** There are few lines, and the novels give him almost no dialogue. This sheet leans on the profile and on a small set of patterns.

## Canon reference lines

- "I will summon my neighboring kings. These events concern us all." (*Tides of Darkness*)
  - A royal "us" that means kingdoms. Decisive.
- "I will not institute quarantine without proof of your claims, Ambassador." (WC3, as cited on Warcraft Wiki)
  - Measured, and he wants proof first.
- "I don't know who you are or what you believe, but this is not the time for rambling prophets!" (WC3, to Medivh)
  - A rare flare of impatience.
- "What is this? What are you doing, my son?" (WC3 Reign of Chaos, last words)
  - His canon last words. Usable as written in Ch22.

## Baseline register

- Kingly, warm and practised: a public voice that has addressed courts for decades.
- In private with Arthas, his sentences go plain and fatherly. He uses "my son" or "Arthas", never "Prince".
- He doesn't suspect his son. Every line in the throne room should assume Arthas is the boy he raised.

## Syntax rules

- Short, full declaratives. No ornament, even in ceremony.
- His relief comes out as action: get up, come here, let me see you.
- His confusion is a question with no time for an answer. The sentence can break off.

## Forms of address

- **Arthas:** "my son", "Arthas".
- **The court:** "my lords".
- **Strangers in his throne room:** he calls the guards before he speaks to them.

## Tells

- He starts to rise from the throne to meet his son.
- His hands reach out before the words do.

## Sample shapes (story-specific, not canon)

- (Relief) "Arthas. Get up, get up. Let me look at you."
- (Relief) "We had the worst from the north for weeks. I'm glad to be a fool about it."
- (Confusion) "Arthas? What—"

## Red flags

- No speeches. He has a handful of lines in the whole book.
- He never suspects Arthas, and he never sounds weak. He sounds like a father caught open.

## Voice test

Would a tired king say it with his arms half open? Then it is probably Terenas.
