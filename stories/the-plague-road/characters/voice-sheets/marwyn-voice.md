# Voice Sheet: Lieutenant Marwyn

*Falric's quieter counterpart and Arthas's friend since boyhood. Present for texture, chain of command, and the widening cost to the army. He has no scripted beats in the current outlines. Minor sheet.*

Canon is very thin. Marwyn has no verified novel dialogue. His only living lines are five short Warcraft III "Captain" lines that Reforged later credited to him. See `research/voice-canon/marwyn.md`. Most of this voice is built for the story.

## Baseline register

- Short. Reports, acknowledgements, the occasional plain observation.
- Lower-key than Falric. Falric hedges, then speaks bluntly. Marwyn notices, and asks.
- He is the one who gets cut off mid-report, not the one who cuts others off.
- His concern for Arthas comes out as something he has seen, never as advice.

## Syntax rules

- Acknowledgements are two or three words: "Yes, milord."
- Reports lead with the fact and stop: "Road's clear to the bridge."
- When something is wrong with Arthas he says what he sees and adds one short question.
- No orders upward, no open disagreement. Below him, he gives orders through Falric's words.

## Forms of address

- **Arthas:** "milord" in the field, "Prince Arthas" in a formal report. In private, rarely, "Arthas," and only in Stage 1 chapters.
- **Falric:** "Captain."
- **Outsiders:** "my lady," "Ranger-General." Polite and brief.

## Tells

- **Oath:** "Light-forsaken" as a mild curse, for weather, ground or the job.
- **Watching the prince:** he notices that Arthas has not eaten, is not cold, has not slept. One line, then silence.

## Sample shapes

- "Yes, milord."
- "Most of them are across. The last wagons are still—"
- "No sign of them on the north road, milord."
- "You haven't sat down since Hearthglen, milord."
- "Light-forsaken mud. Begging your pardon, my lady."
- "Captain wants the second company at the gate."

## Canon reference lines

- "This howling wind cuts to the bone and you're not even shaking. Milord, are you alright?" (Warcraft III, *The Shores of Northrend*; credited to Marwyn only in Reforged). He notices, then asks.
- "Prince Arthas, we haven't found any trace of Mal'Ganis." (same mission). A straight report of a negative.
- "Yes, milord." (same mission). The whole of his acknowledgement.

## Red flags

- No undead gloating or mockery of Terenas from Halls of Reflection.
- No speeches, no jokes for effect, no open defiance.
- Do not make him a copy of Falric. He talks less and watches more.
- Do not give him a scene the outline does not give him.

## Voice test

Is it short enough to be said while handing someone their reins? Does it notice something about Arthas without telling him what to do? Then it may be Marwyn.
