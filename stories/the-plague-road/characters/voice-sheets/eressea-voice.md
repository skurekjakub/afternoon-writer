# Voice Sheet: Magistrix Eressea

*Story mode: the Convocation's liaison. She is formal, ornate and politically frictionless in a way Sylvanas hates on sight. Use her when the institution needs a polite face.*

## Grounding

- Source: `supporting.md` only. **She appears in no outline beat** (grep of `.afternoon/outlines/` found nothing). The location notes say the story does not cut to Silvermoon for full scenes, so expect her by letter, at a border hand-off, or in Sylvanas's memory.
- She is a Silvermoon court elf. The Convocation is not villainous: it is slow, certain, and protected by being narrowly right (`locations/silvermoon-and-quelthalas.md`).

## Baseline register

- Courtly and complete. Every sentence is fully dressed, with titles and qualifications in place.
- Agreeable on the surface. She never says no. She says what would please the chamber, and then what the chamber is able to do.
- She speaks for the Convocation and never for herself. "I" appears only to make herself smaller.
- She is never rude and never hurried, and that is what makes her infuriating.

## Syntax rules

- Courtesy first, then the matter, then a qualification that takes back the matter.
- Passive and collective subjects: "it has been decided", "the chamber holds", "the magisters have read".
- Long, balanced sentences with no sharp verb anywhere in them.
- Her formality comes from **court idiom** (regard, confidence, the chamber, the honour of the Ranger-General). It never comes from memo jargon.

## Forms of address

- "Ranger-General Windrunner", in full, every time. "Ranger-Lord Theron" for Lor'themar.
- Thalassian greeting where it fits: "Bal'a dash, malanore" (canon: "Greetings, traveller").

## Tells

- Perfect stillness, hands folded, robes that have never seen a road.
- She agrees with the objection before she sets it aside.

## Sample shapes (story-specific, never canon)

- "Ranger-General Windrunner. The Convocation sends its regard, and its full confidence in your judgement within the border."
- "The magisters have read your report with great attention. They find the wards sound."
- "Nothing would please the chamber more. The chamber is not, at present, able to."
- "I only carry the chamber's word, Ranger-General. I would not presume to add to it."

## Setting reference lines

- "Do you require aid, human?" — High elf Sorceress, Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos. *Elven courtesy with condescension built in.*
- "If you insist." — High elf Sorceress, Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos. *Polite compliance with no warmth in it.*

## Red flags

- Do **not** use bureaucratese from the hitlist: no "resources", "allocation", "budgetary constraints", "actionable", "pursuant to". She is a courtier, not a clerk.
- Do **not** make her a villain or a liar. She believes the chamber is right.
- Do **not** let her show irritation. Sylvanas's contempt runs off her like rain.
- Do **not** give her an elaborate parting blessing. Any blessing-farewell blurs the Mal'Ganis tell.

## Voice test

Does it sound gracious and complete, and leave the listener holding nothing? Then it is Eressea.
