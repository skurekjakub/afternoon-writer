# Voice Sheet: Muradin Bronzebeard

*Story mode: leader of the dwarven expedition in Northrend. He is Arthas's old trainer and the first casualty of Frostmourne (outline chapter 20, handed to Baelgun in chapter 21). He is blunt and decent, and curious for the cleanest of reasons.*

Canon research: `research/voice-canon/muradin.md`. Warcraft III's Northrend missions are this exact era.

## Canon reference lines

- "Isn't that a bit much, lad?" (WC3 Reign of Chaos, *Dissension*)
  - Understatement used as an objection.
- "I have a bad feeling about this, lad. But I promised I'd see this through." (WC3 Reign of Chaos, *Frostmourne*)
  - He states the feeling, then the commitment.
- "Hold, lad. There's an inscription on the dais. It's a warning." (same)
  - He reads aloud and says what it means.
- "Leave it be, Arthas. Forget this business and lead your men home." (same)
  - Commands that start on a verb. He drops "lad" when he is serious.
- "You're kinda slow for a human, aren't ya?" (WC3 hero response)
  - Ribbing at humans.

## Baseline register

- Short declaratives with gruff warmth. He explains only as much as survival needs.
- **Light dialect.** Warcraft III Muradin is close to standard English: "lad", "lass", "aye", the odd "ya". The heavy spellings ("ye", "ta", "me hammer") belong to later WoW, so keep them rare. Let word choice carry the accent.
- He swears like punctuation ("damn", "hell"), one oath at a time.

## Syntax rules

- He starts commands on the verb: "Sit." "Drink." "Hold."
- He uses understatement for alarm. The worse it gets, the smaller his words.
- He asks direct questions that get shorter under pressure.
- He jokes, then goes back to his ale. He never explains the joke.

## Forms of address

- **Arthas:** "lad", or "Arthas" when he is warning him.
- **Jaina:** "lass".
- **Sylvanas:** "Ranger", once he's seen her shoot.
- **Humans as a group:** "you humans", fondly and with an insult in it.

## Tells

- His beard-rings click when he talks.
- His hammer goes onto its harness when he stands, and he doesn't make a speech about leaving (outline chapter 20).

## Sample shapes (story-specific, not canon)

- (Chapter 20, camp) "Sit. Drink. It's foul, but it's hot."
- (Of Arthas) "He came through here. I told him to leave the thing be and go home. He thanked me for the ale."
- (Naming the blade) "Frostmourne. The runes say whoever takes it pays for it, and I don't think they mean coin."
- (Watching them work the map) "Ha. My cousin and his wife have been married since before the Portal, and they still can't share a map like that."
- (Cave) "Get down, lass!"
- (Joining the line) "Aye, I'm coming. Somebody has to tell him I said so."

## Red flags

- No phonetic soup. One or two dialect markers per line at most.
- No speeches and no lore lectures. He names the blade and the danger, and stops.
- No grim fatalism. His decency and curiosity are why his fall hurts.
- The outline's joke about the map is in its beat card. Write a fresh one rather than the outline's wording.

## Voice test

Could he say it with a mug in his hand and his eyes on your map? Is it shorter than you expected? Then it is probably Muradin.
