# Voice Sheet: Antonidas

*Story mode: Archmage of Dalaran and Jaina's master. He believes her from the start and can't move his institution fast enough to matter. He is mostly a voice on the sending stone (outline chapters 9, 10 and 18), in person at the palace arch (outline chapter 21), and in the tribunal gallery (outline chapter 23).*

Canon research: `research/voice-canon/antonidas.md`. **Canon is thin.** There are very few spoken lines, so the sheet relies on the profile.

## Canon reference lines

- "It's your inquisitive nature that I've come to rely on, child." (WC3, *Jaina's meeting*)
  - Warmth and authority together. He calls her "child".
- "That crazed fool's convinced that the world is about to end." (same, about the Prophet)
  - Dry, and dismissive of what is unorthodox. That is his blind spot.
- "Greetings, Prince Arthas. How fares your noble father?" (WC3 Reign of Chaos, *The Siege of Dalaran*, after Arthas's fall)
  - Courtesy used as a blade.
- "It pains me to even look at you, Arthas." (same)
  - Grief in one plain sentence.

## Baseline register

- Measured, paternal and precise. He speaks in complete thoughts with careful qualifiers.
- He states the institution's position in the first person plural ("We have opened an inquiry"). He speaks for himself in the singular only when it costs him something.
- His sentences stay unhurried even when he is in a hurry. The silences on the stone carry the rest.

## Syntax rules

- He qualifies before he commits: "If what you say holds, then..."
- He gives procedure as a fact, not as an excuse. He knows exactly how slow the Council is and says so.
- His snide courtesy is for people who obstruct him: titles, formal greetings, a question with an edge.
- When the father in him gets past the Archmage, he drops to short sentences and uses her name.

## Forms of address

- **Jaina:** "child" when he is warm or worried, "Jaina" when he is frightened, "apprentice" never.
- **Others:**
  - Full titles for the court and the Council.
  - "Ranger-General" for Sylvanas.
- **The Kirin Tor:** "the Council", "the Six".

## Tells

- Long pauses on the stone. Through it he sounds old, or Jaina hears him as old now (outline chapter 18).
- At the arch he wipes the blood off her lip with his thumb instead of asking her to come home (outline chapter 21).

## Sample shapes (story-specific, not canon)

- (Chapter 9, stone) "The Council has opened a formal inquiry into Kel'Thuzad. It will move at the Council's pace, child, and I can't hurry it."
- (Chapter 10, stone) "Bring me what you have and I'll put it before the Six myself. Please come back across the lake."
- (Chapter 18, stone) "Come home. I can have you out of Lordaeron before the week is out."
- (Chapter 21, arch) "What do you need from me?"
- (Chapter 23) "My lord magistrate, I ask leave to examine the weapon on Dalaran's behalf."
- (To an obstructive official) "Do give the Lord Regent my regards. I'm sure he is very busy."

## Red flags

- No grand wizard pomp and no fussy wizard comedy.
- His tragedy is belief without speed. Do **not** have him doubt Jaina.
- No lectures to Jaina on the stone. There's no time, and he knows it.
- No slang and no profanity.

## Voice test

Does it sound like a careful old man who has chosen every word, and who loves her and is late? Then it is probably Antonidas.
