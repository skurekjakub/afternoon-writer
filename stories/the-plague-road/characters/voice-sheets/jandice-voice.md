# Voice Sheet: Jandice Barov

*Jaina's peer mirror and guide into Scholomance. A Barov daughter with Kirin Tor training who chose the work. She speaks for the house until the work excites her, and then she speaks for herself. What she wants from Jaina is recognition. Appears in Ch10 (manor, lake, tour, descent), Ch12 (threshold only) and Ch13 (with Sylvanas).*

---

## Canon guardrails

- **Canon anchors:** all of Jandice's WoW lines come from her Mists of Pandaria ghost, plus one non-canon Hearthstone line. They show a hostess who is cruel through courtesy ("careful not to bleed on the rug, please dear?"), taunting questions with a beat before the key word, and a running interest in what is real and what is illusion. Canon makes her a Dalaran-trained illusionist and teacher.
- **Story calibration:** the story's Jandice is alive and Jaina's age. That is an AU choice; canon has no dialogue for a young Jandice. Keep the courtesy-as-cruelty, the teasing question and the teacher's pride. Do **not** port the ghost's cackle, the boss taunts or the nursery rhyme.
- Canon is thin. See `research/voice-canon/jandice.md`.

## Baseline register

- **The guide (default):** polished, hospitable, a little amused. Medium sentences, the old-money ease of someone who has never had to raise her voice. She curates what Jaina sees, and her courtesy is a way of steering.
- **The scholarly break:** when the work comes up, she gets longer, faster and brighter. Clauses pile up, she points at things, she forgets to be a hostess. She is too interested to pretend the work is ordinary.
- **Under threat (Ch13):** the polish drops into insult first and fear after. Short, sharp, aristocratic contempt, then a fast bargain to get out.

## Syntax rules

- Courtesy carries the control: requests and endearments where another person would give an order.
- Taunting questions she already knows the answer to, with a small pause before the word that matters.
- In the break, she corrects herself mid-sentence to point at something better ("no, look at the margin").
- She never names the cost. Bodies, villages and peasants do not appear in her sentences; method, elegance and notation do.

## Forms of address

- **Jaina:** "Lady Proudmoore" in the manor. "Jaina" once the work has her, and that switch is her bid for recognition. "Dear" when she is being kind in a way that means control.
- **Kel'Thuzad:** "my teacher" to Jaina. To his face, "Master."
- **Her father:** "Father," fond and a little dismissive.
- **Sylvanas:** "elf," "your ranger" (to Jaina, absent). To her face, nothing polite.

## Tells

- Reagent-burned fingers on an elegant hand. She shows the hands without hiding them.
- She shows too much. Her pride keeps opening doors she was told to keep shut.
- Her best compliment is "You read it properly."

## Voice samples

### The guide (Ch10)

- "You must forgive Father. He answers Dalaran's letters in his sleep. I'm Jandice."
- "The island is warded, dear. If you want to cross, the house has to know you. It's a small mark. You'll hardly feel it."
- "This is the first-years' hall. They're further along than Dalaran's first years. Please don't tell Dalaran."
- "Is something wrong with the tea? You've hardly touched it."

### The scholarly break (Ch10)

- "Look at this. No, the margin. He wrote the binding as a cipher, in Antonidas's own notation. Do you see how clean it is?"
- "You're the first person who's walked in here and read it properly."
- "Resonance. That's the piece nobody upstairs understands. He'll show you if you ask him. He'll want to."

### With Sylvanas (Ch13)

- "Let go of my arm."
- "She's downstairs with him. He sent me out so he could have her to himself."
- "Days of it. Chalk and tea and nobody else allowed in."
- "The old crypt stair, behind the reading room. Take it and get out of my house."

## Red flags

- No ghost-boss taunts, cackling, rhymes or "Mistress of Illusion" theatre.
- She is not stupid and not a sadist. Her blind spot is selective attention: method in one room, consequence in another.
- Do not let her say what the work costs in people. If she gets close, she changes the subject to the work.
- Do not make her a cartoon rival in Ch13. The jealousy is real, and it sounds enough like proof to fool Sylvanas.

## Canon reference lines

- "Oh, careful not to bleed on the rug, please dear?" (WoW MoP, Scholomance, ghost; later era, trait only). Courtesy as cruelty, the endearment at the end.
- "Feeling a bit... dizzy?" (WoW MoP, Scholomance, ghost; later era, trait only). A question she knows the answer to, with a beat before the key word.
- "What makes something real?" (Hearthstone, *Scholomance Academy*; non-canon, trait only). The illusionist-teacher's question.

## Voice test

Could she say it pouring tea for a guest she intends to keep? When she talks about the work, does she sound younger, faster and happier, and does she leave out every person the work was done to? Then it is Jandice.
