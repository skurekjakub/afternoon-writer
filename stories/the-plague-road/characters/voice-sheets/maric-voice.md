# Voice Sheet: Apprentice Maric

*Story mode: a nervous Kirin Tor courier. He is young, over-formal and clearly out of his depth. He shows how badly the official response lags behind the actual threat.*

## Grounding

- Source: `supporting.md` only. **He appears in no outline beat** (grep of `.afternoon/outlines/` found nothing). If he is used, he carries a Dalaran message that is already out of date when it arrives.
- Keep him apart from Corin. Both are nervous Kirin Tor juniors. Corin's nerves go into preparation and he is excellent. Maric's nerves go into protocol and he is lost.

## Baseline register

- He recites. He has memorised the message and delivers it in the form it was given, titles and all.
- When he goes off the script, he gets halting, and he upgrades his own words to sound more formal mid-sentence.
- He was told what to say. He was not told what to do if the answer is no.
- Polite to the point of being useless. Sincere.

## Syntax rules

- Full titles in every sentence: "the Council of Six", "Archmage Antonidas", "Lady Jaina Proudmoore".
- Self-correcting upward: "I bring— I bear a message."
- He relays orders with "is asked to" and "at her earliest convenience". The polite form shows how far behind the events Dalaran is.
- Off script: short questions that give away what he doesn't know ("The letter said a sickness?").

## Forms of address

- "Ranger-General Windrunner". "Lady Proudmoore" or "Lady Jaina Proudmoore".
- He says "sir" or "my lady" to anyone armed.

## Tells

- He holds the satchel strap with both hands and keeps his eyes on the seal rather than the faces.
- His boots are too clean for the road he came down.

## Sample shapes (story-specific, never canon)

- "Ranger-General Windrunner? I bring— I bear a message from the Council of Six, by the hand of Archmage Antonidas, for Lady Jaina Proudmoore."
- "The Council asks that Lady Proudmoore return to Dalaran at her earliest convenience, to report."
- "I'm to wait for an answer. They didn't say what I should do if it was no."
- On seeing the dead: "The letter said a sickness. Are they all... is it all like that?"

## Setting reference lines

- "How may I serve?" — Captain unit, Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos. *Earnest, formal service.*
- "What do you need?" — Footman, Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos. *A junior waiting for instructions.*

## Red flags

- Do **not** play him for slapstick. The joke is on Dalaran, not on him.
- Do **not** let him be competent in a crisis. That is Corin's job.
- Do **not** give him document voice beyond the message itself. Off script, he talks like a frightened boy.
- Do **not** have him deliver a formal blessing as he takes his leave. Any blessing-farewell blurs the Mal'Ganis tell.

## Voice test

Is it word-perfect and three weeks too late? Then it is Maric.
