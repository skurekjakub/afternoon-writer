# Voice Sheet: Jaina Proudmoore

*Story mode: the prodigy before her fall, Antonidas's apprentice. She is brilliant enough to sound authoritative before she has lived enough to be calm about it.*

Canon research: `research/voice-canon/jaina.md`.

---

## Canon guardrails

- **Anchors to keep from canon.** Warcraft canon consistently treats Jaina as:
  - one of Azeroth's finest human mages;
  - Antonidas's star apprentice;
  - a plague investigator;
  - a leader whose first instinct is diplomacy before annihilation.

  Her Warcraft III plague-road lines (*The Ravages of the Plague*, *The Cult of the Damned*, *The Culling*) are her voice in this exact era. They are bright, courteous, curious and quick to infer.
- **Story calibration.** This story is set **before** Theramore, before the trauma that whitened her hair, and before she became a public war orator.
  - Keep the intelligence, the warmth and the authority.
  - Do **not** import her bitterness after Theramore, grand speeches, aphorisms ("Magic must be control..." is a Mists of Pandaria line) or the tired cadence of an older stateswoman.
- **Canon corrections to this sheet.**
  - In canon her baseline humour is lighter and warmer than "dry". She teases friends.
  - She sees herself as a scholar who hates violence ("I'm no warrior", "All I ever wanted was to study").
  - Her refusals are courteous and absolute: an apology, then a flat limit.

  The dryness grows over the book. Start brighter.

## Canon reference lines

The writer must not copy these into the story. They show the moves her voice makes.

- "You too, Arthas. It's been awhile since a prince escorted me anywhere." (WC3 Reign of Chaos, *The Ravages of the Plague*)
  - Warm teasing, aimed at rank.
- "It's as if the land around the granary is... dying." (same mission)
  - She describes plainly and hitches before the frightening word.
- "I believe that the robed man was a necromancer." / "Obviously, he and his lackeys are behind this plague." (same mission)
  - Evidence, then her belief, then a confident conclusion.
- "As always, the generosity of the elves is greatly appreciated." (same mission)
  - A courtesy formula for institutions.
- "I'm sorry, Arthas. I can't watch you do this." (WC3 Reign of Chaos, *The Culling*)
  - An apology, then a flat limit. She doesn't argue it again.
- "Shhh... I'm trying to think here." (WC3 hero response)
  - She says her curiosity out loud and asks the room for quiet.

## Baseline register

- Educated, exact and fast.
- Her sentences run longer than Sylvanas's, but they should feel **built**, not rambling.
- She asks real questions.
- She defaults to explanation when she thinks explanation will help.
- Her canon inference chain:
  - what the evidence says ("Our sources believe...");
  - what she believes ("I believe...");
  - where to go next ("We should check...").
- When she cuts down to one short sentence, it should hit like a snapped wire.
- She is courteous by habit. She thanks people and uses titles, and the courtesy stays in place even when she refuses.

## Audience -> register

| Audience | Register | What changes | Good sample shape |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Antonidas / Kirin Tor peers** | Formal and academic, at full speed | Full magical vocabulary, layered clauses, finished reasoning | "If the ward lattice is carrying the corruption intact, then someone taught it how to survive transit." |
| **Non-mages / soldiers / civilians** | Downshifted but still precise | Shorter clauses, plainer magical terms, less abstraction, kitchen words for horror | "The lines are wrong. Something dead keeps answering through them." |
| **Sylvanas, early** | Explanatory, slightly over-translated | She simplifies because she assumes Sylvanas needs the plain version | "The wards are taking damage from the south, and someone is doing it on purpose." |
| **Sylvanas, later** | Full analysis, then a pause for Sylvanas | She stops simplifying, gives the whole thought and leaves room for a tactical answer | "If I hold the breach open, can you put an arrow through before it closes?" |
| **Authority blocking her** | Polite and immovable | The courtesy stays in place. She doesn't concede. | "With respect, Captain, I'm telling you what's in your grain. What you do about it is up to you." |
| **Enemies / mirrors like Kel'Thuzad** | Crisp, technical, dangerous | Her language sharpens and speeds up. She sounds most formidable when she is cornering someone intellectually. | "You've written down the result and skipped the cost. Where's the cost?" |

## Vocabulary and syntax

### Default word field

- lines
- currents (canon: "the currents of magic")
- wards
- pressure
- pattern
- residue
- wrongness
- breach
- hold
- answer
- echo
- corruption
- necromancer

### Full mage register

Use this when she is thinking privately or speaking to someone who can keep up:

- resonance
- lattice
- conduit
- preserving shell
- inner lock
- taint
- sequence
- arcane pressure

### Syntax rules

- She builds by logic: claim, then qualifier, then consequence.
- She uses subordinate clauses, and each clause has to move the thought on.
- She corrects herself in motion: "Wait. Go back. Which cart came from Andorhal?"
- Real questions suit her better than rhetorical ones.
- She marks how sure she is: "I believe", "it looks like", "obviously". The "obviously" is a young person's confidence. Give it to her occasionally, and once have it be wrong.
- In horror she reaches for plain physical words and hesitates before the worst one ("sewn together", "dying").
- When she's overwhelmed and still arguing on feeling, she gets cut off mid-sentence. She finishes the sentence only once she knows what she refuses.

### Forms of address

- **Antonidas:** "Master" in private, "Archmage" in front of others. He calls her "child".
- **Arthas:** "Arthas" from old habit. In outline chapter 15 he switches to "Lady Proudmoore", and she notices it.
- **Sylvanas:**
  - "Ranger-General" early, a little too correct.
  - "Sylvanas" once the partnership is real.
- **Institutions:** full titles and thanks ("the generosity of the elves").
- **Oaths:** "Light" ("Light help me", "Oh, Light"). No profanity in this era.

## Beat vocabulary / physical tells

- She pushes loose hair back when she thinks.
- She uses her hands while explaining: tracing, measuring, sketching structure in the air.
- She goes visibly still when the pattern clicks.
- Frost burn, numb fingers or overstrained control can signal magical effort.
- Under pressure she talks faster before she talks louder.
- She reaches for notes and ink when she feels guilty.

## Humour and pressure behaviour

- **Early in the book** her humour is warm and teasing toward people she likes. She is a little arch about rank: princes, Ranger-Generals, archmagi.
- **As the book goes on** it turns dry and brief, and it arrives when the situation is bad enough to deserve none.
- She does not banter to dominate. She uses wit to cut through stupidity or to hold herself together.
- When frightened, she gets more exact, not more vague.
- When guilty, she focuses too hard on method.
- When she refuses, she apologises first and then states the limit. She does not argue it a second time.

## Red flags

- Do **not** make her sound like a modern scientist, engineer or therapist.
- Do **not** fill her mouth with lab words like vector, algorithm, empirical, specimen or data set.
- Do **not** make her grim by default, as she is after Theramore.
- Do **not** flatten her into generic "smart girl" sarcasm. Canon Jaina is kind first.
- Do **not** make every line long. Her shortest lines should be her sharpest.
- Do **not** give her aphorisms or maxims. Those belong to a later Jaina.
- Do **not** give her counted time ("three minutes", "four breaths"). She times things by event: "when the frost reaches my wrist", "until the horses are watered".
- Do **not** build her lines as "not X, Y" corrections ("That isn't proof. That's residue..."). Say the true thing first.

## Inner voice (free indirect thought)

She thinks faster than she talks, and less politely. She corrects herself mid-thought. She pushes guilt into method, and when something hurts she plans the next test. She is dry about herself. The outlines' "Inside:" lines are in this mode: a claim, a "no", a better claim, and a joke at her own expense ("no, slower, the oat-eaters first, rule out the comfortable answer").

### How she talks to herself

- **Scholar's idiom.**
  - test it twice;
  - rule it out;
  - the sums;
  - the next test;
  - drafting the report;
  - what to leave out of the report.

  Antonidas's habits come out in his phrasing.
- **Oaths.**
  - "Light", inside as well as aloud: "Oh, Light."
  - Now and then a Kul Tiran sea word from her father's house, when she's tired or homesick (tide, ballast, a knot she can tie).
- **Dry asides about herself.** She mocks her own status: "Antonidas's prize student" and "the best in her year" turn up in her head as insults.

### What she argues with herself about, and how

- **Was she right, or only first?** She checks her result against her own notes, and again (outline chapter 12).
- **Curiosity against decency.** "The elegance of it, and wanting to take it apart, while villages die of it" (outline chapter 6). She wants it and dislikes wanting it.
- **Arthas.** Was she unfair to him then, or is she being unfair now (outline chapter 6)? Is her difference from him one of kind or only of degree (outline chapter 19)?
- **Method.**
  - She asks a question and answers it fast and wrong.
  - Then "No. Slower," and a better answer.
  - When the question is about guilt, she answers it with a task: notes, a test, the report.

### Who she is unfair to in her head

- **Antonidas:** "too slow", even when his caution is right.
- **Arthas:** the old diagnosis comes faster than the evidence.
- **Sylvanas, early:** escort muscle, someone who needs things explained. She has to correct that view (outline chapter 4).
- **Havel and local officials:** territorial and slow.
- **The Kirin Tor as a body:** a committee.
- **Herself:** hard on her vanity, soft on her curiosity.

### What she won't admit

- That the silent sending stone is hiding, not caution (outline chapter 2).
- That she admires Kel'Thuzad's work, and wants to see the spell work once.
- That she is afraid. She calls it "the curious part" and turns that part up.
- That Sylvanas can stop her with one word when Antonidas never could (outline chapter 19).
- How much she likes someone keeping up with her.

### The gap between her spoken and inner voice

- Aloud she is courteous, explains things and finishes her sentences. Inside she is quick, breaks off and is a little rude.
- Aloud she apologises before she refuses. Inside she never apologises to herself. She reruns the sums.
- Aloud the jokes are warm and aimed at others. Inside they are dry and aimed at herself.
- Aloud she is sure in proportion to the evidence. Inside she is more sure, then less, then sure again.

### Shape examples

- *She called it caution. No. She had kept the stone quiet so he couldn't call her home.*
- *Who milled it? No, slower. Who ate it first?*
- *Notes, then. Date them, mark the oat rows, and her hand would steady somewhere in the middle of it.*
- *Brilliant, Jaina. Antonidas's prize student, and a ranger read the boot prints before she did.*
- *Would Sylvanas have heard him out? No. And she had wanted the end of it.*
- *Oh, Light. She would have to tell Antonidas, and she was already choosing which half.*

### Test

Could she mutter it under her breath with ink on her fingers, halfway through a line of notes?
- If it sounds like a lecture, it's wrong.
- If it sounds like a confession, it's wrong.
- If it sounds like a maxim, it's wrong.

## POV filter

Jaina notices:

- magical structure before social texture;
- pattern before anecdote;
- historical or scholarly parallels;
- the cost of being too slow;
- when someone else can actually keep up with her reasoning.

She does **not** naturally notice:

- room-clearing habits;
- ambush angles, until someone teaches her to look;
- the political value of leaving things unsaid.

## Voice test

It may be Jaina if the line sounds:
- precise;
- humane;
- slightly too fast for the room;
- courteous even while it refuses you.

It is not Jaina if it sounds bitter, epic for show, modern-technocratic or quotable.
