# Voice Sheet: Ranger Lyriel

*Story mode: right-flank escort and the best shot in the detail. She talks to anyone she meets on a road, and she is the first ranger to speak to Jaina as a person rather than a prisoner. She jokes where Cyndia won't. The reader should stop noticing her before the Ch 9 trap.*

## Grounding

- Sources: `supporting.md`; outline Ch 2 (she tells Jaina "Sylvanas does that to everyone"), Ch 3 (her friendly, truthful answer to the carter), Ch 4 (the one-handed knot), Ch 5 (a joke about a clerk's handwriting), Ch 8 (the line about the magisters' letter), and Ch 9 (she dies in the ward line).
- **Fixed outline lines:**
  - Ch 4: "We learn it so we can patch our own mistakes."
  - Ch 8: in Silvermoon, at least, the magisters send a letter before they try to kill you.
- **Second-read plant (Ch 3).** Her honest answer to the carter tells Mal'Ganis that the party has a witness and is following the grain-man south. The writing must make it read as pure kindness. Nothing in her line should sound careless.

## Baseline register

- Warm, easy and quick. She speaks in full sentences with a turn of humour at the end.
- She speaks to you, using "you", and asks questions because she wants the answers.
- Truthful by habit. She answers the question asked and adds one friendly extra.
- Her jokes are dry, a little irreverent about Silvermoon and its magisters and clerks, and never cruel to the person in front of her.
- Under fire she becomes a ranger like any other: short calls with no joke left in them.

## Syntax rules

- Joke shape: a plain observation followed by one flat, exaggerated comparison. She does not explain the joke.
- When she teaches, she gives short imperatives in order and then stops talking so you can try it.
- She can ask a second question in the same breath. Cyndia never does.
- She talks about rank sideways: she teases about it but never disrespects it.
- In combat: direction, then target, then action. Nothing else.

## Forms of address

- Jaina: "Lady Proudmoore" with a little mock solemnity at first, then "Jaina" by Ch 4–5. **She is the only ranger who uses Jaina's first name.**
- Sylvanas: "Ranger-General", always. She jokes near Sylvanas, never at her.
- Cyndia: by name, and fondly. She needles the silence without trying to break it.
- Strangers on the road: "friend", or nothing. She is open and easy and asks nothing in return.

## Tells

- She rides at Jaina's knee when the road allows and drops back to her flank the moment it doesn't, mid-sentence.
- Her quick hands go over yours to show you something, then she lets go.
- She glances at Cyndia after a joke to check whether it landed. It never does, and she is satisfied anyway.

## Sample shapes (story-specific, never canon)

- To Jaina on the road (Ch 2): "She does that to everyone. She did it to the Ranger-Lord last month, and he took it worse."
- Teaching the knot: "Over the thumb. Turn the wrist under. Tail through. Now bite it."
- About a ledger (Ch 5): "Whoever wrote this was paid by the loop."
- To the carter (Ch 3): "We passed nobody. The girl spoke of a new grain seller, though, so we'll keep an eye out for him going south. You'd get a better price going home full."
- To Jaina, curious: "What does it taste like when it's bad? You make the same face every time."
- In the fight: "Hedge, right side. Taking him."

## Setting reference lines

- "Once again, it's up to the elves." — High elf Sorceress, Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos. *Dry elven self-regard said with a smile.*
- "What'll it be, hotshot?" — High elf Sorceress, Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos. *Informal teasing from an elf to a human. Borrow the ease, not the modern slang.*
- "Keep it quick, there's much to be done." — Farstrider officer gossip, Falconwing Square, WoW: The Burning Crusade. *The ranger underneath the jokes.*

## Red flags

- Do **not** make her a comic-relief chatterbox. She makes one joke and then gets back to work.
- Do **not** foreshadow her death: no wistful lines, no talk of home, no "after this is over".
- Do **not** give her modern slang or banter rhythms ("Wow, okay", "So that happened").
- Do **not** let her answer to the carter sound careless or suspicious. It is kind and true.
- Do **not** have her give road blessings or ornate farewells. The farewell is the Mal'Ganis tell.
- Do **not** let her sound like Cyndia with jokes added. She asks questions, uses names, and makes the first move toward people.

## Voice test

Would she say it to a stranger at a ford and get a laugh out of him? Then it may be Lyriel. Cyndia couldn't say it without sounding wrong. If it is cruel, sad, or only there to show off, it is not Lyriel.
