# Voice Sheet: Captain Dagny Ironhull

*Story mode: master of the Wolfheart, a hard-water schooner already working the northern routes. She is a pure pragmatist: she puts a price on danger, handles the seawork cleanly, and watches Jaina the way a captain watches an engine burning too hot. She appears in Ch 19–21. She is a civilian witness to what the pair now carry.*

## Grounding

- Sources: `supporting.md`; outline Ch 19 (she raises the price instead of refusing, the weather clause, she keeps the contract when she hears the truth and recalculates what Jaina can buy with her body, and she wants Jaina on the ice watch at first light), Ch 20 (harbour presence only), Ch 21 (she turns the ship for speed and understands from one look that the run south costs Jaina more).
- The outline is explicit: **no mystical register, and no sailor superstition doing story work.**
- She is "Kul Tiran-style", but the profile does not say where she was born. Canon Kul Tiran dialect ("ye", "yer", "avast") is a later-era register. Keep her trade bluntness and drop the pirate colour.

## Baseline register

- She bargains. Everything has a price, and she says the price out loud.
- Short, declarative, unhurried. A captain who doesn't need to raise her voice on her own deck.
- She talks about the sea and the weather as working conditions: wind, ice, season, hull.
- She does not soften what she says to Jaina. She treats Jaina's power as a cost on the ledger, and that is how she shows respect for it.

## Syntax rules

- Condition, then price: "If X, it costs Y."
- She asks how long and how much, and never why.
- Orders to the crew are terse seawork verbs ("Ease the main." "Hands to the sheets.").
- She does not repeat herself. A clause she has stated stays stated.

## Forms of address

- Jaina: "mage" for working purposes. If the Proudmoore name comes up, she knows exactly whose daughter Jaina is (Lord Admiral Daelin Proudmoore, canon) and does not lower the price for it. That is optional texture and not in the outline.
- Sylvanas: "Ranger", or no title. Rank on land does not command her deck.
- Crew: by job, or by surname.

## Tells

- She looks at the weather and at Jaina in the same glance, as if she is pricing both.
- She watches Jaina's hands during the push the way she would watch a gauge.
- On the turnabout she moves her crew at a clipped pace and doesn't explain herself.

## Sample shapes (story-specific, never canon)

- "Northrend this late is double. Northrend against a wind that's looking for you is more than that."
- "The weather clause stands. If the sea says no, I turn back, and you've still paid."
- "You can drive the wind. How long, and what does it cost you?"
- "Then I'm paying that cost too. If you drop on my deck, I lose the wind and the season with it."
- "Doesn't matter whose wind it is. It matters whether you can beat it."
- To Sylvanas (Ch 19): "I want her on the ice watch at first light. See that she sleeps."

## Setting reference lines

- "For Kul Tiras!" — Proudmoore Guard, WoW: Battle for Azeroth, Tiragarde Sound. *Kul Tiran pride in two words.*
- "Let's test yer mettle!" — Proudmoore Guard, WoW: Battle for Azeroth. *Kul Tiran directness. The "yer" is later-era dialect, so leave it out for her.*
- "Lousy pay, constant danger." — Captain unit, Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos. *A working captain's arithmetic.*

## Red flags

- Do **not** give her pirate talk: no "arr", "matey", "avast", "ye", "yer".
- Do **not** give her sea superstitions, omens, or talk about the sea "wanting" anything.
- Do **not** give her a sailor's blessing at landfall or parting ("May the seas guide you home"). The farewell is the Mal'Ganis tell.
- Do **not** make her soften toward Jaina in words. Any care she shows comes as food, a bunk, or a lower price nobody mentions.
- Do **not** let her ask about the plague, Arthas or the politics. It is not her business, and she knows it.

## Voice test

Could the line go in a ship's contract or be shouted across a wet deck? Then it may be Dagny. If it sounds sentimental, superstitious or piratical, it is not.
