# Voice Sheet: Apprentice Corin

*Story mode: a junior Kirin Tor contact in Andorhal. He is nervous and over-prepared, and he is very good at his job. He knows what the paperwork shows: tainted shipments, copied route marks, and granary records Dalaran ignored. He wants speed, because he knows no one above him is coming. He appears in Ch 7–8 and stays in Andorhal.*

## Grounding

- Sources: `supporting.md`; outline Ch 7 (ink-stained fingers and bruised eyes, ignored notices weighed down with a cracked river stone, fumbling for access seals, voting for speed, earning his place by being right), Ch 8 (going pale as he says aloud that Havel was on cult coin, and saying Kel'Thuzad's name "like it burns").
- He must show that the institution can still produce useful people even while its hierarchy fails.

## Baseline register

- Fast, apologetic at the start of a sentence, exact by the end of it.
- He brings more than you asked for and offers it: copies, a second page, the seal he isn't supposed to have.
- His nerves make him talk more. His competence makes what he says correct.
- When the danger is real, he stops apologising and gets shorter.

## Syntax rules

- He often starts with a false start or a self-correction ("I wrote, well, I've been writing..."), then gives a clean fact.
- He describes paper by what it looks like: the tail of a route mark, a seal pressed off-centre, a clerk's hand. Never "the records indicate".
- He gives the "what" before the "why". He leaves the conclusion to Jaina and is relieved when she reaches it.
- When he is frightened, he drops all the cushioning and says a short sentence once.

## Forms of address

- Jaina: "Lady Proudmoore". He is a little in awe of Antonidas's apprentice, and over-formal at first.
- Sylvanas: "Ranger-General", carefully. He is afraid of getting it wrong.
- Havel: "Captain". He treats him as an equal he does not quite like. He has no proof and assumes the dislike is snobbery.

## Tells

- Ink on his fingers. He spreads his notes flat and weighs them down with whatever is to hand.
- He pats the satchel before he answers, as if checking that the answer is still in there.
- He goes pale before he says the worst thing, and he does say it.

## Sample shapes (story-specific, never canon)

- "I've written to the Citadel about the Brill shipments since the thaw. Every week. I kept copies, if you'd... yes. Here."
- "The route mark is copied. Look at the tail. Our clerks lift the pen, and this one drags it."
- "I can get you into the inspection office. The seal room needs a magister's ring, but the copies are kept next door anyway."
- "Nobody's coming. From Dalaran, I mean. If we wait for them, we'll be waiting till spring."
- On Havel: "His debts were cleared when the suppliers changed. I saw the ledger. I thought he'd had a good year."
- On the name: "Kel'Thuzad." (He gets to the name and says nothing else.)

## Setting reference lines

- "Have you talked to anyone who hasn't started getting sick?" — Stratholme Citizen, WoW: Wrath of the Lich King (Culling of Stratholme, set in the Third War). *A local noticing a pattern in plain words.*
- "I'll do my best." — Captain unit, Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos. *Earnest service from below.*

## Red flags

- Do **not** give him document voice: no "records indicate", "shipment data", "distribution network", "actionable", "vector". He talks about ink, seals and copies.
- Do **not** play his nerves for comedy, or let them make him useless. Every time he stammers, the fact that follows is correct.
- Do **not** let him explain the magic to Jaina. He brings her the paper, and she reads it.
- Do **not** make him resemble Maric. Maric's nerves come out as protocol. Corin's come out as preparation.
- Do **not** give him road blessings or ornate farewells. The farewell is the Mal'Ganis tell.

## Voice test

He stumbles at the start of the sentence and lands it at the end, with a paper in his hand to back it up. That is Corin. If he stays vague, sounds like a report, or is only funny, it is not.
