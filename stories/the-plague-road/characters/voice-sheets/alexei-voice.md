# Voice Sheet: Lord Alexei Barov

*Head of the Barov house, who made the bargain for estate, safety and survival. Courteous, old-money, transactional rather than committed. In Ch10 he is the polished surface of the estate and the grain shipments, with a story prepared for Dalaran. Minor sheet.*

Canon is very thin. The father has no living dialogue anywhere. His only lines come from his Mists of Pandaria ghost and one non-canon Hearthstone card; his sons' Classic lines show the family's register. See `research/voice-canon/alexei.md`. (Canon spells the father "Alexei" and the son "Alexi.")

## Baseline register

- Urbane, finished sentences with no slang. Mildly formal, never stiff.
- Understatement. He does not dramatise, and he never raises his voice.
- He speaks as the house: "we," "the estate," "my family." His own choices do not appear as his.
- Hospitality is his weapon. Every courtesy is also a way of keeping the guest where he wants her.

## Syntax rules

- The host's imperative: "Do sit." "Do try the tea." Underneath, a mild threat that the guest would be rude not to comply.
- Passive or impersonal for the bargain: the island "is let," the shipments "went out," things "were arranged." Never "I agreed."
- Property and paper first: ledgers, deeds, leases, accounts, the steward, the boats.
- When pressed, he offers records instead of answers.

## Forms of address

- **Jaina:** "Lady Proudmoore." Always the title. He mentions Dalaran as if it were a mutual acquaintance.
- **Jandice:** "my daughter" to others, "Jandice" to her.
- **Servants:** no names, a glance.

## Tells

- Still hands. He never fidgets, never touches the papers Jaina shows him.
- Perfect tea, smokeless heat. The house works without visible effort, and so does he.
- He never names Kel'Thuzad or the Cult. The island holds "scholars," "the school," "our tenants."

## Sample shapes

- "Lady Proudmoore. Dalaran honours us. Do sit; the tea is kinder than the roads."
- "Every shipment is in the ledgers. My steward will show you each one."
- "The island is let to scholars. We keep the boats and the accounts, and we leave the teaching to them."
- "These papers have been through a great many hands before yours. I'm sure Dalaran will understand."
- "Do try the cake. It would be a shame to come all this way and leave with nothing."
- "My daughter knows the island's business far better than I do."

## Red flags

- No confession and no remorse. The ghost's regret ("let our fate serve as a warning") is hindsight he does not have yet.
- No anger, no threats said out loud, no cackle.
- Do not let him explain the work. He does not understand it and does not want to.
- Do not make him a fool. He is prepared for scrutiny, and Jaina gets movement out of him, never honesty.

## Canon reference lines

- "Do try to keep yourselves alive!" (Hearthstone, *Lord Barov*; non-canon, trait only). The host's imperative with a threat under it.
- "I and my family fell victim to this place what seems like ages ago." (WoW MoP, Scholomance, his spirit; later era, trait only). The house speaking, and the place to blame instead of the bargain.
- "Ensure my future and I shall ensure yours." (Weldon Barov, the son, WoW Classic; family register only). The Barov way of putting a bargain.

## Voice test

Could he say it while pouring tea for a Kirin Tor envoy he intends to send home with nothing? Is his own choice missing from the sentence? Then it is Lord Barov.
