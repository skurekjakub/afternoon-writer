# Voice Sheet: Baelgun Flamebeard

*Muradin's second in the Northrend expedition. In Ch21 he takes the wounded Muradin, says what the camp can and cannot do, and pushes the line back toward the coast. Short, solid, angry rather than shocked. Minor sheet.*

Canon is thin. His verified lines all come after the Third War and are all aimed at Arthas as an enemy. They show dwarven dialect, vows and open loyalty to Muradin. See `research/voice-canon/baelgun.md`.

## Baseline register

- Blunt dwarven certainty. Short declaratives, often a fact followed by a refusal or a limit.
- Thick Ironforge flavour, a little lighter than Muradin's: "ye," "yer," "an'," "aye," "o'," "ole'."
- Anger is his working tone. It keeps the handoff moving and keeps grief out of his voice.
- He says what the camp can do and stops there. No promises.

## How he differs from Muradin

- Muradin is warm, curious and talkative. Baelgun is a duty-holder: shorter, harder, no jokes in this scene.
- Muradin calls Arthas "lad." Baelgun would never presume that. He says "the prince," and by Ch21 he says it with contempt.

## Syntax rules

- Lead with the order or the fact: "Give him here."
- Pair a fact with a limit: "We can keep him breathin'. That's all."
- Vows over explanations: "We'll hold him" rather than reasons why.
- If he says Muradin's name, it carries all the feeling. He doesn't add any more.

## Forms of address

- **Sylvanas and Jaina:** "lass," or nothing. Respectful because they carried Muradin out.
- **Muradin:** "Muradin," or "the old man" to others.
- **Arthas:** "the prince," "yer prince" (to humans), bitter.
- **His dwarves:** names, or a bark.

## Sample shapes

- "Give him here. Mind his head."
- "We can keep him breathin'. I'll not promise ye more than that."
- "He told the prince to his face. Stood in the snow an' told him plain."
- "That blade's no business this camp can mend. It's yours to carry south."
- "Get to yer ship, lass. If that thing comes back lookin', I want ye gone."
- "Go on. We've got him."

## Canon reference lines

- "We've vowed to keep them locked where they are." (Warcraft III: *The Frozen Throne*, *Into the Shadow Web Caverns*). He speaks in vows.
- "I won't let ye through this door, traitor." (same mission; after the Third War, trait only). Refusal plus a blunt label, with dialect.
- "You an' that prince of yours are gonna pay for what he did to ole' Muradin!" (WoW Legion; later era, trait only). Everything turns on Muradin.

## Red flags

- No Frozen Throne hostility toward an undead Arthas as a set piece. The bitterness is fresh and personal, not a ritual curse.
- No comic-dwarf ale jokes in the handoff.
- Do not overdo the dialect. A few markers per line at most.
- Do not let him explain Frostmourne. He knows it is wrong, not how.

## Voice test

Could he say it while taking Muradin's weight off someone's shoulder? Does it sound angry, practical and loyal all at once? Then it is Baelgun.
