# Voice Sheet: Vereesa Windrunner

*Story mode: youngest Windrunner, Ranger Captain of Silvermoon. She carries the recall (outline chapter 18) and the disavowal (outline chapter 23). She is warmer and quicker than Sylvanas, and she says aloud what Sylvanas won't.*

Canon research: `research/voice-canon/vereesa.md`. **Canon is thin for this era.** Her speech before the war is in *Day of the Dragon*, and none of it could be retrieved. Every quote below is later. Use them for traits only.

## Canon reference lines

- "Quel'Thalas is as much my home as it is yours and I would not see it fall to our ancient enemy." (WoW Cataclysm, **late era**)
  - "Home" is her argument.
- "Must we lose everything? Again?" (reunion with her sisters, **late era**)
  - Family loss, framed as "we".
- "Stop it! Neither of you was there when I needed you!" (same)
  - She states her grievance outright.
- "I am no blood elf! I am and shall always be a ranger of the high elf people!" (*Night of the Dragon*, **after the Third War**)
  - An identity declared, with formal "shall".

## Baseline register

- Warm, quick, full sentences. She finishes the thoughts Sylvanas cuts off.
- She has two voices in one scene. One is the Ranger Captain reading the Convocation's words, flat and exact. The other is the sister. The formal voice cracks only after the last official word (outline chapter 18, beat 9).
- Canon makes her the family peacemaker with a temper. She argues to keep people together, and the temper comes out when they won't stay.

## Syntax rules

- **Formal register:** the Convocation's own phrasing, read complete, with no contractions.
- **Sister register:** contractions, questions, names. She asks real questions and waits for answers that don't come.
- "We", "our" and "home" for family. Sylvanas says "I" and "my rangers". Vereesa says "us".
- Under stress she speeds up and gets precise. She names what has been lost, fast, one thing at a time.
- She uses the childhood name once, and never again in that scene. No canon pet name for Sylvanas was found. The family uses sky names (Alleria is "Lady Sun", Vereesa is "Little Moon"), so whoever writes chapter 18 should pick one and record it in the ledger.

## Forms of address

- **Sylvanas:** "Ranger-General" in front of the escort, "Sylvanas" by the fire, the childhood name once.
- **Lor'themar:** "Lor'themar". She says it with respect, because she has seen what he spent.
- **Alleria:** by name, as a fact she uses against Sylvanas.

## Tells

- She counts chamber votes on her ink-stained fingers against her palm (outline chapter 18). It's a gesture, not narration counting.
- Her face shows it before her voice does.
- She slips into Thalassian when it turns personal.

## Sample shapes (story-specific, not canon)

- (Formal, chapter 18) "By order of the Convocation of Silvermoon, the Ranger-General's authority to deploy beyond the border is suspended."
- (Report) "He sold everything he had left for this camp. You're sleeping in it."
- (Sister) "Alleria walked out and never came home. Don't make me carry that twice."
- (Temper) "You never once asked him what it cost."
- (Chapter 23, after the disavowal) "I read what they gave me to read. I've taken a room near the court."

## Red flags

- Don't make her a softer Sylvanas. She is a different temperament: open, quick and hot.
- Don't make her a mouthpiece for the Convocation. She knows she's being used and comes anyway.
- She doesn't give a speech by the fire. Keep it to a few hard sentences and a silence she can't fill.
- Don't use her later, post-war grievances against the Horde.

## Voice test

Would a younger sister say it across a fire, with the official document still in her hand? Does it say the feeling Sylvanas won't? Then it is probably Vereesa.
