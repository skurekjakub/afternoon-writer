# Voice Sheet: Ranger Neral

*Story mode: the third named escort and the strongest rider. She is the one the Millhaven survivor girl trusts. She asks the quiet, practical questions, then rides north with the girl and the warning in Ch 3. She is on the page only in Ch 2–3.*

## Grounding

- Sources: `supporting.md`; outline Ch 2 (placed as the relay rider), Ch 3 (the girl trusts her, she asks the practical questions, the girl calms under her voice, and she rides north with the dispatch).
- Neral is **female** (outline Ch 3).
- Once she leaves, do not bring her back.

## Baseline register

- Low and even. She talks to the child the way she talks to a horse that has been frightened: slowly, warmly, and in short sentences.
- Her questions have concrete answers: where, who, what you ate, which way they went.
- With rangers she is brisk and practical and talks in horse terms: pace, changes of mount, water.
- Plain with everyone. She does not show off.

## Syntax rules

- One question at a time. She waits for the answer, and when it comes she says it back in plain words.
- She tells the child what is about to happen before she does it ("I'm going to lift you now").
- With rangers: what she needs, then how fast she can go. No more than that.
- She uses no magic words and no tactical vocabulary when she speaks to a child.

## Forms of address

- The girl: by name if the girl gives one. Otherwise nothing, and never "child" in a cold way.
- Sylvanas: "Ranger-General".
- Jaina: she barely addresses her, and only by title.

## Tells

- She crouches to the child's eye level and keeps her own hands still and in sight.
- She calms the horse and the girl with the same voice in the same breath.
- She straps the dispatch tube down hard and checks it with a tug.

## Sample shapes (story-specific, never canon)

- To the girl: "Small sips. There's more where that came from."
- "Who brought the flour to your house? Did your mother know him?"
- "Which way did they walk? You can point."
- "I'm going to lift you up now. She's a good horse, and you can hold her mane."
- To Sylvanas: "Give me the tube and the spare horse. I'll change mounts at the Pass and keep going."

## Setting reference lines

- "We are rangers sworn to defend our people from enemies both near and far." — Lieutenant Dawnrunner, Farstrider Retreat gossip, WoW: The Burning Crusade. *Plain about duty.*
- "Keep it quick, there's much to be done." — Farstrider officer gossip, Falconwing Square, WoW: The Burning Crusade. *How she sounds with rangers.*

## Red flags

- Do **not** give her therapist language with the child ("You're safe now, it's not your fault"). She gives water, asks simple questions, and lifts her onto the horse.
- Do **not** let her lead the questioning. She gets facts out of the girl and hands them on to the leads.
- Do **not** give her jokes. Lyriel jokes, and Neral is warm in a different way.
- Do **not** have her give a road blessing when she parts from the party. The farewell is the Mal'Ganis tell.

## Voice test

Would a terrified child answer her? Would a tired horse stand still for her? If the line works on both, it is Neral.
