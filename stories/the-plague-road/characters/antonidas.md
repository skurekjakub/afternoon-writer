# Antonidas

- Role: Jaina's mentor, Archmage, the voice on the stone.
- Core: the best of the Kirin Tor and still too slow. He believes Jaina from the start. That is what makes the failure hurt.

## Look
- Seventy-one, thin, white-haired, long beard, scholar's posture, administrator's hands stained by ink instead of reagents.
- Dalaran violet worn as office, not costume.

## Voice
- Measured, paternal, precise.
- Complete thoughts, careful qualifiers, silences that say more than the words.
- Calls Jaina "child" when the father in him slips past the Archmage.

## What matters
- He trained Jaina to trust her mind and then could not move the institution fast enough to back her.
- He trained Kel'Thuzad and failed to stop him before procedure ran out of usefulness.
- He mirrors Lor'themar: one good man inside a system built to delay.

## Stone progression
- Ch 2: no contact. Jaina keeps her paired stone silent so he cannot order her home. He has only the note she left.
- Ch 9: first contact. He makes Kirin Tor suspicion formal.
- Ch 10: caution, delay, and procedure when Jaina reaches him from Scholomance.
- Ch 14: Jaina gives him the Lich King chain and the Legion threat but not the room. He tells her to come home, and she refuses.
- Ch 18: last real plea before Northrend. He offers extraction after Stratholme.
- Ch 18: Jaina tells him she is going north and closes the line herself.
- Ch 19: the stone is dead.
- Ch 21: the stone wakes once the Wolfheart is clear of the ice, and Jaina warns him. At Southshore he confirms Arthas is in Capital City. He arrives in Capital City in person with two Kirin Tor magi and meets the pair at the palace margin.
- Ch 23: he stands in the tribunal gallery, argues, asks for time, and still cannot save her.

## Carry-forward
- Keep him warm but never safe.
- His tragedy is not disbelief. It is belief without speed.
- Book 2 inherits two things from him: the coming death and the question of whether the Kirin Tor can be worth saving after failing this badly.
