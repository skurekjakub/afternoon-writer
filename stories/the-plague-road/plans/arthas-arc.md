# The Arthas Arc — Chapters 15–18

- Tone: humor dies by Ch 16. Stratholme kills whatever is left of the lighter register.
- Partnership track: damaged trust -> mutual rescue -> shared witness and shared guilt -> welded bond.
- Carry-in: Sylvanas set Arthas moving in Ch 11. The Scholomance crack is still live until Stratholme burns it shut.

## Ch 15 — The Prince
- POV: Sylvanas.
- Smoking edge of Hearthglen aftermath; Uther is healing beside scorched tent poles. Arthas is already running hot.
- Uther tells them Arthas ordered the fever tents burned with the living sick inside, and Uther stood in the tent mouth until he took it back.
- Sylvanas gives ground truth; Jaina gives mechanism. They brief as one unit.
- Uther clocks the partnership at once.
- Arthas calls her "Lady Proudmoore", hears the actionable part, and overcorrects toward speed.
- Sylvanas realizes the certainty she aimed in Ch 11 is becoming dangerous.
- Cyndia hears the fourth farewell from a quartermaster's aide nobody can name. Sylvanas orders a record: where, when, what face.

## Ch 16 — Before the Gates
- POV: Jaina.
- Jaina, Sylvanas, and Cyndia ride ahead to spoil the source before the city tips.
- Jaina spends Kel'Thuzad's knowledge on strangers at once: granaries, bakehouses, routes, stores — including a hostile east granary where infected workers turn under a cult handler's whistle.
- The tactic works locally and fails strategically. Stratholme is already too far gone.
- Sylvanas starts to answer Arthas first. Jaina speaks over her, alone: "Not cleanly. Not now." She does not lie to buy time.

## Ch 17 — The Gate
- POV: Sylvanas.
- Arthas answers with the Culling. Uther breaks with him on the spot and cannot stop him this time.
- Sylvanas can see both truths at once: the move is militarily coherent and still atrocity.
- This is where the chain she started by recruiting Arthas becomes visible in full. Each woman knows her own hand in it. Neither says so.
- Sylvanas hears the fifth farewell herself from a grain carter, turns to follow him, and chooses the wall as the city breaks.
- End beat: Arthas leaves changed and sees S&J watching.

## Ch 18 — The Wall
- POV: Jaina on the wall and road; Sylvanas at the field camp.
- Tightened wall sequence: Jaina freezes from comprehension, not fear; Sylvanas gets her moving with a grounding shoulder-touch and direction, not comfort.
- Stratholme welds shut the Scholomance crack. After this Sylvanas stops assessing Jaina and starts counting on her.
- Jaina's hands are still gray with flour; his came out clean. She told Kel'Thuzad Arthas would have listened if she had stayed. Today she was at his side, and he listened.
- Parallel institutional severance at the road and field camp: Jaina gives Antonidas the Lich King/Legion warning on the road, refuses extraction, and closes the line that night; Sylvanas defies Vereesa's recall in silence and orders `"North."`
