# The Partnership Arc — Sylvanas x Jaina

- Slow-burn romantic tension and unspoken devotion built through disciplined tactical intimacy, physical awareness, earned trust, and the habit of survival.
- Zero melodrama, zero speeches about feelings, zero cheesy romance tropes: proximity, touch, denied jealousy/possession, and choosing to stay in prison for each other carry the pull inside the thriller pressure.
- Arc line: threat -> asset -> partner -> necessary -> unspoken devotion that outlasts every institution.
- Track it through changed behavior, naming, timing, physical awareness, and who covers whose blind spots.

## Phase 1 — Threat / Contained Asset (Ch 1-4)
### Sylvanas on Jaina
- Ch 1: security breach with a famous Kirin Tor name, in worn road gear.
- Ch 2: brilliant, condescending, poor road instincts, still useful.
- Ch 3-4: academic recklessness remains true, but Jaina turns magical chaos into usable intelligence faster than anyone else on the board.
- Marker: Sylvanas thinks "the mage," never "Jaina."

### Jaina on Sylvanas
- Hard soldier. Cold custody. Little patience for theory.
- She keeps translating herself downward and misses how much Sylvanas already sees.
- Ch 2 road fight is the first time she sees the shape Sylvanas gives a fight instead of brute force.
- Ch 3-4 teaches her that Sylvanas reaches some of the same conclusions through a discipline Jaina never took seriously.

### First cracks
- Sylvanas catches the register shift and says nothing.
- Deer attack makes Jaina necessary in the field.
- Millhaven makes Sylvanas's tracking and Jaina's magical read feel like halves of the same job.
- Sick-village logistics force their first real collaborative solve, and it is on time: the village lives.

## Phase 2 — Grudging Respect & First Pull (Ch 5-8)
- The plague only makes sense when their methods are combined.
- Ch 5: the midnight map read narrows the physical distance between them for the first time — heads close over routes and manifests, sharp awareness neither names — and Sylvanas quietly starts thinking "Jaina."
- Jaina stops simplifying and starts waiting for Sylvanas's tactical answer because the analysis feels unfinished without it.
- Andorhal is the first phase where they divide work by aptitude without arguing about control.
- Kel'Thuzad's name turns respect into dependence: each woman has only half the picture alone.

## Phase 3 — Tests of Faith & Intimate Fracture (Ch 9-14)
### The mistakes and closeness
- Ch 9: Sylvanas reads a real patrol seam and calls the move before Jaina's ward read is done. Lyriel dies in the trap. Sylvanas takes a serious wound, and Jaina binds it using Lyriel's one-handed knot — close, steady hands against skin and blood, the first charged caretaking neither comments on.
- Ch 11: right again and alone, Sylvanas gives Arthas a war he knows how to want.
- Jaina's matching solo act is Ch 16: she answers Arthas alone, over Sylvanas.
- Neither apologizes. Sylvanas changes behavior: she waits for the read.

### The split
- Jaina follows the Barov trail to Scholomance.
- Sylvanas follows the Arthas trail on the torn Hearthglen dispatch, genuine intelligence the cult lost.
- Separation proves the partner is not helpful extra weight. The partner is structural.

### The crack
- Kel'Thuzad is Jaina's dark mirror: same brilliance, same rigor, no ethical anchor.
- Ch 13: Sylvanas arrives after the worst internal moment and only sees Jaina apparently listening. The misread stings like a personal betrayal, sharper than pure tactical caution can explain.
- Ch 14: From that point on, trust is still present but no longer effortless. In the overrun Eastweald ford/bridge road clash, Sylvanas's fractional post-Scholomance hesitation nearly draws blood; Jaina catches the delay and feels it land.
- Jaina keeps the price of the room from Sylvanas and answers her one direct question at the stream stop with a true, partial answer.

## Phase 4 — Saving Each Other & Parallel Severance (Ch 15-18)
- The saves stop being speeches and become embedded habits.
- Ch 15-16: Sylvanas sees Jaina spend dangerous knowledge to save strangers immediately, which matters more than anything argued in Scholomance.
- Ch 17-18: Stratholme welds the crack shut. Shared witness and shared guilt replace unfinished explanations. Each knows her own hand in it; neither says so.
- Ch 18: on the wall, Sylvanas anchors Jaina with a shoulder-touch and direction rather than comfort; at the camp, both women sever their home institutions in parallel (Sylvanas defying Vereesa's recall, Jaina closing Antonidas's stone) and choose each other and the mission.
- After the wall, Sylvanas stops evaluating Jaina and starts tracking her automatically as part of the combat picture.

## Phase 5 — Competence Recognition & Unspoken Devotion (Ch 19-21)
- Northrend strips away almost all friction.
- Ch 19: at sea, the ship forces quiet. At the mast Jaina confesses what she paid Kel'Thuzad; Sylvanas asks one practical question, does not absolve her, and later pulls her back from wind-burn into warm waterskin and cabin closeness where neither moves away.
- They communicate in shorthand, split work without discussion, and move like people who have been operating together for years.
- Ch 20: Muradin notices what they are to each other before either of them names it. The Frostmourne retreat proves the team was right even when the fight was unwinnable.
- Ch 21: in Capital City, Sylvanas claims her place beside Jaina with a single word (`"I."`) and swallows a flash of denied jealousy over Antonidas at the kitchen service arch.

## Phase 6 — Peak Competence & Condemnation Together (Ch 22-24)
- Ch 22: Sylvanas takes the direct fight, takes a real wound, and holds it. Jaina gets frost and arcane through the seam and has no fire left. Unplanned, she closes her hand over Sylvanas's arrowhead — burning her palm to pour the last fire into the steel — and Sylvanas's arrow carries it through the seam. The kill is the spell.
- Ch 23: in the tribunal, Sylvanas holds Jaina's gaze across the court without flinching even as Kel'Thuzad's journal is read aloud; afterward, when Lor'themar opens one door for her alone, she refuses the solo pardon without argument.
- Ch 24: reduced to adjoining cells, the partnership holds down to two voices and foreheads pressed to the same dividing stone. Every institution failed them; the bond they built themselves did not.
- Final note: the qualities Sylvanas first filed under "dangerous mage" are exactly what save her life — and the woman she refused to leave behind is the only loyalty left standing.

## Tracking cues
- Naming change: "the mage" -> "Jaina."
- Register change: Jaina stops explaining downward.
- Habit change: Sylvanas waits for the read (Ch 9 trap, paid off in the Ch 20 cave).
- Physical and emotional truth: devotion and slow-burn attraction are shown in proximity, touch, denied possessiveness, and irreversible choices — never in speeches about feelings.
