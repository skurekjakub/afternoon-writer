# The Causal Chain — Sylvanas and Arthas

- Use this as the note that Sylvanas recruits the weapon that later destroys a city.
- It is guilt she never speaks, carried as the weight of causation: the right briefing still helped light the worst possible fire.

## How Sylvanas reads him
- She reads commanders, not lovers.
- What she tracks: decision tempo, use of expert input, situational awareness, and sustainability.
- His pause between assessment and action shrinks every time she sees him.
- Her own pause widens over the same span because Jaina taught her to wait for what she cannot see alone.

## The chain
- Ch 6: Arthas is a useful but mis-aimed military asset.
- Ch 11: Sylvanas briefs him with everything but Kel'Thuzad's name and Jaina's position, because she needs institutional power on the plague. She gives him a war he knows how to want.
- Ch 15: she sees what that speed has become at Hearthglen, where Uther stopped him burning the fever tents with the sick inside, and knows the tempo is now wrong.
- Ch 15-16: Jaina spending plague knowledge to save real people starts repairing the Scholomance crack.
- Ch 17: Stratholme is the detonation. Arthas no longer needs anyone else's judgment once he has the answer he wants. From the wall Sylvanas knows whose war he is running.
- Ch 22: Sylvanas closes the same chain with the arrow that carries Jaina's fire.

## Thematic note
- Crossing the border is still the right decision.
- The cost is that engagement means causing outcomes, not just witnessing them.
- Sylvanas is right at every step and still helps produce catastrophe.
