# The Endgame Arc — Chapters 18–24

- Tone: stripped thriller. No playful register left.
- Partnership track: competence recognition -> peak competence -> survives condemnation.

## Ch 18 — The Wall (camp half; wall half in arthas-arc.md)
- Uther brings word Arthas sails north. Vereesa brings the recall to the field camp; Sylvanas refuses it by silence. Jaina refuses Antonidas twice and closes the line.
- Vereesa shows what the political war has done to Lor'themar.
- Both women choose the mission over institution. Sylvanas's order: "North."

## Ch 19 — The Voyage
- The ship forces the quiet they have been dodging.
- Talk duty, obedience, and what happens when the protector becomes the threat.
- Jaina confesses the three Kel'Thuzad trades. Sylvanas asks one practical question and does not absolve her.
- Jaina drives the ship with wind magic. Sylvanas stops her from burning herself out.

## Ch 20 — Frostmourne
- Northrend: burned fleet, Muradin, cave too late.
- The fight proves their sync even in defeat. Sylvanas holds a shot for Jaina's read.
- They retreat clean with Muradin because they read the same instant to disengage.

## Ch 21 — The Return
- Dual POV.
- Jaina finally gets a workable read on how to hit the blade.
- Every warning route fails, Antonidas included. Arthas is already home and already being celebrated.
- They plan the room: Jaina needs a few seconds; Sylvanas will buy them. Nobody plans the arrow.

## Ch 22 — The Throne Room
- Peak sync through the service passages.
- They arrive as Arthas kills Terenas.
- First arrow only pulls his attention; the blade has carried him past ordinary death.
- Frost and arcane breach the seam. Jaina has no fire left and puts it into Sylvanas's arrowhead. The arrow carries it through and kills him.
- Frostmourne remains active. Guards enter to the worst possible picture.

## Ch 23 — The Condemned
- Evidence looks perfect for guilt and useless for truth.
- Uther moves the court. Ashcombe reads Kel'Thuzad's journal of Jaina's words, and the room turns.
- Lor'themar's three hundred in the square buy the commutation to indefinite imprisonment.
- Verdict: condemned. Sylvanas refuses the one door, her own.

## Ch 24 — The Stinger
- In cells, the partnership reduces to two voices and holds.
- Jaina packages the counter through the wall, addressed to Sylvanas. No spoken thesis.
- Mal'Ganis passes through toward the vault, speaks the farewell in Kaldorei, and Sylvanas sees five faces as one.
- Exit state: Arthas dead, blade gone from the vault, real enemy still moving.
