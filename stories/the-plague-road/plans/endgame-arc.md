# The Endgame Arc — Chapters 18–24

- Tone: stripped thriller. No playful register left.
- Partnership track: competence recognition -> peak competence -> survives condemnation.

## Ch 18 — The Wall (camp half; wall half in arthas-arc.md)
- Uther brings word Arthas sails north. Vereesa brings the recall to the field camp; Sylvanas refuses it by silence. Jaina refuses Antonidas twice and closes the line.
- Vereesa shows what the political war has done to Lor'themar.
- Both women choose the mission over institution. Sylvanas's order: "North."

## Ch 19 — The Voyage
- Tightened Seastone Harbor departure; the ship then forces the quiet they have been dodging.
- Talk duty, obedience, and what happens when the protector becomes the threat.
- Heightened slow-burn mast confession: Jaina confesses the three Kel'Thuzad trades; Sylvanas asks one practical question and does not absolve her.
- Jaina drives the ship against Lich King headwinds until wind-burn nearly breaks her; Sylvanas pulls her back into warm waterskin and cabin closeness where neither moves away.

## Ch 20 — Frostmourne
- Northrend: Arthas has burned **all** his ships at Daggercap Bay (no reserve vessel left behind). Muradin's camp, cave too late.
- The fight proves their sync even in defeat. Sylvanas holds a shot for Jaina's read.
- A cavern ice-collapse forces/earns Arthas's departure deeper through the northern glacier pass while Sylvanas and Jaina retreat clean with Muradin because they read the same instant to disengage.

## Ch 21 — The Return
- Dual POV; tightened 11-beat return.
- Jaina finally gets a workable read on how to hit the blade.
- Arthas reached a hidden dead-crewed vessel at a northern ice-fjord and sailed straight home before they even reached Daggercap Bay, giving him a four-day lead into Capital City.
- At Southshore Jaina chooses to wake the sending stone (Antonidas does not yet know where Arthas is); a Farstrider relay runner on the King's Road brings word of Arthas's homecoming and celebration.
- Every official warning route fails. Antonidas comes in person with two magi and meets them at the kitchen service arch (`"You came."`), where Sylvanas claims her place beside Jaina (`"I."`) and swallows a flash of denied jealousy.
- They plan the room: Jaina needs a few seconds; Sylvanas will buy them. Nobody plans the arrow.

## Ch 22 — The Throne Room
- Peak sync through the service passages; the third ranger is planted at the outer service landing (and slips away when arrests begin to carry word to Lor'themar).
- They arrive as Arthas kills Terenas.
- First arrow aims for Arthas's sword-wrist/hand to pull him off the king; the blade has carried him past ordinary death.
- Frost and arcane breach the seam. Jaina has no fire left and closes her hand over Sylvanas's arrowhead, burning her palm to pour the last fire into the steel. The arrow carries it through and kills him.
- Frostmourne remains active. Guards enter to the worst possible picture.

## Ch 23 — The Condemned
- Evidence looks perfect for guilt and useless for truth; every fact in Ashcombe's prosecution is grounded in real records and testimony (Marsh's arrest report, Hearthglen/Stratholme officer accounts, seized Kel'Thuzad journals/grimoire, Vereesa's disavowal).
- Brask's sworn letter is undisputed and ruled irrelevant; Antonidas is refused the stand; Uther moves the court. Ashcombe answers by reading Kel'Thuzad's journal of Jaina's three admissions aloud, and the room turns.
- Sylvanas holds Jaina's gaze across the court without flinching. Ser Edric Valecrest is seeded once by name on palace guard rotation.
- Lor'themar's three hundred arrive in the square during the trial before the verdict, buying commutation from death to indefinite imprisonment. Afterward he opens one solo door for Sylvanas alone; she refuses it.

## Ch 24 — The Stinger
- In adjoining cells, the partnership reduces to two voices and foreheads pressed against the same dividing stone — and holds.
- Jaina feels Ser Edric Valecrest's mind catching on Frostmourne's pull from the vault and packages the counter through the wall, addressed to Sylvanas. No spoken thesis.
- Mal'Ganis passes through toward the vault, eyes going past Sylvanas toward his work, and speaks the farewell in ancient Kaldorei (*Anu kal dela ana'diel.*); Sylvanas recognizes five faces as one.
- Exit state: Arthas dead, blade gone from the vault before dawn, real enemy still moving.
