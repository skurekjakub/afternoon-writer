# The Kirin Tor Thread

- Mirror to the Silvermoon thread: Antonidas and Lor'themar are both right, both late, and both trapped inside institutions built to slow crises down.
- Use this as the Antonidas/Council state tracker, not as a second story.

## Arc I — Investigation
- Ch 2: no call. Antonidas has only Jaina's note; she keeps the sending stone silent so he cannot order her home. The Council has not grasped the crisis.
- Ch 4-6: offstage, plague reports move through Dalaran at committee speed. Antonidas starts quiet consultations while the Council treats the whole thing as an academic problem.
- Ch 7: Corin's granary reports went to Dalaran and got no answer. The institution is slow, not worthless.
- Ch 8: Jaina names Kel'Thuzad, the mage Antonidas expelled. The plague now carries Kirin Tor fingerprints as well as Scourge ones.

## Arc II — Scholomance
- Offstage: the Council shocks, reviews, drafts, and delays.
- Ch 9: first stone contact. Antonidas makes Kirin Tor suspicion formal, and backing narrows.
- Ch 10: inside Scholomance, Jaina keeps finding Kirin Tor method turned inside out: notation, books, structure, handwriting, Jandice's citation habits. Her stone call gets caution and procedure, and she descends anyway.
- Ch 12: no call. Antonidas is present only through notation and Jaina's first price to Kel'Thuzad.
- Ch 13-17: no call reaches the road. The field is always chapters ahead of the Council.

## Arc III — Arthas
- Ch 15-17: offstage, Antonidas wins monitoring and consultation, not intervention. Corroboration piles up while the Council keeps debating scope.
- Ch 17: Stratholme damages the cautious faction but does not make them fast.
- Ch 18, on the road: Antonidas offers extraction and asks her home. She gives him the Lich King and the Legion, the first time Dalaran hears them, keeps back the price, and answers "Alive. Not yet."

## Arc IV — Endgame
- Ch 18, that night: Jaina tells Antonidas she is going north and closes the Dalaran line herself. This is the emotional severance.
- Ch 21: at Southshore she chooses to wake the stone. He comes to Capital City in person with two magi, asks what she needs, and gets "You came."
- Ch 22: the Kirin Tor cell holds a side lane at the palace margins and misses the chamber. Real help, nowhere near enough.
- Ch 23: he is refused the stand and hears Jaina's words about him read from Kel'Thuzad's journal. This is his wall, same as Lor'themar's.
- Ch 24: he remains in the city as guilty institutional pressure, not rescue. Book 2 starts with him diminished by public impotence.

## Tone line
- Start: mentor's calm.
- Middle: stripped-down urgency.
- End: presence without power.
