# The Scholomance Arc — Chapters 9–14

- Mid-book pivot: split the pair, let each flaw run without the other's counterweight, then bring them back together damaged.
- Partnership track: tests of faith -> crack -> damaged reformation.
- Guardrail: Ch 10 = entry and choice, Ch 11 = external interrupt, Ch 12 = the trade, Ch 13 = damage event, Ch 14 = road out.

## Ch 9 — The Trap
- POV: Sylvanas.
- Jaina gets spell fragments and Barov papers in the short window Sylvanas gives her.
- Sylvanas sees a real patrol seam and calls the move before Jaina's ward read is done. The ward kills Lyriel and wounds Sylvanas.
- Cyndia catches the courier: a false Tyr's Hand lead, a fatal fall, a torn Hearthglen dispatch, and the farewell secondhand.
- New habit is born here: Sylvanas waits for the read.
- Split routes emerge: Jaina takes Barov and Scholomance. Sylvanas takes Arthas on the dispatch.

## Ch 10 — The Estate
- POV: Jaina.
- Jaina bluffs Kirin Tor authority she does not hold to get into Barov lands, then accepts Jandice's guest ward to cross to Scholomance.
- The place reads like Dalaran method turned rotten: libraries, wards, lecture rooms, research discipline, necromancy in the bones.
- Antonidas gives her caution by stone. She chooses to go deeper alone instead of leaving with partial proof.

## Ch 11 — The Fuse
- POV: Sylvanas.
- Operating without Jaina feels tactically wrong from the first step.
- Sylvanas briefs Arthas with everything but Kel'Thuzad's name and Jaina's position, and lights the rest of his arc by aiming state power at the plague.
- He believes her too fast.
- Once Sylvanas learns Jaina went inside Caer Darrow, she pivots toward Scholomance.

## Ch 12 — The Necromancer
- POV: Jaina.
- Kel'Thuzad does not threaten first; he teaches, and he sells each piece of the spell for one true thing about Jaina.
- She pays three times: Antonidas, Arthas and the end of their romance, and how happy she was at his table. He writes each answer in his journal.
- Jaina follows the logic far enough to see why it could tempt someone like her. That is the real danger beat.
- He gives her the final theory: Lich King, Legion, plague architecture, fidelity of preservation.

## Ch 13 — The Arrival
- POV: Sylvanas.
- Sylvanas infiltrates the fortress alone and reads it like hostile terrain.
- She finds Jaina apparently engaged with Kel'Thuzad and arrives after the internal fight has already happened.
- She reads the surface exactly and the truth incompletely. That is the crack.
- They still kill Kel'Thuzad together in seconds, which makes the damage worse, not better. Jaina carries out his journals.

## Ch 14 — The Revelation
- POV: Jaina.
- Jaina briefs the new intelligence clinically: Lich King, Legion, scale, grain already out.
- She keeps back the price. Asked once how she got him to talk, she gives a true, partial answer, and Sylvanas does not press.
- Sylvanas's trust now carries a fractional delay. Jaina notices.
- Road pressure returns immediately: Hearthglen barely held and Arthas is marching on Stratholme.

## Key notes
- Mal'Ganis lays the Ch 9 ward and the courier's false lead. The Hearthglen dispatch is genuine; Sylvanas's choice to arm Arthas is her own.
- Jaina alone with Kel'Thuzad is the three-way mirror at full strength.
- Sylvanas's Arthas briefing is correct and catastrophic.
- The arc must feel sharp, not smeared: entry, trade, damage, exit.
