# The No-Look Handoff

One character holds something out without looking, and the other takes it without a word. It is stock shorthand for wordless trust between people who work together. Opus reaches for it in almost every draft with a partnership in it.

**Hard limit:** one per book, and only when it is earned.

---

### 1.
**Bad:**
Without looking, she held her bow out to the side. Cyndia took it.

**Good:**
She gave Cyndia her bow.

**Why:** Plain is fine. The trust is shown elsewhere.

### 2.
**Bad:**
He reached back blindly, and the reins were already in his hand.

**Good:**
"Reins," he said. Tam passed them up.

**Why:** A word is more particular than a stock gesture.

### 3.
**Bad:**
She didn't have to ask. The knife was in her palm before she finished turning.

**Good:**
She turned for the knife, and Jaina handed it over, handle first and still warm from the fire.

**Why:** If the handoff matters, give it a detail that belongs to these two people.
