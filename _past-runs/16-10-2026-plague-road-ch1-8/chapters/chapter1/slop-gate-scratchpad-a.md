## intent-smear-agency-laundering-guide.md (17 keeps)

**KEEP** | condition 3 literal non-human (animal)
> The jays had forgotten her, quarrelling again in the branches overhead.
Animals going back to normal behaviour. The birds are the real agents.

**KEEP** | condition 3 literal
> The smell reached her before she stood, troll blood, rank and coppery, cutting through the resin
Smells really do travel and mask each other. Physics.

"Caught itself" is how you'd describe a stumbling rhythm. The stone's beat really does recover and slip. That is the chapter's subject.

**KEEP** | simile, no agency transfer / subject-matter defense

An explicit "as though" simile drawn from her horse idiom. The stone gets no intent, judgment or command.

**KEEP** | condition 3 literal (camouflage note: "the road led" is fine)

The stone is the physical source of the cold she felt through her palm. The main clause uses the approved state form ("The cold was still there"). This is the one kept instance of "cold". L77 and L209 were killed under the repeat-noun rule.

**KEEP** | condition 3 literal

**KEEP** | P8 exception (involuntary)

**KEEP** | condition 3 literal (magic mechanism, subject-matter defense)

This is the damper and crystal behaving as described in the world. No human verb.

**KEEP** | condition 3 literal

The lamp is the real cause of the shadows. "Dug" is a physical verb, not intent.

**KEEP** | static body position

"Lay" carries no volition, decision or knowledge.

**KEEP** | P8 exception (involuntary expression)

The point is that the frown is involuntary (she hears something in the stone). Sylvanas is reading an unguarded tell.

**KEEP** | actor stays subject
> In her palm she remembered moss, a warm face of stone, a beat that came late and then late again.
Sylvanas is the subject who remembers. The palm locates the memory and doesn't demote her.

**KEEP** | subject-matter defense / condition 3
> The whole ring could be limping by then.
"Limp" is Sylvanas's established word for the stone's uneven beat (L65, L109, L181). She has no magister's word, and the chapter makes that a plot point at L73. No human agent is being laundered; the verb names the ring's actual irregular rhythm.

**KEEP** | literal (document stating text)

A report "says" what is written in it. That is ordinary, literal usage.

**KEEP** | P11 exclusion (physical allowing with object and result)

Matches "She let the dog out." A real animal, a real result.

**KEEP** | P11 exclusion (person allowing person)

One person permitting another person's action. This is not a feeling or a silence taking the verb.
