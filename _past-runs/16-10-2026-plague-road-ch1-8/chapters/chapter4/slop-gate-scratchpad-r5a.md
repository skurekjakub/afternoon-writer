# Slop-Gate Scratchpad - chapter4 / pass a (iteration 5)

Target: v2-r5.md
Stage: standard
False-positive filter: the POV is Jaina, a Kirin Tor mage and Kul Tiran admiral's daughter. Her diagnostic thinking runs as self-argument ("So it wasn't the water. It might still be the water"). The subject matter is the necromantic "drag" she senses through the staff, wards that hold without curing, and a built construct with "one part to wait". These are literal in-world mechanisms.
