- Excerpt: "...ran at the gate, barking, hackles up. A rowan stood at the gate, and in the wooden Light shrine nailed to the gatepost..."
- Defense: positional anchoring, not a label. All three nouns name one locus: the dog's target, where the rowan stands, and the post the shrine is nailed to. Repeating "gate" stages the space (dog charging at the gate, rowan beside it, shrine on its post) and adds no new information categories. The phrase "A rowan stood at the gate" was already in v2g.md, and iteration 0's own suggested fix kept it. Killing it now would ping-pong against the previous gate's fix. It is not comparable to the hawthorn echo, which repeated a descriptive label without any positional work.
- The paragraph's rhythm still holds: dog, rowan/shrine, woman, door shut. The new clause is one sentence and does not stall the beat sequence.
