# The contamination has left the lines

In Ch3 it is in Millhaven's well water, soil, footprints and cellar, and south of the village it is 'wider... a stain in a cloth that's been wet through'. The horses that drank from the culvert stream remain clean. Mechanism unknown.

Chapter 4: in the village the pump jug reads thin (a little in the water); the answer is flour. The contamination moves through people and bread; a built, concentrated form sits in a clay jar.
