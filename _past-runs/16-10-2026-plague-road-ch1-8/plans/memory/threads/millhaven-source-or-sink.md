# Millhaven: source or sink

The deep read at the crossing came back mixed: all four lines heavy, two heavier far out than under the crossing, the north lines late by distance, the east not lagging, the west drowned out. 'I don't know.' The storehouse reads loudest but Jaina will not call it the origin. At noon the next day the sickness reads wider and thicker down the road than Millhaven was. Still open.

Chapter 4: in the second village's granary the staff pulls hard. Jaina remembers the Millhaven storehouse and thinks 'Here was where it ended up.' Leans toward sink, not confirmed.

Chapter 5: Millhaven took its first short-rate load at midsummer, a fortnight after any other road's first; the mage's marks are blackest there but the stain is older and heavier farther down the south road, with a new layer on top. 'It started there, or it started there first. That's not the same.' Leans further toward sink; unresolved.
