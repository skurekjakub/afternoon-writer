# Jaina's fear: a Dalaran-trained author

The plague is built the way Dalaran teaches a ward, every seam closed, and it uses the death-currents craft kept behind the library grille. Jaina runs down the faces of masters, senior students and the ones who left and never wrote, stops at one, and makes herself look at the road. She does not name it, to the reader or to herself. She calls it study, wants to see the keeping part work once, and is sick over the saddle. She tells no one. Open; no name in Ch6.

Chapter 7: Corin's weekly letters to the Citadel inspection desk and the Archmage's secretary went unanswered; Jaina's fingertips go white on the staff at his 'No.' She says nothing; Sylvanas makes nothing of it on the page.
