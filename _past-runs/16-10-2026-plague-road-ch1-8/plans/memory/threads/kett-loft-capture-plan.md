# The Kett's loft capture plan

Decided at the militia post minutes before Chapter 8. 'Tonight.' Sylvanas wants one living man out of Kett's upper loft before the shift ends, while the crew still think the city is theirs. Jaina: the loft is wet tonight and she can still read it there; she will freeze one man's jaw before he can bite his collar, fingers on his skin, once tonight. Cyndia takes the roofs over the back lane by the chimney across from the loft and does not loose until Sylvanas calls; Lyriel holds the bend in Tenter Row so the party comes out past her; Havel's lads hold the Hurdle Lane end; Corin comes (he knows the kind of lock on the loft door) and stays behind Proudmoore. Unknown: whether the window man saw Sylvanas, how fast the cell scatters, what is in the crock.
