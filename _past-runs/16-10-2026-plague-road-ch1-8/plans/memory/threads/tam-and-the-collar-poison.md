# The lookout and the collar poison

Tam, the hoist hand, is caught in the loft at the hole under the floorboard with a jar and a lamp. He says only 'I'm not to speak to you... You don't know what you're keeping them from.' In the cage he goes calm, foams, arches and dies in minutes: poison in a fold of waxed paper sewn into his collar hem, his chin on it the whole way down the lane. Sylvanas: 'Somebody taught him that. They sewed it in for him and told him when.' His gut reads clean; he never ate the flour. Brask: 'He ate at my table.' Who 'them' are, and what Tam was told he was protecting, are unanswered.

Chapter 7: Sylvanas sees Tam's trained waiting in the eight at Fenner's. She and Jaina plan around a sewn-in poison: Jaina offers to freeze a man's jaw before he can bite, once tonight, her fingers on his skin. Ch8 tests it.
