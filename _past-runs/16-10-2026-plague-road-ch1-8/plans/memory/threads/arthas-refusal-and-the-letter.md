# Arthas heard the warning and chose the orc war

At Strahnbrad Arthas listens to Jaina all the way through, believes her, and does not act. His reasons are concrete: the Garrow farm burned yesterday, four dead he helped carry out, a band out of the camps with axes, men whose families are in the next farms. 'I can see that from my tent door. I can't see your grain.' He sends her away with a task (write it down, he will send it to his father tonight with his own rider) and a promise (he will be in Andorhal when the hills are quiet). He never takes the paper. Sylvanas called the whole exchange before Jaina finished.
