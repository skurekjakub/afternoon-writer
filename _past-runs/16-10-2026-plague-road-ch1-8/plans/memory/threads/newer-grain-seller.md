# The newer grain seller

Heard twice, connected by no one: the girl (a cheaper man than Mister Hollis, caused a quarrel, called at her house) and the carter (a young fellow with a grain cart, a new man, whom he wants to see). Sylvanas files it as village gossip. Lyriel tells the carter the party will watch for him going south. No grain-carrier claim anywhere.

Chapter 4 connects it: a young civil man with a cart, three months, the west road, cheaper by two coppers on the measure, is the supplier of the flour that carries the plague (see young-man-with-the-cart). Millhaven's 'cheaper than Mister Hollis' and this keeper's new man are one. The carter is not mentioned.

Chapter 5: the cart man shrinks from a quarry to one carrier among many (31 to 53 places). A miller past Coldwell says a young fellow came back yesterday to buy back his last four sacks at a loss; a Wick ostler saw a free carter leave south before light. No face; no link stated between those two and Millhaven's man.
