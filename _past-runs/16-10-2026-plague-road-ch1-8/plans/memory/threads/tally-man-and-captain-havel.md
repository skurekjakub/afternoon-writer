# The tally-man and Captain Havel

On the afternoon road into Andorhal Sylvanas stops a man walking out of the city with a ledger satchel and asks who runs the granaries. He answers with a wry list of rival authorities and sends them to Captain Havel of the militia at the post just inside the market gate: keep straight on at the bottom of the hill, the post is on the left with a bench outside. Havel knows the granary district better than anybody, 'and they'd sooner not hear it'. He glances back toward the granaries as he talks, wishes Jaina 'May the stars keep your road', and is gone in the traffic. Sylvanas finds the farewell wrong (the second time); Jaina read nothing off him. Chapter 7 opens at the market gate.
