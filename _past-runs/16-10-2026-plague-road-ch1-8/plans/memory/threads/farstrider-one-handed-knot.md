# Lyriel's one-handed knot

Jaina cannot tie a bandage with one hand. Lyriel puts her hands over hers: loop over the thumb, wrist under, tail through, set it with your teeth. 'Go on, Jaina. Bite it. It won't bite you back.' It is the first 'Jaina'. Every Farstrider learns it in the first year, left hand until it works, then the right, 'so we can patch our own mistakes'. Jaina swears in gunner's slang, pulls it out and tries again; ties the cooper's boy's; cannot yet do it on her own hand ('Tomorrow'); at dawn ties Garran's alone. Chapter-4 locked fact: she can tie the knot.
