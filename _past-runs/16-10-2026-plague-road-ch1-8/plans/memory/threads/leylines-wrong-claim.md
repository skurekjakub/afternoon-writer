# Jaina's claim: the leylines are wrong

Jaina says it is necromantic residue in the leylines, moving north through the ward-stones. Sylvanas tests it. The road, the fields and the doe prove the contamination is physical and out of the lines; Sylvanas shows from the map that the lines all converge at Millhaven, which sends Jaina back to ask whether Millhaven is the source or the sink.
