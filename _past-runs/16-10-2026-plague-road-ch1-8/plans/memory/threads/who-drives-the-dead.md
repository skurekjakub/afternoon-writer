# Who or what moves the dead together?

Sylvanas looks for a bellwether in the cut and finds none: no apart-walker, no print ahead of the first. 'Who drives sixty dead up a cow way in the dark?' Open. No cult proof, no commander, no ranks. The rope loop on the oak gate looks like a drover's habit.

Chapter 4: the organised hand shows. A built stack of spells, a supplier with a cart, a lookout with a poison sewn into his collar ('They sewed it in for him and told him when'). Tam: 'You don't know what you're keeping them from.' Who drives the walking dead is still open.

Chapter 5: the organised hand has yards, books, boys, sealed paper, a day's lead at every door, an overseer told what to burn. Still no cult or name; still unconnected on page to the walking dead.
