# The Barov name on the paper

In the cold ash of the river depot's stove Sylvanas and Cyndia lay out burned waybill halves, parchment and broken red seals. Every readable slip has Andorhal at one end; thirty-one carry Barov ('Barov stores', 'for Barov's factor', 'his lordship Barov'); the biggest parchment has one word whole, 'Barov', above a melted shield seal. Jaina gives the background: an old house at the King's court that holds the land and, she thinks, most of the granaries round Andorhal.

Sylvanas wants it to be him ('Sun's sake, she wanted it') and does not let it be. The granary lord's name is on every sack, sound or rotten; the overseer burned every line, not only the lordly ones. Status: open, connection not guilt. Do not resolve in Chapter 6.

## Chapter 6
Arthas draws a Barov slip half out and reads the seal ('That's Barov.'). Jaina: 'It's on every sack in the south, good or bad. It's his granaries. I'm saying nothing about Lord Barov, only that the grain goes through Andorhal.' Arthas jokes that Barov would sue him and talk for an hour first. Connection only; no accusation. Still open.
