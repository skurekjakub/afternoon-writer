# The south road, the army and Jaina's unchecked read

Four sources point down the same road: Jaina's contamination read (heavy, old at the bottom, a thin new layer going the same way), the road talk of Arthas's army at Strahnbrad, the toll-keeper's King's foot cutting across for the hill road, and Cyndia's iron-tyred army wagons going up full and coming down empty. The King's road is straight, drained and clean. Sylvanas stakes four horses on the south road, cannot check the read, and refuses a re-read ('No.'). She warns that the army will outrank flour; Jaina: 'Then I'll have to show it to them.' The chapter ends with a brown, moving smear low over the hills ahead. Open; Chapter 6 picks it up.

## Chapter 6: resolved
The brown smear is army dust with smoke rising into it. The Strahnbrad camp fills the slope under the cut. Jaina brings the plague to Arthas and he believes her and goes back to the orc war; Sylvanas called it before Jaina finished. Jaina: 'You were right. At the fork.' Sylvanas: 'He's a good commander... The other one is ours.' Consequence thread: arthas-refusal-and-the-letter.
