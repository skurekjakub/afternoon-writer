# Jaina's camp memory of Arthas

'Out of the camps' sends Jaina to one night years ago: the dare, the fern, the fence, the female orc humming to the infant, Arthas as sure after as before. She asks herself whether she has been unfair to him for years, notes that the band that burned the Garrow farm came out of the same camps, and answers 'No.' She does not tell Sylvanas, who does not ask.
