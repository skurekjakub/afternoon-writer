# The ward-stones read wrong

Ch3 turns it toward the border: the cow way points at the long empty stretch of ring between the Pass and the eastern posts, patrolled once a fortnight by a warden with a lens. Sylvanas's dispatch asks for the wardens to be doubled and eyes put on the high ground. She still carries the noon stone's cold in her palm. Elves still cannot feel the contamination; only horses and the mage tell her.

## Chapter 6
Next rung of the horse ladder, on the walled approach to Andorhal: Sylvanas's horse stops dead and scrapes her boot on the wall; Cyndia's half turns, ears flat; Jaina's mare backs and sidesteps toward the ditch. At the ring stones they did it once and stood; these keep shying and go on crabwise, whites showing. Sylvanas and Cyndia calm them by voice. The rangers feel nothing themselves.
