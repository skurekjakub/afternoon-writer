# Millhaven's dead walked toward the ring

At least sixty, four and five abreast, up the cow way north-northeast toward the ring between the Pass and the eastern posts, a day or more ahead. Confirmed by the girl, the lane prints, and the cut (Cyndia's switch count and the mage's heavy-or-light sorting). The party did not cross them. The warning goes north by Neral. Count is a floor. No commander shown.
