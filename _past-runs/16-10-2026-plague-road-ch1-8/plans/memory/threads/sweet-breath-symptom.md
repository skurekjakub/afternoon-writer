# Sweet breath before fever

The girl's mam: breath sweet like pears gone brown, before she was hot; they had no pears. Jaina confirmed 'before'. Sylvanas thinks drunks and dying men have sweet breath too. A reported symptom only.

Chapter 4: seen in the chapel: sweet in the early and middle cases, 'meat kept too long' in the late case (Garran). The same pears-gone-brown comes off the broken jar in the loft, with a struck-flint sharpness under it.
