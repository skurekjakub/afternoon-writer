# Jaina's deep reads cost her

Escalated in Ch3. At the well the left hand goes under, frost forms a white plate, and Sylvanas tears it free, leaving skin on the stones. At the storehouse the frost runs further. Right hand still dead. By night both hands are in linen; Sylvanas has banned hand-reading. The mage now reads with the staff heel and looks green and grey after each hard listen.

Chapter 4: no hand-reading, but the cost follows every spell. Wards chill her chest and teeth; the frost cast on Tam and the shell over the spill tear the left palm low in the heel (an overripe plum, Lyriel's knife through the linen); the arm is ice to the shoulder. Fingers curl halfway; two tips are usable for the knot. Right hand still dead.

## Chapter 6
Crystal like straw in the rain; head aching; no hand-reading. On the walled Andorhal approach the drag lands like diving deep: ears hurting, teeth chattering in the heat, frost across the bare fingertips and creeping over the linen, the crystal sinking until she sees the grain of the stone, and her first nosebleed (the linen comes away red with a hen-feather stuck to it). Lyriel offers the wall; Jaina says no and rides on.

Chapter 7: a day of sample-room reads costs her a grey face, a dead staff crystal, frost on the linen on a warm night and blots when she tries to write (Corin writes at her dictation). She offers a once-tonight jaw freeze that needs her fingers on a living man's skin. Hands remain unhealed.
