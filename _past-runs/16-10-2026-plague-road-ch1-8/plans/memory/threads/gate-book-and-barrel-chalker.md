# The gate book and the barrel chalker

Two counts of who comes in. The clerk at the market gate writes Jaina's name, 'Escort, Farstriders, three', and Sylvanas's name into a book left open on a table 'where anyone with a reason could turn back a page and find it'. A man in a brown coat on a barrel at the head of the granary lane chalks a mark for every wagon that turns down it; he looks up at the bows and chalks again, and Sylvanas cannot see whether the mark is for them. She walks on without looking back. A factor's count, the Exchange's, or somebody's. Open; keep ambiguous.
