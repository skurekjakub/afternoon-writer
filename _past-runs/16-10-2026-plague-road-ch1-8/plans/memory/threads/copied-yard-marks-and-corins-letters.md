# Copied yard marks and Corin's letters

Corin's second copy of every bill shows Fenner's hook drawn from the bottom with a bead of ink at the foot, where the real clerk starts at the top; on the day billed, Fenner's own book has both south wagons in the shed with a cracked axle. Forty-one bills since midsummer on Fenner's, the Exchange and Garside's marks, mostly south, some on the weir and east roads. Some villages on copied marks have written in sick; the rest have not yet.

Jaina's sample-room reading matches: every bag on a copied mark heavy, every bag on a true mark clean. Corin wrote weekly to the Citadel inspection desk and then the Archmage's secretary and got no answer. Havel: 'I can't arrest a village.' Kett's bills are written for the first carts out. Open: who copies the marks.
