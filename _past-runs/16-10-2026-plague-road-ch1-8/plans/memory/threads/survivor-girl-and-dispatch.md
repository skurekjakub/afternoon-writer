# The survivor girl and the dispatch

Neral rides north on the bay with the girl in front of her, the sealed tube strapped behind her leg, and the fourth ranger leading the spare grey. The message: Millhaven dead, sixty-plus north-northeast toward the ring, double the wardens, the Pass road south clear, a child witness coming, 'I am going on south.' Sylvanas keeps four south. Fate of the dispatch is open.

Chapter 4: the girl's testimony does work for the party at a distance (Sylvanas uses her words to turn Brask; Jaina uses her sweet-breath answer to read the chapel). Two empty places at the back of the line; Jaina turns to speak to Neral and she is not there.
