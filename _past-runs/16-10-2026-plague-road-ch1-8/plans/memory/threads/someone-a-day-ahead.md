# Someone is a day ahead

The miller says the young cart man came back yesterday to buy back four sacks at a loss. The Wick ostler says a free carter left south before light. The river depot is swept and its stove crammed before the party arrives, and the overseer fled the day after. Sylvanas, riding the dark wood, decides it is either bad luck or a hunter being read ('somebody keeping a day ahead of her, shutting doors, or behind her reading the road as she was reading it and sending word on'). She cannot name who: 'Anyone.' Open; the mechanism is not shown.

Chapter 7: at the gate the party's names go into an open book and a barrel chalker keeps a second count; Sylvanas cannot tell a tired clerk from a paid one. At the table she wants the capture tonight partly because she is sick of arriving late. A masked man at Kett's window looks toward her roof for a long time; whether she was seen is open.
