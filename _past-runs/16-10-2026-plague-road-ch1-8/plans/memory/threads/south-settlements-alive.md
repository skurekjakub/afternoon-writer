# The settlements south are alive

The carter says the south is busy: market at the ford, smoke in every chimney, barley up a copper. The mage says it is 'ahead of us... down the road', wider and spread out. A living farm at the end of Ch3 stares at the bows and shuts the door. The staff points past it.

Chapter 4: the next village is alive and misunderstanding. A market, a hammer, bread smoke, three houses shuttered, a chapel full of 'fever'. It is saved in one night by a ranger's timing and a mage's slate: the flour is burned. Brask says everything in the south goes through Andorhal.

## Chapter 6
The hub itself is alive: Andorhal, seen from the rise, with hoists taking sacks, wagons in lines, ovens baking and a man singing a rude song. Jaina braced for Millhaven and finds it working. 'People would eat that bread tonight.'
