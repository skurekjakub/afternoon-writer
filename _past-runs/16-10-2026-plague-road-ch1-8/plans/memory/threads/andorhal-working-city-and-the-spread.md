# Andorhal is working

The city is not a ruin. Hoists take sacks, wagons queue at the gates, ovens bake, a man sings a rude song, and under it all the drag beats so hard Jaina feels it in her teeth. Whoever keeps it running is inside and knows which wagon goes to which yard. The party enters by the back gate, at the market gate, asking for Captain Havel. Open; Chapter 7 starts at the gate.

Chapter 7: the hand behind the spread shows itself in the granary district: eight sentries at Fenner's (one a runner using a wall-crack dead drop), five masked contaminators at Kett's loft, three more going up Hurdle Lane at the bell. Grain leaves on other yards' copied marks. Who sits above them, and the factor whose name is on the contracts, remain unknown.
