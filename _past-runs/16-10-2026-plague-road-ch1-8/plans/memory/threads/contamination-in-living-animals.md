# The contamination is active in living flesh

Ch3 pays the horse re-check (clean, to the mage's puzzlement), puts the sickness in the prints of the walking dead, and adds sweet breath as a reported symptom. Sylvanas compares the walking dead to the doe and forbids herself the equation. The barn cat is plainly ordinary. No grain, no mechanism.

Chapter 4: it is in living people before death and goes in by the mouth. Early cases: a fist or less under the breastbone and a thread along the gut. Late (Garran): liver, lungs, vessels, arms, every thread back to the gut. Tam, who guarded but never ate it, is clean. Entry route is now known (the flour); what makes the dead rise is not.
