# How the sickness enters the body (resolved, chapter 4)

It is swallowed. In every new case the brown knot sits under the breastbone and along the gut; in the late case it runs outward from there. Jaina rules out water (one well, three houses, worst to best), contact (the worst case lives alone) and grain in general (oats are grain) with a slate and a handful of questions, and lands on one flour: bread, dose by dose. Mother Haw eats her own oats and is well. Stopping the bread breaks Elsie's fever by dawn.

Still open: what turns the sick into the walking dead, and how it came to be in Millhaven's water and the doe.
