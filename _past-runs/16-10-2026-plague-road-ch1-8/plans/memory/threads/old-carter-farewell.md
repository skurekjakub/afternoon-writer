# The old carter's farewell

'May the stars keep your road.' Sylvanas: not Light, not 'Safe road'; some hill saying. 'Leave it.' Cyndia turns her head after the wagon and holds it a moment. The carter's eyes went once along the line of horses. He also said Millhaven was 'well enough' on market day, which is not reconciled with 'five days dead'. Observable-only plant.

Chapter 5: at the fork a wagon-board bench packed with grain dust brings the carter back to Sylvanas: the empty plank-sided wagon, 'Nothing for me here, then', that he did not turn at the dead mill but drove on north, and that he asked after the young man with the cart. 'An empty wagon, going the wrong way.' She tries him beside the depot and the cart man; he fits neither, 'except that he had asked after him.' Filed unmatched.

## Chapter 6: the second time
A granary tally-man on the Andorhal road sends the party to Captain Havel and then, as Jaina passes, says 'May the stars keep your road.' Sylvanas turns right round in the saddle, then asks Jaina, 'Did you read anything off him?' Jaina: 'Nothing but a tired clerk.' By the time they look back, no one in the road wears a pale strap across his coat. 'The blessing. I had the same one at Millhaven, from an old carter going north over the bridge.' Jaina: country people have dozens of farewells. Sylvanas: 'Once, it's a farewell. Twice, it's wrong. That's as far as it goes yet.' Cyndia and Lyriel are silent on the page. The man looks nothing like the carter. Status: advanced, open; Cyndia's Ch3 turn of the head is held for Ch9.
