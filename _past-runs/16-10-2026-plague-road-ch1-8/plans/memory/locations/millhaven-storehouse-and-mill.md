# Millhaven storehouse and mill

A stone storehouse with a slate roof and a cart-wide door on the near side of the bridge, beside the mill. Inside: grain dust like a slow gold fog under the lantern, sacks stacked shoulder high and chalk-tallied along the seam, a bin of loose grain with the wooden scoop standing up in it, a tally board on a nail with the last line half-written. 'Somebody had been filling sacks.'

It is the loudest place the mage finds in Millhaven. She will not call it the source; she tells Sylvanas a store is where things end up, and that if she said it started here and it came from a day's ride off, they would ride the wrong way. Sylvanas has expected the mage to seize on it, and does not conclude 'grain'. The prose never names grain as carrier in Ch3. (Chapter 4 recasts the read: in the sick village's granary Jaina recalls 'the storehouse by the bridge, the loudest place in that whole dead village' and thinks 'Here was where it ended up.' It is still not confirmed that Millhaven's grain came from the same new man; the girl's 'newer grain seller, cheaper than Mister Hollis' is the only link.) The frost runs further here than at the well and the mage leaves more skin on the boards.
