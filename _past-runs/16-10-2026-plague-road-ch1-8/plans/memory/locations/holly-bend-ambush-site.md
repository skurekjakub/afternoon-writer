# Holly bend (ambush site)

Where the left hedge gives out and an oak-and-ash wood with thick holly comes down to the road. The staff hums, Windrunner stops, and deer and wolves in the same track burst out together toward the riders with milk-filmed eyes. Dead after: two does, a stag and at least two wolves. One doe (Windrunner's arrow through the heart, then another in the neck) would not go down until Cyndia put an arrow through the skull.

Jaina read the second doe in the ditch: contamination through blood, meat and organs, liver worst, predating the arrows. Windrunner cut the belly open with her own knife. Cyndia's tracks show nothing was chasing anything. The party then rode hard for Millhaven.
