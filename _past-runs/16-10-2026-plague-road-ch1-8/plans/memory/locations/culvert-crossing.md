# Culvert crossing

First real Lordaeron hedgerow country below the hills: hawthorn and bramble, haws dark red, a rutted road of mud edges and packed earth, straw trodden into the ruts, a stream out of a low wet wood passing under the road through a stone culvert. Dawn, sky the colour of dishwater.

Windrunner halts the party here for Jaina to put a hand on the road. Jaina goes down through the crust, the clay, the water, the hedge roots and the line far below, and finds the contamination has left the lines and is in everything, a little, like salt. Frost fans a hand's width round her palm. The rangers ring her, facing out, while she is gone; Windrunner calls three times.
