# Granary and loft

A long, blind fieldstone building next to the bakehouse with a hoist beam over a high door at cart-bed height. The yard-end doors are barred from outside, no lock ('Who would steal a granary?'); a low side door behind a water butt swings in on leather hinges, unlatched. Inside: dark, grain dust, mouse, old sacking, the sweetness, and a plank stair without a rail up to a loft where a lamp throws a yellow wash on the rafters.

The loft: a pale clean new stack of flour sacks to the roof-tree, one slumped and spilling, a floorboard up, a post, and a clay jar the size of a baby's head with waxed cloth over its mouth. Flour dust hangs lit gold in the air (Jaina's memory of the Boralus quay mill explosion). The jar breaks and the dark substance runs along the grain of the boards toward the stack below; Jaina clamps a blue shell over it. The sacking is stencilled with a sheaf of wheat in a ring and, underneath, ANDORHAL.

By night the yard before it is full of fire; by dawn the heap is black, steaming, flattened by rain, and still being forked.
