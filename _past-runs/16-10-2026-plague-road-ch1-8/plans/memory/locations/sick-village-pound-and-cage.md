# The pound and the cage

At the foot of Chapel Lane: a ring of drystone wall with a ewe in it, and against the wall a little round fieldstone house 'no bigger than a bread oven' with a stone dome roof and a door of iron bars. A stone bench along the back, straw on the floor. Brask finds the key by feel, 'as he must have done a hundred times for a drunk on a fair night'; the door opens with a scream.

Tam is locked in, sits with his hands between his knees, foam on his lip, arches and dies on the straw within minutes. Sylvanas kneels with her fingers in his mouth; Jaina's face is against the bars without her knowing when she put it there; Brask says 'Cover his face, somebody'; Lyriel tucks a spare cloak round his head.
