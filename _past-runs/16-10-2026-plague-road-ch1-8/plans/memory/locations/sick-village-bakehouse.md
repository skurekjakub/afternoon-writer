# Bakehouse

A low stone building with a chimney and a long brick oven against its end, next to the granary. By day it breathes the brown bread smoke that lies over the whole village; by dusk the oven is raked out but the brick still gives off a dry warmth smelling of scorched crust. It takes all its flour from the granary. Hob and Nan light the oven by cockcrow; Ellen carries loaves out under a cloth. After Brask's shutdown the chimney stands up 'black and cold against the glow' for the first time all day and Hob stands next door with a fork, watching the heap smoke.
