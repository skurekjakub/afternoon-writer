# Silvermoon and Sunfury Spire

Not shown on page in chapter 1; exists only as what Sylvanas is avoiding. The Convocation chamber at Sunfury Spire is where she must sit in formal greens and hear magisters and their nephews argue about the patrol budget (bowstaves for the southern posts, a full company at the Pass). The Convocation sits the day after chapter 1's events. Lor'themar goes in her place. Silvermoon is a day's ride from the troll hollow and is reached by the old north road from the fork.
