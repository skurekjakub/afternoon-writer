# Andorhal inspection office and sample room

Offstage in Chapter 7. The mage quarter lies across the city from the militia post. The inspection office keeps a second copy of every waybill that leaves under a yard's mark and a sealed bag from every flagged load on the sample-room shelves; nobody opens them. The sample room opens only to a magister's ring, the copy room has its own key. Corin has the tags. Jaina goes through it with Corin and Lyriel and reads every shelf; she returns grey to the lips with the verdict. Nothing about the interior is described on the page.
