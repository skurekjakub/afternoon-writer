# Eversong troll-sweep hollow

A hollow in southeastern Eversong Woods near the Amani frontier. A ridge path runs along the ridge above; a slope of waist-high ferns and bracken falls to a stream that can be heard but not seen. At the foot, a leaning ash and a boulder form a gap: the "stupid" route versus the "suicidal" open slope. Jays quarrel in the branches and fall silent when Sylvanas calls. The air is resin and sun-warmed Eversong blossom, then troll blood, rank and coppery. Wind moves the ferns, so a still frond means something is hiding.

Here Sylvanas lies in damp bracken, the patrol waits at the tree line in a half-circle, and she drops the last Amani as his foot slips on a mossy root. Lor'themar orders the dead burned. Birdsong returns finch first, then jays.
