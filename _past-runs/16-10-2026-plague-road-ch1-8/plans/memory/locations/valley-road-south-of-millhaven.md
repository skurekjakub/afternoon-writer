# Valley road south of Millhaven

Out over the bridge, up between two banks of hawthorn, and into open country: ploughland brown and shining, barley stubble with rooks walking it, bramble gone red, a long blue valley with willows at the bottom where the river and the ford must be. 'Fat and flat and wide open, the wind coming across it with nothing to stop it, and nowhere at all for a ranger to stand that could not be seen from half a mile off.'

The road is churned by a season of carts and the rain has filled the ruts. The carter says markets are busy at the ford and every chimney smokes. At the noon halt at a little gravel stream crossing the mage reads the contamination as 'wider... a stain in a cloth that's been wet through', ahead, down the road. By evening the party passes a living farm: smoke, a dog, a rowan, a shrine candle. A woman with a bucket stares at the bows and the mage's wrapped hands and shuts her door. The staff points past it, south.
