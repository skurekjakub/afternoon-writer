# The south road into the hills

The bending road at the carters' fork. Cut to pieces by iron-tyred wagons with the same wide flat King's post-horse shoes Cyndia knows from the Pass, running up full and back empty. The chapter ends with the party riding onto it at first light, with a brown moving smear on the slope of the hills ahead that Sylvanas takes for mist and cannot: it is moving. It is the road Jaina's staff leaned toward, the one Sylvanas cannot verify, and the one that runs into Prince Arthas's army near Strahnbrad. Chapter 6 opens here.

## Chapter 6
Morning on the climb: mist lying to the mare's knees in the road, grey fields and bracken, and above it the brown smear, which proves to be army dust with smoke going up into it from behind the hill. At the culvert a stream runs under the road and the drag comes up so hard the staff crystal gutters. Out of the mist the sun is on the hedges and the air smells of woodsmoke and horses, more than any farm keeps. Empty wagons with iron tyres and heavy-footed teams grind down; a loaded one goes up under a roped tarpaulin. The road cuts over the shoulder of the hill between two banks with deep ruts (the staff knocks Jaina's ribs). At the top of the cut the Strahnbrad camp fills the slope below. In the afternoon the road is nearly empty below the camp, then fills with carts as farm lanes join it, each lane adding a thread of lateness to the drag.
