# Thalassian Pass holding cell

Second door from the end of the cell passage; a room the size of a wardrobe with one chair, a bucket and empty pegs. A loaf-sized ward-stone hums in the wall at seated head height. Behind the chair, lower, a wedge-shaped stone with one gold rune sits in a cut notch: it presses a flat hand on a mage's chest and stops her reaching for magic. Pulling it lets the whole current-field in at once.

Jaina sat here from first light through the noon bowl into the night. The ward-lamp hangs behind a standing Sylvanas and throws her shadow over the prisoner's boots. The bar lifts from outside and the door swings in.
