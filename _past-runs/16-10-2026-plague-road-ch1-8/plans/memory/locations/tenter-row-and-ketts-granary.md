# Tenter Row and Kett's granary

Tenter Row is the narrow dyers' street that opens off Hurdle Lane on the right: straight for a stretch, then bending left, the dye-vat stink sour in the air and the day's cloth hanging on tenter frames in blue and rust-red, slack in the still air. Dust lies thick at the edges of the cobbles and shows every print, so Sylvanas keeps to the crown. Over the bend a granary gable and a ropeless hoist beam stand against the sky: Kett's. Havel had pointed past it twice ('Kett shut his gate at noon').

Round the bend the row meets a wider street, and a turn left leads to a long, straight back lane with loading doors shut and barred, a lamp at each end and no place to stand. Sylvanas goes up a water butt and a lean-to onto the roofs. Kett's is the last door on the left: stone below, black timber above, smaller and older than Fenner's. Its upper floor has a row of small dusty windows on the lane side with one bottom pane knocked out for air. Inside are the open sacks (ANDORHAL sheaf-in-a-ring stencil), the crock, the five masked workers, the bucket of gloves and the basin. At the post later, Corin reads Kett's bills as all for the first carts out, and Lyriel counts seven loaded carts already gone.
