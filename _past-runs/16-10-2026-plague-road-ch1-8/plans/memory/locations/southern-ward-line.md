# Southern track and ward-stone line

Grey ward-stones taller than a rider, runes with gold fill, humming low enough to be felt in the teeth. Chapter 1: the noon stone at the dry-creek bend, the fork to Silvermoon and the climb to the Pass.

Chapter 2: the party rides out through the outer stones at night at an easy walk, every horse stepping sideways once as if making room for someone on a stair. The hum cuts off clean behind them, like a door slammed on a crowded room, and the contamination Jaina has been hearing muffled through the rings hits her whole. The rings hold it off like a hand over an ear. Elves do not seem to feel it, but their horses do. Most of Jaina's circled stones are on the second ring either side of the high road; one on the outer ring far east is the noon stone.
