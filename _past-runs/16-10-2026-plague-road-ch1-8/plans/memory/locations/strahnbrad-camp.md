# Strahnbrad army camp

From the top of the cut the army fills the slope below: rows of tents across what was pasture, lanes trodden to soup, hundreds of horses along the hedges, blue-and-white banners with the King's gold cracking over the biggest tents, Silver Hand plate and white tabards by the stream. A huddle of grey roofs lies where the road crosses the water. Across the valley to the east, dark smoke columns lean together, and at the foot of one a small orange flicker is a roof coming down.

Sylvanas reads it in a breath: the command tent is the big one, riders go out east and come back east, the east pickets are doubled, and on the road side there is one sergeant and a hedge. The archers on the bank above the stream have their backs to the road. Empty wagons come down and loaded ones go up and turn in behind the cook-tents, and nobody turns to see what is in them.

At the hedge-gap a dusty sergeant holds his spear crossways. Arthas arrives in a few minutes and the audience is held in the mud, standing, with officers closing in. Mud, wet canvas, horse lines, a mule that will not stop braying, officers shouting about a ford. The party declines food and leaves.
