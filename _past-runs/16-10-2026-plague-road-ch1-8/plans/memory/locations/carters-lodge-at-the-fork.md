# Carters' lodge at the fork

Turf-roofed stone hut in the angle where the road splits, one door, a chest-high dry-stone wall between it and the road. Inside: a crust of old ash, trodden bracken, a bench of old wagon boards (bolt holes, rust stains, one iron strap, grain dust packed hard in the cracks), a charcoal horse with a fifth leg. One thick beeswax candle smelling of honey. Lyriel sits on the wall with her bow; Cyndia walks the fork.

Here the slips are read out one by one against Jaina's reads, the south road is chosen over the straight King's road, Ashford is changed from light to heavy, and the word Strahnbrad is stabbed into the bench. The bench's grain dust is what reopens the old carter's wagon in Sylvanas's mind. The party rides out in the old order, onto the bending south road, with a brown moving smear low over the hills at dawn.
