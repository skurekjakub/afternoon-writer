# Thalassian Pass post

A squat dark tower at the saddle of the pass, white-blue gate lamps, a yard, a stable, a guardroom lit by steady ward-lamps, a rack alcove with a slate dampening plate that numbs staffs, and a short passage of cells. Below the gate the road drops by switchbacks toward the line of outer stones standing across the saddle; the hum rises through the hooves as the road approaches them.

Chapter 2 adds the yard and stable at night (stars, snow in the wind off the saddle) and the departure: gate rangers drag back the bar, the party rides out under the lamps. Neral expects to change horses at the Pass if she must relay word north.
