# Andorhal approach road

Below Strahnbrad the south road is nearly empty (an army wagon going up, a boy with a switch driving geese). On the flat it fills: carts heaped with roped sacks join from the farm lanes, empty ones rattle out, a cart of hen crates sheds white feathers. Every lane that joins adds a thread of lateness to the drag Jaina feels in the road. A man comes along the verge on foot, out of the city.

Past the next farm the road goes in between walls: old orchard walls with moss on the coping, then the backs of barns and long low sheds hard against the road. Loaded carts and empty carts take turns at the narrow places and the dust has nowhere to go. It hangs at face height, fine and pale, and tastes sour, like flour left in a damp sack. No wind. Here the drag hits all at once. The horses go rigid and keep shying.

The walls end at the top of a long low rise, and the dust thins. From the crest the road runs down to Andorhal's smaller gate (the market gate, with a militia post inside on the left and a bench at its door); a broader straight road to the right runs to a bigger gate: the King's road. At the bottom of the hill the carts bear left for the granary yards.
