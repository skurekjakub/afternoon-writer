# The cow way (drove way) north of Millhaven

A sunken drove way that starts at the open oak gate at the top of the east lane and runs north and a little east, over a shoulder of the hill, toward the ward line. The banks stand higher than a head, hazel and thorn meet over it, and under the hazel it is still nearly night at first light. At its mouth the yellow clay floor is scored from bank to bank by long parallel grooves, as if a close-tined harrow had been dragged up it, between pocked foot prints.

The girl calls it 'the north road'. It is not the Pass road: that lies two fields and a hedge to the west and bends away from it. The party rode down the Pass road in Ch2 and saw nothing because the dead were 'the whole hill away'.

Cyndia names it a drove way (spring grazing, down about now) and warns half the marks are cattle. The mage reads each mark with the heel of the staff; Cyndia counts the heavy ones with hazel switches. Result: sixty at least, four and five abreast, bunching where the cut narrows between two old gateposts, nothing going downhill, no walker apart from the rest.
