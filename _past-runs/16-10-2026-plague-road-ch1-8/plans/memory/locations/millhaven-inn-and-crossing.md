# Millhaven inn, well and crossing

The well sits at the dead middle of the crossing; four roads leave it. The inn faces it with a yard and wall, an outside stair to a gallery over the door that overlooks all four roads, a taproom with a settle and a table under the window, and a cellar through a trapdoor behind the bar. A horseshoe over the lintel hangs points down. The inn door knocks in the east wind all night.

Sylvanas picks this ground for the mage's deep read: Lyriel on the gallery, Cyndia at the smithy corner, the fourth ranger on the bridge, Sylvanas herself at the well-head. The well rope is slick with green; the water has the sickness 'thinner than in the doe'. The crossing read comes back mixed (north lines lag, east does not, west drowned out). The mage's left hand leaves skin on the cobbles.

The party holds the inn overnight. Neral and the girl sleep on the taproom settle; the mage sits on the mounting block in the yard with her hands in linen; the watch is on the gallery. At first light the party leaves by the bridge and Neral by the north road.
