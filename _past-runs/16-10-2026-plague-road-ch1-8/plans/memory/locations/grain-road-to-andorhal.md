# The grain road

Below the sick village and past the brook the road splits. One branch goes north, back toward Millhaven. The other goes south and west between hedges, rutted by every loaded cart that has ever come up it. Brask says it leads past the ford to Andorhal, the big granaries, three days with a cart; 'everything in the south goes through Andorhal'. Jaina's staff leans toward the ruts on its own. Sylvanas turns her mare onto it without a word.
