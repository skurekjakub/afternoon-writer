# Culture

Elves: 'Sun's sake'; rangers learn stones by palm; Neral and rangers speak Thalassian to horses; green formal dress; the Convocation and its figures. Humans (Lordaeron): 'Light' oaths; Light shrines on gateposts; rowan by the gate; 'Light keep you' or 'Safe road'; a horseshoe over the lintel points down; shaving glass at a dying mouth; box beds, hams behind the chimney, drover's gate loop; hill cattle in spring, down in autumn.

Chapter 3 gives the carter's non-Lordaeron blessing ('May the stars keep your road') as the page's only anomaly; Sylvanas files it under 'some hill saying'.

## Chapter 4
The sick village: eggs, herring, geese, bread smoke; a horseshoe points down over a lintel; a stool and cup on the step for someone who will not come out; Light chapel with bell-cote and no bell; willow bark, vinegar, bleeding, goose grease; sops for the toothless; oatcake; 'shop bread is chalk'; a cage for drunks; the harvest supper at the captain's table. Locals say 'my lady' and 'miss'. Jaina's Kul Tiran references (bowline, gunners' knots and oaths, Boralus's cathedral reliquary) and the Farstrider habit of teaching one-handed knots left then right.

## Chapter 7
Lammas and midsummer mark the supplier changes; the Wheatsheaf is the loaders' tavern; the Exchange bell and a lamplighter boy rule the evening; dye cloth on tenter frames; Havel says 'Ranger-General' correctly unprompted and 'my lady' to Jaina.
