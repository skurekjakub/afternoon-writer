# Timeline

## Chapter 5 (summary; full entry in timeline.json)
About three and a half days, autumn, after the sick village. Corley's depot and the first map; a second day of stops ending in the table at a south-road inn and a night ride through a wood; the river depot and the overseer's lie; a day lost on the Brill road; the return to a padlocked depot, the stove ash, 53 slips and the Barov name; midnight at the carters' fork, the south road chosen; first light south under a brown smear over the hills.

## Chapter 1
One day: pre-dawn crossing (off-page), troll sweep, noon stone, afternoon ride, dusk at the Pass, evening in the cell. The Convocation sits the next day.

## Chapter 2
The same night into the next day: ride out at night; culvert at dawn; farms and ambush in the afternoon; Millhaven at the last of the light. A long day's ride from dawn to dusk. Autumn.

## Chapter 3
Last light: ride into Millhaven; sweep (no dead); well, cellar and storehouse read; the churned lane. Night: the hatch and the girl; the horses re-checked clean; the warning held; the night watch. First light: the cow way (sixty at least, north-northeast); the dispatch; Neral, the fourth ranger and the girl ride north; the carter and 'May the stars keep your road'; four ride south; noon water; evening past a living farm. Chapter 4 opens two days into the ride south.

## Chapter 4
One long day and a night into the next morning, in the sick village (plan: two days' ride south of Millhaven; the prose gives no count). Late morning: spear across the lane, Sylvanas defuses Brask, Jaina to Tobin's chapel. Afternoon: readings, wards, the knot, the slate, the bread answer; Sylvanas and Cyndia find the flour at the granary. Dusk: reports converge ('On purpose'); granary loft; Tam, the jar, arrow, frost, shell ward; Brask with torches; the cage. Full dark: Tam dies; Brask's shutdown and fire. Night: wards, Lyriel rebinds the hand, oatcakes, 'It's burned.' Before dawn rain; morning: Elsie cool, Garran unchanged, the ANDORHAL sacking, Brask's ledger, the grain road southwest.

Notes (Ch4): the Millhaven interval is not stated in the prose; Garran ill 'since before market'; the ford cart was sent 'this morning' and caught that night; the baker lights his oven before light; the old carter and Neral's party are not mentioned.

Notes (Ch3): 'five days at the most' (mould); 'a day and more' ahead of the party for the dead; the carter's 'market day' is unreconciled; one stray line has Sylvanas arriving 'an hour before dusk' where Ch2 says last light.

## Chapter 6: Day 5
Morning: the south road climb under mist, the culvert, Jaina's model of the plague, her unspoken Dalaran fear. Late morning to midday: the Strahnbrad camp; Arthas believes the warning and chooses the orc war; Sylvanas calls it; Jaina concedes. Afternoon: the flat fills with carts; the tally-man gives the name Captain Havel and the farewell; Sylvanas marks it wrong twice. Late afternoon: the walled approach, the horses shy and the drag breaks Jaina's body; Andorhal appears working in the low gold light. The Garrow farm burned on day 4; the tally-man left Andorhal 'this noon'.

## Chapter 7: Day 5, evening
Last of the low sun to the night shift bell. The four enter Andorhal on foot on Sylvanas's Farstrider pass (horses left in a carter's yard); Havel and Corin give the street and paper truths at the militia post; the work splits (Jaina, Corin and Lyriel to the sample room; Sylvanas, Cyndia and Havel to the granary district); Sylvanas sees eight sentries at Fenner's yard, follows a runner to Kett's loft and sees five masked men dress sacks from a crock; at the bell the crews answer; at the post Jaina's reads and Sylvanas's sighting fit; Sylvanas orders the capture for tonight. Chapter 8 opens minutes after the decision.
