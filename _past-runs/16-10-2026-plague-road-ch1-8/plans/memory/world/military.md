# Military

Farstriders: green cloaks, bows, belt knives, exact-phrase runners. Sylvanas's escort formation south of the Pass: herself in front, the mage in the middle, Cyndia left, Lyriel right.

## Chapter 3 procedure
Her village-sweep drill: perimeter and bridge first, pairs only, never stand in a doorway with the light behind you, every house cellar door to roof-tree, a second sweep of hiding places, the mage's read only after the houses with cover on every side. Night watch on the inn gallery. Dispatch practice: oiled-paper tube, red wax, ring seal, strapped behind the rider's leg and girth-tested, delivered by hand to Lor'themar.

## Split of the party
Neral, the fourth ranger and the girl ride north; four ride south. Wardens double on the ring between the Pass and the eastern posts if Lor'themar acts on the dispatch. Cyndia counts a track with hazel switches laid a man's step apart.

## Chapter 4
The village militia (Brask, Piers, the Aldon boys, Dickon) bars the lane with a boar spear; Sylvanas de-escalates by unarming, conceding and speaking low. Farstrider first-year training includes one-handed bandage knots. Sylvanas pins a running man's sleeve to a post with an arrow; Cyndia holds the stair. Brask's shutdown: granary and bakehouse closed, every house's flour burned in Joss's yard, the ford cart turned round, all before cockcrow.

## Chapter 5
Army wagons on the south road: iron tyres, King's post-horse shoes (wide flat, heavy heel, as at the Pass), full going up, empty coming down. A company of King's foot cut across the hill road a week ago and does not pay tolls. The army has taken remounts and oats. Formation (the 'old order'): Sylvanas front, Cyndia left, Lyriel right at Jaina's knee, Jaina middle, two empty places behind. In the dark wood Sylvanas rides with her hand on the bowstring; she waters at the Plough and does not stop. Thalassian horses stand to the staff's cold; carters' cobs shy.

**Chapter 6.** The Strahnbrad camp faces east to the smoke of burning farms. Command tent central; pickets doubled east; the road side held by one sergeant and a hedge; archers with their backs to the road; wagons filing in unexamined behind the cook-tents. Silver Hand knights in plate and white tabards by the stream. Orc bands out of the camps with axes are burning farms; the Garrow farm burned yesterday. Arthas will send a letter to his father tonight and come to Andorhal when the hills are quiet. Thalassian horses, which stood at the ring stones, will not settle on Andorhal's approach.

## Chapter 7
Five gate watchers and grain spikes. The cult sets sentries at corner, trough, hoist and gate in trained waiting, uses wall-crack dead drops and separates outer and inner layers; the crew locks itself into the rope-walk at the bell. Cyndia counts; Lyriel holds doors and bends; Sylvanas tails over the roofs; orders are by event ('until I call').
