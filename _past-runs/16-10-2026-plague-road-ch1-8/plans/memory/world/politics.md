# Politics

The Convocation of magisters sits at Sunfury Spire and votes patrol budgets; Sylvanas has a bench; the wardens answer to the magisters. South of the wards is Lordaeron's business. Kirin Tor/Dalaran: Antonidas and Jaina; the Kirin Tor do not know she is here. Farstriders keep files on human mages.

## Chapter 3
Sylvanas's dispatch will reach the Convocation through Lor'themar by the end of the week; the magister's nephew will count the courier's cost. The Kirin Tor ink on the dispatch will be recognised. She judges her duty done 'by any measure the chamber would put its seal to' and still rides south into 'somebody else's kingdom'.

## Chapter 4
The village runs on a militia captain and a medic. The Kirin Tor teach first-years the signs of the death-currents craft but lock the grammar behind a grille. Antonidas's authority works 'because of the hat'. Andorhal is the hub of the south's grain trade. Brask will keep a ledger of the party's names (sworn letter, Chapter 23).

## Chapter 5
Lord Barov: an old Lordaeron house with a seat at the King's court; his family holds the land round Andorhal and, Jaina thinks, most of the granaries; his seal (a shield) and name are on the river depot's paper: connection, not proven guilt. Prince Arthas has an army at Strahnbrad against orcs from the camps; the army takes remounts and oats at the King's price. Depot books go to a regional office or Andorhal at the quarter. Sylvanas thinks Silvermoon magisters would let a village burn before moving a mark of their own on a map.

**Chapter 6.** Arthas turns the warning into a letter to King Terenas and the council and a later visit to Andorhal. Andorhal's granary authority is divided among the factors, the corn guild and the town, and none wants Captain Havel's knowledge. Arthas treats the Barovs as a court house and jokes that Barov would sue him. Jaina offers no accusation. The Silver Hand appears as plate and tabards only.

## Chapter 7
Local power in Andorhal: the Exchange (its bell rings the shifts; it has ordered sacks spiked at the gate), town council, magistrate, factors and the corn guild. Havel has twelve men. The Kirin Tor has a house and an inspection office in the mage quarter; Dalaran's inspection desk and the Archmage's secretary never answered Corin. Sylvanas's pass is written under her own name, rank and seal with no cover name; the gate book records it.
