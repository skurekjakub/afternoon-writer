# Geography

Quel'Thalas's southern border runs through the Thalassian Pass post (a squat tower on a saddle of rock) with two rings of ward-stones below. South of the wards is Lordaeron: switchbacks, birch and bracken hills, then hedge country, farms, a culvert, a holly wood, and Millhaven, the first village of any size, at a crossroads in a shallow fold. Millhaven has four roads around a well, a stone bridge over a stream, a mill and storehouse, an inn, and a few dozen thatched houses.

Chapter 3 adds the drove way (the cow way): from the field gate at the top of the east lane it runs north and a little east, over a shoulder of the hill, diverging from the Pass road two fields and a hedge to its west, toward the stretch of the ring between the Pass and the eastern posts. South of the bridge the road runs into open ploughland and a long valley with a river and ford; a living farm lies a long day's ride on.

## Chapter 4
The sick village lies south of Millhaven (plan: two days' ride). Its chapel is at the top of the hill; Chapel Lane runs down past the pound and its stone cage to the granary and bakehouse and a brook at the bottom. Three wells (Cooper's Lane, the top farm's own, the brook for Ashby's). Below the brook the road splits: north to Millhaven; south and west the deep-rutted grain road past a ford to Andorhal, the big granaries, three days with a cart. The new flour man came by the west road. Jaina's memories add Boralus (quay mill, cathedral) and the reefs off Tiragarde.

## Chapter 5
Below the ford the road runs straight and wide through stubble, built for carts, to a depot at a crossroads (four roads: north to Millhaven, west bending toward the sick village, south, east). Places on the books: Coldwell (past the bridge), Wick, Hollow Green (own mill, in no book) and Brent's farm beyond it, Elm Ferry on the King's road, Ashford on the south road, Brill (corn market), Andorhal, Strahnbrad up in the hills. The big depot is a day's ride down the river road, past an inn called the Plough, through an oak-and-hazel wood; the Brill road leaves north through the depot's town over a lord's causeway of stone, then clay hills and a toll-bar. At the fork the King's road runs straight south-west into Andorhal by the front gate; the south road bends under the hills, past a bridge, past Strahnbrad, and into Andorhal by the back.

**Chapter 6.** Strahnbrad is a huddle of grey roofs at a stream crossing under a long slope covered by Arthas's camp, reached by a cut over a hill. Below it the south road runs nearly empty, then fills with farm-lane carts, then runs between orchard walls and barn backs, then over a long low rise. From the rise Andorhal fills a shallow valley: grey wall, packed roofs, a granary quarter of tall timber buildings on stone feet. The south road reaches the market gate (militia post inside on the left); the carts bear left for the yards; a broad straight road to the right is the King's road and a bigger gate.

## Chapter 7: Andorhal at ground level
The market gate is a low arch in a chest-high field-stone wall with no ward-stone; carts bear left to the granaries; the militia post is a squat stone house on the left. Mill Lane runs from the weir bridge to the Corn Exchange (Holt's, Fenner's, the Exchange granary, Garside's). Hurdle Lane runs along the backs of the yards (blind loading lanes); Tenter Row (dyers) opens off it on the right with Kett's at the foot, reached by a back lane. Outbound: south road, weir road, east road. The mage quarter, inspection offices and sample room lie across the city. Warm, dry, windless dusk.
