# Mother Haw

An old woman at the end of Cooper's Lane who 'won't have shop bread in the house, says it's chalk' and cooks oatcake on a griddle for herself and her grandson. She stopped Tobin that morning to tell him his apron was a disgrace. She is well, drinks from the same Cooper's Lane well as Garran and the cooper's household, and is Jaina's control case: not water, not contact, not grain, but bread. Late that night she gives Cyndia a cloth bundle of her own oatcakes, from her own field ('She wants the cloth back'), which Tobin breaks into milk for Garran.
