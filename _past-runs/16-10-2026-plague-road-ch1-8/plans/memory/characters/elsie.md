# Elsie

A dairymaid of about fifteen at Ashby's, brought into the chapel that morning after fainting in the byre the day before. On a door laid across two trestles under a striped blanket. The first patient Jaina reads: pulse fast and light, a fist of brown under the breastbone, a thin thread along the gut, nothing in the heart, lungs or arms. 'What's in me?' 'I don't know yet.' She cannot be told who she is wondering about; she answers questions plainly ('I had two pieces at noon yesterday... Mistress gave me the end one, because I'd done the calves.'). 'Is it the bread? I heard you. With Tobin.'

Jaina feeds her ward last on each round; she calls it 'a wet sheet' and then 'nice'. At dawn after the rain she wakes wet through and cool: 'I'm all wet. Is there water?' Her cooling forehead on Jaina's cheek is the first proof the plan works.
