# The fourth ranger (unnamed)

The ranger who rode at the tail of the Ch2 line and held the horses at the culvert. Never named, no gendered pronoun in Ch2 or Ch3, no dialogue. In Ch3: held the bridge, stood watch with Lyriel, brought the girl out to the horses, and rode north behind Neral leading the spare grey. Sylvanas's single thought about them: the one she had never once had to think about.

Keep them ungendered and unnamed.
