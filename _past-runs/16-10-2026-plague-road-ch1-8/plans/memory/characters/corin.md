# Corin

A young Kirin Tor clerk-mage at Andorhal's inspection office (plain grey coat, violet cord round the collar), thin, ink on every finger of his right hand, skin under the eyes dark as a bruise. First on the page in Chapter 7 at the militia post, where he files flagged-shipment notices that Captain Havel never reads (they sit under a cracked river stone). He asks Jaina at once whether she was sent about the shipments. She says nobody sent her; he keeps a hand pressed on his satchel.

He is nervous and over-prepared and right every time. His voice is formal and breaks into half-sentences ('I— it isn't, though.'), then steadies on facts. He has copies of the inspection office's second waybills and has found forty-one bills with a copied yard mark (Fenner's hook drawn from the bottom, with a bead of ink at the foot) since midsummer; he wrote weekly to the Citadel inspection desk and then the Archmage's secretary and got no answer. He opens the sample room for Jaina with a magister's ring and writes her readings at her dictation after her own attempt turns to blots (Heavy, Heavy, Clean). Back at the post he argues down Havel's dawn arrest by reading Kett's bills (every bill is for the first carts out) and tells the room nobody from Dalaran is coming. He volunteers for the raid; Sylvanas tells him to stay behind Proudmoore.

Voice: 'Ranger-General' said carefully, 'Lady Proudmoore', 'Captain'. Habits: pressing the satchel, winding the strap round his fist, hugging it to his chest; fumbles tags and buckles. Sylvanas thinks of him as 'the boy' and 'the Dalaran boy'. She is unsure only for a moment whether his patience hides something (a patient man with the keys to the inspection office could be sitting on bills for somebody else) but sets it aside because he has been right every time.
