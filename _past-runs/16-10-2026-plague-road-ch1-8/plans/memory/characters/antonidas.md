# Antonidas

Archmage of the Kirin Tor; still off-page. Chapter 2 adds, through Jaina: she left him a note on his desk and he reads everything there eventually, between three other things, a cup going cold at his elbow. He is always slow and usually right to be. He says everything kindly. His twin of the rune pebble sits in his study; Jaina kept hers wrapped all the way north so he could not tell her to come home. He drilled her to clear her head and listen again over cold benches, and would say "wait for the light".
