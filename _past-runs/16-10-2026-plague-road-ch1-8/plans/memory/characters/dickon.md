# Dickon

Brask's youngest militia hand: about sixteen, with an unstrung hunting bow he holds 'as if it might go off'. He guides Jaina and Lyriel to the chapel ('It's this way, miss'), stares at Lyriel's ears and tries not to, holds a torch in the loft, and at the cage-night is sent with ponies after Joss's cart on the ford road to turn it round. He catches it: the Hallams had not unloaded; the alehouse had taken two sacks.
