# Millhaven villagers named in the girl's account

None of these appears alive on the page. They exist only in the girl's testimony or as traces in houses.

- **The girl's Mam**: sweet breath ('pears gone brown') before fever; died; the shaving glass did not mist; rose in the dark.
- **The girl's Da**: held the glass, told the girl to bolt the hatch 'not for anybody, not if it was him', and is unaccounted for.
- **Missus Hale's baby**: the first to fall sick. (A cradle with a tucked blanket and no baby was found in the house by the smithy; the prose does not say whose.)
- **Mister Pell**: second sick. 'Up toward Pell's' is the way the feet went.
- **Gran Ottley**: came to sit with Mam in the last days.
- **The tinker**: came for the pot.
- **Mister Hollis**: the established grain seller in Millhaven.
- **The newer grain seller**: cheaper than Hollis, a quarrel in the girl's kitchen, a caller in the last days. Never seen on page; see the thread newer-grain-seller.
