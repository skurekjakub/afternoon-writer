# The Millhaven survivor girl (unnamed)

Eight or nine. Found alive in a bolted cellar under the end house on the back lane. She has never given a name; Neral asked once and let it go. One stocking, a darn at the heel in brown wool on grey, a black bare foot, a fist forever clenched round a scrap of blue cloth.

## What she gave
Fragments, not a narrative, and all of it to Neral's collar: Missus Hale's baby sick first, then Mister Pell, then 'everybody coughing at the well'. Her mam's breath 'went sweet... pears gone brown' before she was hot (and they had no pears). Her da held his shaving glass to Mam's mouth and it did not mist. 'And then she got up. In the dark.' Da put her down the cellar and told her to bolt it, 'not for anybody. Not if it was him.' She heard feet go by the barred slot 'away. Up toward Pell's', 'hundreds, all night, every night', and said it was always dark. Callers in the last days: Gran Ottley, the tinker, and the newer grain seller (cheaper than Mister Hollis, a quarrel between Mam and Da). Her 'north road' is the cow way past Pell's.

## How she is with people
She trusts only Neral ('You talk funny.' 'I do.'). She flinches at Sylvanas's flat common-tongue question, watches her like a hare watches a hawk, and goes white round the mouth at the bay until Neral explains the horse. She rides north in front of Neral with her hands in the mane and the blue scrap in her fist.

Do not give her a name or a continuation south. Her father's fate is unknown.
