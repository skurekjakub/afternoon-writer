# Neral

Relay rider and best horsewoman of the escort, long plain face, a voice she uses on frightened horses and, in Ch3, on a frightened child. She leaves the book's southbound party at the end of Chapter 3.

## Chapter 3
At the hatch in the end house she hears the child breathing under the floor, lays her hands flat on the boards as she would on a horse's neck, and talks down through the cracks in Thalassian and then the common tongue in exactly the same voice. She tells plain facts ('We came down off the hill on horses. I've got water.'), spills water on the boards so the child can hear it, asks to lift the lid slowly, keeps the light at the side. 'You talk funny.' 'I do.' She repeats the girl's own words back, says *small* in Thalassian each time the skin comes away, carries the girl up the ladder, and sleeps on the taproom settle with the child on her chest.

She is brisk with Sylvanas ('Give me the tube and the spare grey, Ranger-General. I'll change at the Pass and keep going.'). At first light she has the bay and the grey saddled and a blanket roll strapped on before anybody has told her. She presents the bay to the child ('He's big and he's stupid, and he's never stepped on anyone in his life'), takes the tube from Sylvanas, girth-tests it twice, and rides north with the girl in front of her and the fourth ranger and the grey behind.
