# Tam

A boy of about sixteen ('Dickon's age or thinner') who works the hoist for Joss at the granary; came looking for work 'at the back end of summer' and was taken on. He sat at Brask's harvest supper next to Brask's youngest and cut her meat for her.

He is the watcher: found in the granary loft at dusk, kneeling beside a floorboard with a lamp and a clay jar of the made contamination (waxed cloth tied over the mouth), no light visible from the street. When Sylvanas orders 'Put it down' he does, then reaches for the lamp to throw it into the flour-dusted air. Sylvanas pins his sleeve to a post with an arrow; Jaina freezes him to the floor. He cries without sound. He will say only: 'I'm not to speak to you... You don't know what you're keeping them from.' Jaina thinks of the Boralus reliquary: half hoping it would do something, terrified that it would.

In the cage by the pound he sits calm, foam bubbling on his lip, then arches and dies within minutes of a poison bitten from a sewn-in collar hem (waxed paper, brownish smear). Sylvanas puts her fingers in his mouth to clear it and is bitten. Jaina reads his gut: clean. He had never eaten what he guarded. 'Somebody taught him that. They sewed it in for him and told him when.' Lyriel covers his face with a spare cloak.
