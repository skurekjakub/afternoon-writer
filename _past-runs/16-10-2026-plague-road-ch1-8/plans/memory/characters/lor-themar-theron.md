# Lor'themar Theron

**Role:** Sylvanas's second in the field and her political cover. Present in chapter 1 scene 1 only; exits north with the patrol.

## On the page
He rides a half-length behind her mare's shoulder, bow across the saddle, and sees through her stall to the south instantly: "Today?" He teases in precise bureaucratic detail (the report said stable "in three different hands"; the magister's nephew has "prepared figures"; "he'll bring a clerk"). He laughs quietly. When Sylvanas finds the stone wrong, he lays his own palm on it, feels nothing, and sends it down the proper channel: write it up, he'll carry it in, the magisters will send a clerk and a lens in a fortnight.

He notices more than he says. When the runner says "leylines are wrong" he looks at her closed fist. He takes the Convocation himself ("They like me better anyway; I don't make them look at the map"), promises to say she was "called off by something with teeth", and asks her for word when she knows what the prisoner is.

## Voice and address
Dry, courteous, ironic. "Ranger-General" when teasing, "Sylvanas" when serious. She calls him "Lor'themar", bare first name.

## Physical
Old scar along the jaw (he scratches it), brow that drops over a trail. Horse's bit chimes.

## Limits of knowledge
Knows the stone was "off" and the runner's report. Does not know about the map or the circled stone, nor Jaina's name.
