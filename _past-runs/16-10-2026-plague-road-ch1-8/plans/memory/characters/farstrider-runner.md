# Farstrider runner (unnamed)

A young ranger in a dust-grey green cloak who rides a long-legged Farstrider horse that drags slightly on the near hind and arrives "dark with sweat from neck to flank, foam hanging off the bit in ropes". He tries to salute and breathe at once. He carries Cyndia's message in clipped shorthand and her instruction to repeat the human's words exactly: "She said the leylines are wrong." He rides back to the Pass with Sylvanas while his horse is still blowing; Sylvanas asks "Can he go on?" and receives "He'll go."

Unnamed in chapter 1. Appears in scene 1 only.
