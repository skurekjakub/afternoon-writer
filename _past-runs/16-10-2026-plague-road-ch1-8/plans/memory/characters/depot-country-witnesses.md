# Depot-country witnesses

One-scene Lordaeron locals who each hold a piece of the trail. The miller past Coldwell tells of the young cart man buying back four sacks at a loss yesterday; a hamlet woman has a notch-stick of cheap-flour debts since midsummer; Wick's ostler saw a free carter ride south before light; a second depot's clerk names the big river depot as the breaking point; two men in good brown coats and an innkeeper say the army (Prince Arthas, at Strahnbrad, against orcs from the camps) has taken every remount and oat, and that the Plough is the halfway inn; the Plough's ostler lights them without a question; a toll-keeper with fine beans proves no grain went by the Brill road; a pipe-smoking bridge-keeper saw the overseer and his weeping wife flee south. Each has a line of the dialogue in Sylvanas's notes. None should be reused.
