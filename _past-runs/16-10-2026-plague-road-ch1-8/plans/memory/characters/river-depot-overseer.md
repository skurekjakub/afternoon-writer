# River-depot overseer (unnamed)

Fifty or so, in a good dark coat, with a pleasant broad face that has no colour in it at all. His desk is the tidiest in Lordaeron (five pens trimmed alike) and his cuffs are black with old and new ink. He receives four armed elves 'the morning after he'd emptied' his yard with polite hospitality and a ready answer for every question: the books went to the regional office, the last grain left two days ago, north on the Brill road, six wagons, sold whole to Tolliver and Sons. Jaina's crystal finds nothing on him. He is frightened and watches the mage, not the elves.

It was a lie. Sylvanas spends a day on the Brill road before she learns it. When they come back the gate is padlocked and the house emptied in haste; the bridge-keeper saw him and his weeping wife go south over the bridge at a trot. In the stove he left the waybill halves (53 readable, all Andorhal at one end), parchment with the name Barov, and broken red seals. Do not bring him back.
