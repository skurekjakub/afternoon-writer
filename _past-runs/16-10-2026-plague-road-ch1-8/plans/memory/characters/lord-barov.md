# Lord Barov

Referenced only. A Lordaeron lord whose name and seal (a shield with a melted device, red wax) turn up across the burned river-depot paper, including on 31 of 53 readable waybill halves. Jaina places him: an old house, a seat at the King's court, land round Andorhal and most of its granaries, and a Boralus visit in her childhood where he talked about wheat through a whole dinner. Sylvanas wants it to be him and refuses to treat the name as proof: 'his name was on every sack that went out of them, rotten or sound.' Never on page.
