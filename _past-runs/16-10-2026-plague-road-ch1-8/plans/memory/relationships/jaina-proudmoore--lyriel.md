# Jaina and Lyriel

Lyriel is the friendlier escort. In Ch3 there is no dialogue between them, but her hands do it all: she grips the mage's mare by the cheekpiece at the spook, moves up to her knee when she sleeps in the saddle and hooks two fingers through the cheek-strap, holds the far stirrup when she mounts. She calls her 'Lady Proudmoore' in Ch2.

## Chapter 4: the knot
Lyriel holds the staff's crystal to the patients under Jaina's cheek, braced for it to bite. When Jaina cannot tie a bandage with one hand Lyriel crosses the bed without a sound, puts her hands over Jaina's, and teaches the Farstrider one-handed knot: loop over the thumb, wrist under, tail through, set it in your teeth. 'Go on, Jaina. Bite it.' It is the first time any ranger uses her given name and Jaina hears it at once. 'Again.' By the cooper's boy's dressing she is tying it herself; by dawn she ties Garran's unaided. In the loft Lyriel says 'Jaina, leave it'; in the night she cuts, washes and rebinds the torn palm, tying the same knot, and says Jaina cannot tie it on her own hand yet: 'Tomorrow.' Lyriel puts it into Brask's ledger that one of them ties a good knot. Jaina calls her 'Lyriel' aloud.

## Chapter 5
Seen from Sylvanas. Lyriel is the one who gets Jaina down and up from the saddle, rides at her knee in the dark with her fist in the back of her coat after the mare goes down on the causeway, and makes her laugh aloud with a story about a clerk's looped g's ('paid by the loop, Jaina... I've seen less thread on a spindle.'). She calls her 'Jaina' aloud. At the dawn start she holds the burnt end of the bread out ('the best bit, whatever Cyndia tells you') and Jaina eats it with two fingers.

## Chapter 6
The care becomes bodily and uncomplaining. Lyriel lifts Jaina down in the Strahnbrad mud and back up, hands round half an apple (the other half for the mare), and on the Andorhal approach says 'Jaina. Your nose.' and offers the wall and a rest. Jaina says 'No.' Lyriel moves her horse in until their knees touch, puts her hand over Jaina's on the reins until the mare calms, and lets go. She does not press, and Jaina rides on.


## Chapter 7 (Sylvanas's POV)

Silent, practical care: Lyriel lifts Jaina down in the carter's yard, takes the pass from her fingers at the gate, goes with her and Corin to the sample room, and walks her back across the city on her arm, stopping when Jaina stops. Sylvanas's thought is that Lyriel will watch the corridors and catch Jaina when she goes over. No spoken exchange between them on the page.
