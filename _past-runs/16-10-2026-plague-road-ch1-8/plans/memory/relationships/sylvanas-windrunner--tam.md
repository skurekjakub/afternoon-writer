# Sylvanas and Tam

She takes him alive. A single arrow through the sleeve above the wrist and into the post; the lamp lifted by the bail and its wick wound down; the oil ground out under her boot. 'Put it down' is an order he obeys once and then reaches for the lamp. She walks behind him down the lane near enough to touch. At the cage she stands a step back with her arms at her sides: 'Who gives you the jar? ... The young man with the cart. Does he bring the jar too?' Nothing. When he convulses she takes the key from Brask, goes in on her knees and hooks poison out of his cheek with her bare fingers until his teeth close; she does not take them out until there is nothing more to stay for. She finds the waxed paper sewn into his collar. 'Somebody taught him that. They sewed it in for him and told him when.' She never says his name.
