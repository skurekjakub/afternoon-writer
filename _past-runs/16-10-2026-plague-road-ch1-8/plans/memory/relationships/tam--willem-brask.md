# Tam and Brask

'That's Tam. He works the hoist for Joss. He came looking for work at the back end of summer. Joss took him on.' Then, in a different voice, a father's: 'Tam. What's this? What've you done?' Tam turns his face away. Brask locks him in the cage by the pound and, when the boy dies, stands in the door with the torch held low: 'He ate at my table. At the harvest supper. He sat next to my youngest and cut her meat for her.' 'Cover his face, somebody.'
