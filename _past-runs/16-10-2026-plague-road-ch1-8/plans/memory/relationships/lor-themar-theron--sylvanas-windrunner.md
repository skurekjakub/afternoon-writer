# Lor'themar and Sylvanas

Old patrol partners. He covers her at the Convocation; she writes him the Millhaven warning in Chapter 3 (Thalassian, no greeting, 'I am going on south') and tells Neral to put it in his hand herself. She pictures him reading it to the chamber in his pleasant voice, being liked for it, and the magister's nephew counting the cost of the courier. He is referenced but off the page in Ch2-3.
