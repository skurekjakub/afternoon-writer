# Captain Havel and Jaina

Havel stands her a seat ('My lady. Sit, please. You look done in.'). Jaina introduces herself, names the tally-man who sent them, and later asks him two plain questions that take his dawn-arrest plan apart (what is left in the loft; where is the crock by then). She sets her staff down at Sylvanas's shoulder to face him. He does not argue with her.
