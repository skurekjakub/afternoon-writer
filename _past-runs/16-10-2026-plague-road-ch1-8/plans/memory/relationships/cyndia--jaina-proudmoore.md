# Cyndia and Jaina

Guard and charge, and now a working edge. In the cut the mage calls 'Heavy. Light.' down the staff and Cyndia turns her head to each mark and lays hazel switches; the count of sixty is theirs together. Cyndia wraps the mage's raw hands in strips of linen when Sylvanas tells her to. They still do not address each other aloud on the page.

Chapter 4: still no direct address. Cyndia rides on Jaina's left, spots the shuttered houses Jaina missed, reports the supply chain to Sylvanas at the chapel with Jaina listening (Jaina takes the report as hers too), opens the granary's side door with Jaina behind her, and holds the loft with an arrow while Jaina's frost pins Tam. At night she arrives with oats for Garran. Neither says a word to the other.

Chapter 5: still no address on page. Cyndia hands Jaina over the depot sill on her arm and back to the gate; counts while Jaina marks; corrects the Hollow Green mark by showing it is in no book. They agree by evidence: Cyndia's counts and Jaina's reads match.
