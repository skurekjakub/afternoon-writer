# Jaina and the survivor girl

A careful, low-voiced distance. In the cellar the mage keeps the heap of turnips between herself and the child; in the room she sits against a wall with her palms up and asks short questions in a voice 'pitched down nearly as low as Neral's'. The girl will answer the mage only after turning to Neral for leave. The mage says 'Thank you' for the shaving glass. Neither speaks to the other again.
