# Lyriel and the old carter

The carter asks about a young grain-man; Lyriel answers warmly and truly, with the girl's remark about a newer grain seller, the promise to watch for him going south and the line about a better price going home full. He laughs once through his nose ('That's the truth'), takes in the line of horses front to back, and goes on north. Cyndia's bow stays up. The page frames the exchange as plain road courtesy; do not retcon Lyriel into carelessness or suspicion.
