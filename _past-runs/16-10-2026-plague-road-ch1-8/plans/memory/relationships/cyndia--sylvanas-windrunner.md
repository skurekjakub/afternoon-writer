# Cyndia and Sylvanas

Commander and her most trusted tracker. Cyndia: 'Ranger-General' every time. Sylvanas: 'Cyndia', curt, one-word orders. In Millhaven Sylvanas hands her the east side, the lantern in the lane, the count in the cut and the mage's hands. Cyndia will not give a number she may have to take back, and Sylvanas does not push; when Cyndia puts her name to 'sixty' Sylvanas takes it.

At the carter's arrival Cyndia's bow stays up after Sylvanas's is lowered. At the farewell Cyndia turns her head after the wagon and holds it a moment. Sylvanas sees it and thinks 'Leave it.' Neither says a word.

Chapter 4: the same pairing works the granary and the bakehouse. Cyndia takes Sylvanas's bow at Brask's spear, reports the supplier history standing 'weight even' ('Cyndia, tell her.'), opens the side door, holds the stair head ('Cyndia, the stair. Nobody comes up.'). The carter is not mentioned.

Chapter 5: the paper hunt. Sylvanas tasks in lists; Cyndia counts (31, 47, 53; 'Barov on thirty-one'), tracks (slumped ruts, trap prints, army-wagon shoes) and sorts burned slips 'faster than Sylvanas was'. Cyndia says 'on purpose' of the raked yard and 'Road's good' of the horses; Sylvanas listens to both. Cyndia is the one watching the trees while the others laugh in the wood.


## Chapter 7

In Andorhal Cyndia is the counter. She gives Sylvanas the gate (five watchers, four on the wagons) and the yard (eight she is sure of, with positions) in low flat lists at her shoulder, holds Havel at the lane corner while Sylvanas tails the runner alone, reports the rope-walk and the three men who went up Hurdle Lane, and at the end is given the roofs over the back lane: no loosing until Sylvanas calls. No reply on the page; no farewell reaction.
