# Neral and the survivor girl

'You talk funny.' 'I do.' That exchange, through the cracks of a floor, opens the only living contact in Millhaven. Neral does it with water, patience, plain true statements, hands where the girl can see them, and the Thalassian murmur she uses on horses. The girl trusts her and nobody else, speaks only into her collar, and falls asleep against her. At first light Neral introduces her to the bay ('He's big and he's stupid') and rides north with the girl in front of her, hands in the mane.
