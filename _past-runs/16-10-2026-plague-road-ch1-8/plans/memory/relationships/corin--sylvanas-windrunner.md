# Corin and Sylvanas

Corin is careful with her ('Ranger-General', getting all of it in); she asks him short questions ('How many?') and gives him no name aloud, thinking of him as 'the boy' and 'the Dalaran boy'. She briefly weighs whether a patient man with the keys to the inspection office could as easily be sitting on bills for somebody else, then sets that aside: he has been right every time he opened his mouth. She lets his speed carry the argument against Havel's dawn plan. At the end he offers to come on the raid and she tells him to stay behind Proudmoore.
