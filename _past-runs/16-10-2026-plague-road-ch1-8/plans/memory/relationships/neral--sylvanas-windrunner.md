# Neral and Sylvanas

Sylvanas trusts Neral with the one thing she herself cannot do: the child. She stands half in the door and 'gave Neral the room'. At first light Neral has the bay and the grey saddled before Sylvanas has finished shouting her name. Sylvanas's last order: 'Don't stop at the Pass longer than it takes to change saddles. Put it in Lor'themar's hand yourself.' 'Yes, Ranger-General.' Neral rides north.
