## intent-smear-agency-laundering-guide.md (24 keeps)

"Gone quiet" is literal: animals and birds stopped. The deciding agent is the troll.

Joint pain described as felt. No command, judgment, or deception is transferred. (Also slop-checker contact_settled.)

The thrower is named ("thrown axe"). "Kissed" is a contact metaphor for a graze, not a verb of intention, command, memory, or judgment.

**KEEP** | animal action

Horses are agents, and these are reflexes.

**KEEP** | elapsed-time idiom, evidence named
