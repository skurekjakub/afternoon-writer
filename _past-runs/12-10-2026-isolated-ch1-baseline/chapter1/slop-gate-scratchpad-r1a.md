Target: v2-r1.md

False-positive filter in force: limited-third Sylvanas (dry, exact, tactical interior; ranger-general who counts, inventories, and eliminates hypotheses). KEEP rate note: the negation guide (>20%) and intent-smear guide (>15%) both flag high keep rates as leniency. I re-examined every KEEP below. Most are plain negative statements with no pivot shape, procedural imperatives, literal physics, or the in-world ward mechanism. This is a revised draft, so the remaining candidates skew legitimate.
